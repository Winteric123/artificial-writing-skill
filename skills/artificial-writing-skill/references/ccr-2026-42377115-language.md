- Samples at diagnosis, after neoadjuvant chemotherapy and2–6weeks after surgery. One figure case labelled baseline was sampled after two treatment cycles. Do not describe every diagnostic draw as untreated.
- Tumor-agnostic NGS panels were used sequentially:105-gene Tempus for patients1–56 and152-gene Predicine for57–106. Three-hotspot KRAS G12D/V/R ddPCR is more narrowly targeted. Methods assay LODs .25% VAF for NGS and .01% for ddPCR are not generic technology limits.
- Baseline positivity: NGS17/99=17.2%, ddPCR63/97=64.9%. Text p4 gives16.0% and59.4%, which use the full106-patient denominator; keep tested versus enrolled denominators explicit. Overall ever-detected20/106 byNGS versus85/106 byddPCR is another endpoint.
- Post-neoadjuvant positivity3/67 byNGS and45/75 byddPCR; postoperative4/47 and31/55. Paired Figure2 analysis uses97/67/47 time-point pairs, distinct from all assay-evaluable samples.
- Figure3G compares14 dual-positive,49 ddPCR-only and31 dual-negative cases (94 displayed); the three NGS-only cases in the97 paired baseline samples are not represented as a separate survival curve. Abstract dual-positive median10.9months differs from Results10.1: withhold that exact median pending source correction.
- Diagnostic NGS positivity associates with shorter OS:11.2 versus30.5months, P<.001; ddPCR24.7 versus70.9months, P=.004. Long survival tails are uncertain relative to22.4-month median follow-up. The reported baseline adjusted HRs4.17(2.00–8.71) and3.46(1.49–8.07) lose significance when dynamic CA19-9/radiological response covariates are included; these supplemental-model details are prose-reported only.

- Twelve healthy controls tested negative; this is not perfect clinical specificity. Tissue matching is available only for a subset. More positive tests do not by themselves establish greater clinically actionable sensitivity or improved patient outcomes.

### Results paragraph
- `At diagnosis, positivity differed between the two assays when calculated using their respective tested-sample denominators. Paired analyses identified a substantial discordant subgroup. Baseline positivity was associated with survival in selected models, whereas the later time-point comparisons were not statistically significant and the baseline estimates attenuated with additional adjustment.` 结构：分母→不一致→模型/时间点限制；pp4–7。

### Discussion paragraph
- `The comparison highlights information recovered by a lower-threshold assay, while also illustrating the trade-off between sensitivity and mutation coverage. Sequential platform use, incomplete paired sampling and limited control numbers restrict generalization. The data support assay-development research, not a proven survival benefit from biomarker-guided management.` 结构：贡献→技术与队列限制→转化边界；pp7–10。
