### Evidence and numerical boundaries

Retrospective nine-center cohort of365 acquired EGFR C797X cases after osimertinib, plus selected literature cases for outcome analyses. Targeted168/520-gene NGS with shared-gene comparisons, tissue/plasma and serial profiles; not multiomics, RNA sequencing, single-cell or functional perturbation. Same-gene C797X/T790M allelic context (cis/trans/mixed/only) must be separated from cross-gene co-mutation and CNA/fusion context. Read-based phasing does not automatically prove two mutations occupy the same clone; relative allele-frequency ratios provide inference, not single-cell validation.

Timepoint2:365 (C797S349, C797G16); cis275(75.3%), trans23(6.4%), mixed38(10.4%), only29(7.9%). Timepoint1 genomic128 with106 patient/sample-type matched; timepoint3 genomic122 with106 matched. Figure1 distinguishes previous-osimertinib outcome70 including9 published cases and subsequent-treatment84 including18 published cases. Figure3 actually displays65 (31/18/7/9); source prose additionally states84 and treatment-line counts totaling74. These populations are not interchangeable; unresolved discrepancies must accompany denominator reuse, not be silently reconciled. Table1 describes365 and substantial missing treatment-line data.

Figure2 original panel/legend labels are inconsistent: main D is the lollipop and E is the cross-driver bar plot, whereas legend maps descriptions differently and Results mentions F. Cite the visual panel and description explicitly. Main bar in-cis non-C797X resistance8.0% differs from abstract8.4%; do not quote an unqualified exact prevalence. DDR enrichment P=.055 in p4 is not conventionally significant; mechanistic/synthetic-lethal treatments are hypotheses, not tested efficacy.

PFS includes radiographic progression, treatment discontinuation OR death (p3), not the conventional narrower definition by assumption. OS starts when C797X is detected. The paper's Discussion describes C797X-only first/second-TKI median4.0 whereas Results says5.0; withhold that exact contrast pending clarification. Retrospective selection, incomplete annotations, literature-case enrichment and small subtypes limit claims. A proposed multiomic model in Discussion is not an implemented assay.
