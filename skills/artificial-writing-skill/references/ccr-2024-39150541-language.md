### Evidence reconstruction and numerical checks

| Evidence unit | Source-grounded reconstruction | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical source set | Clinical histories describe 11 patients, whereas spatial profiling selected specimens from 10 patients. Fifty-nine ROIs comprised 22 LADC, 30 SCLC, and 7 combined regions. | The 11-patient clinical description and 10-patient spatial analysis must not be conflated. |
| Treatment course | Median time to transformation was 18.1 months; median PFS with platinum–etoposide after SCLC diagnosis was 5.9 months. | Small retrospective series without a comparator. |
| Subtype transition | Before EGFR TKI, 14/21 combined-SCLC ROIs were non-neuroendocrine; after EGFR TKI, 15/16 t-SCLC ROIs were neuroendocrine-high. | ROI-level counts are not patient-level transition probabilities. |
| Heterogeneity | Multiple subtype markers occurred within tumors; mRNA/protein dominant-subtype discordance was reported in 5/12 evaluable comparisons. | IHC and RNA measure different quantities; discordance is not simple assay failure. |
| Lineage programs | t-SCLC showed neuronal/cell-cycle/epigenetic programs, reduced RTK and immune signaling, and lower EGFR expression, including in some EGFR-TKI–naive components. | Cross-sectional combined histology suggests early lineage plasticity but does not directly observe temporal transformation before treatment. |
| Functional models | SNU-2962A was NEUROD1/high-MYC; SNU-4505 was YAP1/EMT-like. Drug responses differed, including BET, MEK, HDAC, taxane, and kinase vulnerabilities. | Two cell lines cannot establish subtype-wide therapeutic rules. |
| Fimepinostat mechanism | Fimepinostat restored EGFR/p-EGFR and increased H3K27 acetylation at the EGFR promoter in SNU-2962A, but not SNU-4505. | Fimepinostat is a dual PI3K/HDAC inhibitor; the effect cannot be assigned to HDAC inhibition alone. |
| Combination | Fimepinostat plus osimertinib showed combination-index synergy in SNU-2962A cells and organoids and delayed growth in xenografts (n=5/group). | Preclinical synergy in one responsive model is not clinical resensitization. |
| Clinical BET example | One ASCL1-subtype patient treated with molibresib had PFS 1.2 months. | Single-patient lack of benefit cannot define a subtype-treatment interaction. |

### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. Distinguish 11 patients with clinical histories from 10 patients contributing spatial ROIs.
2. The 15/16 result is ROI based and post–EGFR-TKI; it is not 15 of 16 patients.
3. Pre-treatment combined histology is cross-sectional evidence compatible with early lineage plasticity, not direct proof that transformation occurred before therapy in every case.
4. Spatial RNA profiling resolves selected ROIs but is not single-cell sequencing; each ROI can contain mixed cells.
5. The key fimepinostat effect was model specific and absent in SNU-4505.
6. Fimepinostat inhibits both PI3K and HDAC; do not call the experiment selective HDAC proof.
7. Organoids were derived from a patient-derived cell line, not directly expanded from fresh patient tissue in this experiment.
8. Xenograft groups contained five mice; growth delay is not a clinical response estimate.
9. The authors acknowledge rare paired samples and lack of multi-omics validation.
