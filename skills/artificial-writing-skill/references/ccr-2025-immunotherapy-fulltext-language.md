# CCR 2025 immunotherapy full-text language assets

## Purpose and source discipline

This reference was curated on 2026-09-08 from the complete main text of 17 immunotherapy-related, lung-cancer-relevant CCR research articles published in 2025. The full-text pass covered **294 PDF pages** and **167,400 whitespace-delimited extraction tokens**. All 17 sources report original clinical, translational, computational, imaging, or preclinical data. No commentary, editorial, review, author reply, rebuttal, or response-only correspondence contributed language to this asset.

The set was selected because immune checkpoint therapy, cellular therapy, immune toxicity, immune-treatment monitoring, or a directly immune-therapeutic target was central to the intervention, comparator, biomarker question, or mechanism. The label `immunotherapy-related` does not imply that every study was an immunotherapy efficacy trial.

Use the entries as functional language patterns, not as source sentences to reproduce. Reconstruct every statement from the user's own design, population or model, comparator, endpoint, estimate, uncertainty, time point, and analysis status. Never transfer a source article's number, assay, causal link, drug effect, or clinical recommendation into another study.

## Modality coverage boundary

This set directly supports language for:

- phase I, phase Ib, phase II, pilot, single-arm, multicohort, randomized-source post hoc, prospective imaging, retrospective clinicogenomic, and exposure-response studies;
- PD-1, PD-L1, CTLA-4, and TIM-3 blockade; toxicity-related discontinuation; rechallenge; primary and secondary resistance;
- CAR T cells, CAR NK cells, engineered TCR T cells, T-cell engagers, gene therapy, bispecific ADCs, and immunotherapy-targeted imaging;
- bulk and single-cell transcriptomics, TCR clonotype analysis, whole-exome and targeted DNA sequencing, immune deconvolution, and public-dataset reanalysis;
- global proteome and secretome LC-MS/MS, MHC-I immunopeptidomics, Olink proximity-extension plasma profiling, and cytokine panels;
- flow cytometry, CyTOF, imaging mass cytometry, IHC, immunofluorescence, Western blotting, ELISA, Luminex, ELISpot, and live-cell cytotoxicity assays;
- radiotracer synthesis, biodistribution, dosimetry, PET uptake, target occupancy, PK, exposure-efficacy, exposure-safety, and nonhuman-primate toxicology;
- cell-line, isogenic, coculture, syngeneic, CDX, PDX, PBMC-humanized, drug-tolerant-persister, and acquired-resistance models.

Proteomics support is **direct but narrow**. It comes mainly from one DKK3 study containing discovery-scale global proteome, secretome, immunopeptidomic, and plasma-protein analyses. Do not generalize one article into a journal-wide proteomic style profile, and do not relabel flow cytometry, imaging mass cytometry, Western blotting, or cytokine assays as proteomics.

This set does not establish clinical utility for any imaging, ctDNA, transcriptomic, or genomic biomarker; does not provide a randomized comparison of the newly investigated cellular or combination therapies; and does not establish human safety for the preclinical CAR or ADC platforms.

## Evidence-first labels

Before drafting, label each evidence unit on these axes:

1. **Design:** dose escalation, safety run-in, single-arm trial, multicohort trial, pilot study, post hoc randomized-trial analysis, prospective observational study, retrospective cohort, exposure-response analysis, or preclinical study.
2. **System:** patient, analysis population, tumor specimen, plasma, serum, single cell, cell line, coculture, CDX, PDX, syngeneic mouse, humanized mouse, or nonhuman primate.
3. **Measurement:** directly measured, pathologist scored, centrally reviewed, investigator assessed, sequencing derived, deconvolved, modeled, or inferred.
4. **Time structure:** baseline, pretreatment, on-treatment, landmark, post-discontinuation, rechallenge, serial imaging, or resistance state.
5. **Analysis status:** primary, secondary, exploratory, post hoc, subgroup, sensitivity, multivariable adjusted, or model selected.
6. **Claim tier:** descriptive, associative, prognostic, mechanistic, treatment predictive, comparative efficacy, dose selection, clinical utility, or safety.

Use the lowest defensible tier. A randomized parent trial does not make a post hoc biomarker analysis confirmatory. Concordant RNA, protein, and immune-cell findings strengthen coherence but do not alone establish mediation. A treatment-specific association is not a clinically usable predictive biomarker until its interaction, reproducibility, assay performance, and prospective utility are established.

- `model predictive check` - 模型预测检验

Exposure-response associations can be affected by disease severity, treatment duration, and informative dropout. Do not describe an exposure plateau as proof that higher doses are equivalent.

### Efficacy, safety, and statistical reporting

- `objective response rate` / `disease control rate` - 客观缓解率 / 疾病控制率
- `duration of response` - 缓解持续时间
- `best overall response` - 最佳总体缓解
- `investigator-assessed` / `independent central review` - 研究者评估 / 独立中心审评
- `median follow-up` - 中位随访时间；说明估计方法时保留
- `median was not reached` / `confidence interval was not estimable` - 中位数未达到 / 置信区间无法估计
- `exact Clopper-Pearson confidence interval` - Clopper-Pearson精确置信区间
- `Kaplan-Meier estimate` - Kaplan-Meier估计
- `Brookmeyer-Crowley confidence interval` - Brookmeyer-Crowley置信区间
- `Simon two-stage minimax design` - Simon两阶段极小极大设计
- `continuous reassessment method` - 连续再评估法
- `restricted cubic spline` - 限制性立方样条
- `time-varying Cox model` - 时变Cox模型
- `inverse probability weighting for missingness` - 针对缺失数据的逆概率加权
- `mixed-effects model` - 混合效应模型
- `Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery-rate control` - Benjamini-Hochberg假发现率控制
- `nominal P value without multiplicity adjustment` - 未经多重性校正的名义P值
- `wide confidence interval` / `imprecise estimate` - 宽置信区间 / 不精确估计
- `treatment-emergent adverse event` / `treatment-related adverse event` - 治疗期间出现 / 治疗相关不良事件
- `immune-related treatment-emergent adverse event` - 免疫相关治疗期间不良事件
- `dose-limiting toxicity` - 剂量限制性毒性
- `cytokine release syndrome` / `immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome` - CRS / ICANS
- `adverse event leading to discontinuation` - 导致停药的不良事件
- `no treatment-related deaths were reported` - 未报告治疗相关死亡；不等同于治疗安全

Report the estimate and confidence interval before the P value. Do not infer interaction from significance in one subgroup and nonsignificance in another. Do not call a null comparison `equivalent` unless an equivalence design and margin were prespecified.

## Paragraph architecture

### Biomarker paragraph

`biomarker definition and assay -> prevalence -> unadjusted association -> adjusted estimate -> interaction or comparator evidence -> TMB/PD-L1 dependence -> validation boundary`

`modality-specific sample sizes -> transcript-level signal -> protein-level signal -> cellular/spatial corroboration -> perturbational evidence -> unresolved mediation`

## Mandatory misuse checks learned from this set

1. Do not call an ICI-treated-cohort association predictive without a valid treatment comparison and interaction evidence.
2. Do not translate `stable disease` into `response`, or `disease control` into objective tumor shrinkage.
3. Do not describe a single-arm combination signal as synergy, resensitization, or superiority.
4. Do not suppress an unsafe dose level because a lower-dose cohort appeared feasible.
5. Do not convert post hoc TIM-3, STK11, LRP1B, ctDNA, PD-L1 PET, or B7-H3 RNA findings into clinically validated selection rules.
6. Do not call ctDNA clearance or molecular response a surrogate endpoint without formal validation.
7. Do not infer optimal ICI duration from a retrospective plateau or post-discontinuation cohort.
8. Do not present rechallenge outcomes as beneficial without accounting for strong selection and time-dependent bias.
9. Do not call a tracer predictive of immunotherapy efficacy when the study established only target-expression correlation and feasibility.
10. Do not equate bulk-RNA deconvolution with direct immune-cell counting or RNA abundance with cell-surface protein density.
11. Do not relabel imaging mass cytometry as mass-spectrometry proteomics.
12. Do not claim neopeptide generation when immunopeptidomics detected no increase.
13. Do not infer immune mediation solely from a humanized xenograft or from related-payload ICD experiments.
14. Do not convert mouse tumor regression, disappearance, or survival into patient efficacy or cure language.
15. Do not interpret absence of activation against nine normal-cell types as comprehensive human safety.
16. Do not treat wild-type NK cells as inert negative controls when natural cytotoxicity is substantial.
17. Do not claim CAR NK superiority over CAR T without a clinical head-to-head comparison.
18. Do not interpret an exposure plateau as dose equivalence or absence of benefit at every higher exposure.
19. Do not infer a PD-L1 treatment threshold from subgroup significance alone.
20. Do not omit confidence intervals, denominator changes, assay origin, or analysis status when they materially constrain the conclusion.
