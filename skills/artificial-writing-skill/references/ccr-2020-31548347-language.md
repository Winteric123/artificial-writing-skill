### Evidence reconstruction and numerical checks

Question and design: does Keap1 deletion increase drug resistance in Trp53-null murine lung cancer cells, and are KEAP1/NFE2L2/CUL3 mutations associated with poorer outcomes after first-line platinum doublet chemotherapy in metastatic human NSCLC, including KRAS-wild-type disease? The laboratory intervention can support a causal statement within its models. The human comparison is retrospective and observational and supports an outcome association under chemotherapy, not a validated treatment-predictive biomarker.

### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. Preserve the two internally incompatible adjusted HR/CI/P combinations; no corrected value is inferred. Safe qualitative phrasing is that the authors report loss of statistical significance for STK11 after pathway adjustment, with its numerical estimate unresolved.
2. Table 1 control histology totals 51 rather than 52. Do not invent the missing category or recalculate proportions as if complete.
3. The primary displayed chemotherapy endpoint is TTF, which includes non-progression treatment discontinuation. References to PFS in statistical prose and near Table 2 must not convert TTF into radiographic PFS.
4. The clinical evidence does not separate treatment-specific prediction from a generally adverse prognosis. Matching and a consistent KRAS-WT result do not establish a treatment interaction or causal human chemoresistance.
5. The small KRAS-mutant subgroup, individual NFE2L2/CUL3 groups and immunotherapy-exposed population are insufficient for the analyses the authors discuss. Insufficient power is uncertainty, not a negative effect estimate or evidence of equivalence.
6. Historical radiotherapy experiments, NRF2/ROS mechanisms, and a possible extension to chemo-immunotherapy are cited background or hypotheses; they are not new experiments or demonstrated outcomes in this study.
7. Unexpectedly long outcomes in the WT controls may reflect tertiary-center selection/referral and demographic composition. Retrospective single-center recruitment and residual confounding limit generalization.
8. Supplemental Figure 1 is referenced in the main text; the separate supplement was not supplied. No claim of full supplementary reading is made.
