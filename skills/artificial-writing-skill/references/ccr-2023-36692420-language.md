### Evidence reconstruction and numerical checks

| Evidence unit | Source-grounded reconstruction | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| CAR discovery | More than 50 anti-DLL3 antibody-derived constructs were screened before lead selection. | A large screen does not make every construct independently validated. |
| Antigen context | SCLC models expressed relatively low DLL3 density, often about 500–3,500 molecules per cell; PDX material was generally below 2,000 per cell. | Engineered cell lines and endogenous models must remain distinguishable. |
| In-vitro function | Candidate CARs were compared for cytokine production, cytotoxicity, low-density recognition, and repeated tumor-cell challenge. | Technical replicates are not independent biological subjects. |
| Lead construct | The 4-RSR construct showed the most consistent activity across the reported assay set and advanced to in-vivo testing. | Lead selection is an internal comparative result, not clinical optimization. |
| In-vivo activity | DLL3 CAR T cells produced marked tumor control, including complete regressions in several subcutaneous and systemic immunodeficient-mouse SCLC models. | Xenograft regression does not establish patient response or durability. |
| Safety switch | Rituximab-binding mimotopes enabled pharmacologic depletion of engineered T cells in model systems. | Demonstrated depletion is not proof that every clinical toxicity would be rapidly reversible. |
| Normal-tissue assessment | Mouse-cross-reactive CAR T cells entered intermediate/posterior pituitary tissue, without the reported structural injury or hormonal disturbance; brain/pituitary targeting was specifically examined. | Absence of observed injury in the tested models is not clinical safety. |
| Off-target assessment | Tissue cross-reactivity included inconsistent pancreas staining that required follow-up and orthogonal assessment. | Inconsistent staining should remain an unresolved assay signal rather than be dismissed. |

### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. The study is preclinical. Do not write that DLL3 CAR T cells are clinically effective or safe.
2. NSG and related immunodeficient hosts do not reproduce a complete human immune microenvironment, cytokine toxicity, trafficking, or graft-versus-host risk.
3. Complete responses refer to mouse tumor models. Preserve the model, CAR construct, dose, and observation window.
4. Pituitary infiltration without detected injury is a bounded observation; it cannot exclude delayed, species-specific, endocrine, or low-frequency toxicities.
5. Several assays use engineered target expression or technical repeats. Separate antigen-density experiments from naturally expressing SCLC and PDX models.
6. The rituximab off-switch demonstrates controllability in model systems, not guaranteed clinical rescue.
