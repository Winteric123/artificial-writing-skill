Evidence: Prospective multicenter open-label randomized phase II trial, NCT03330847, second/third-line PARP-inhibitor-naive advanced TNBC. Initially 1:1:1 olaparib, ceralasertib/olaparib, adavosertib/olaparib; after toxicity-related closure of the third arm, 1:1 for the first two. Molecular strata: BRCA1/2-mutated, other qualifying HRR-gene mutation, or no qualifying mutation in a specified 15-gene panel. Tumor-only testing did not establish germline origin. A non-HRRm classification does not establish functional HR proficiency. Primary endpoint: BICR PFS within each stratum. Stratified log-rank testing handles ties by Breslow; stratified Cox uses Efron ties and profile-likelihood intervals. Design used two-sided 10% significance and 90% CIs. ORR logistic regression adjusted for prior platinum; response confirmation was not required. No reported multiplicity control establishes a confirmatory subgroup response claim.

The adavosertib arm closed for unacceptable toxicity. Early overall termination followed BRCA-stratum PFS futility; the other-HRRm stratum remained underaccrued (20 per compared arm instead of approximately50). OS did not reach planned70% maturity and was not reported. Four AE-associated deaths were considered unrelated to treatment. No unexpected safety signal does not mean low toxicity. Exploratory oncogene-positive subgroup (30/103 non-HRRm patients) HR0.46,90%CI0.19–1.06 and P=0.0965 use different inferential procedures; preserve all values and do not recast the interval as excluding1 or assert validated treatment interaction. Follow-up1.9 months was computed only among censored patients, not reverse-KM overall follow-up. Table3 duration intervals are 25th/75th percentiles, not CIs. Table1 notes central BRCA-negative and unknown patients within the allocated BRCA stratum; distinguish allocation from central confirmation. RAD51 foci, methylation and genome-wide LOH are proposed ongoing analyses, not completed datasets in this article.

Claim ceiling: Primary PFS improvement was not demonstrated in any stratum. A secondary non-HRRm response signal does not reverse the primary result, prove survival benefit, or validate an HRR/replication-stress predictor. Cross-study dosing comparisons cannot establish that a different schedule would succeed. No direct STK11 or lung-cancer efficacy evidence.
