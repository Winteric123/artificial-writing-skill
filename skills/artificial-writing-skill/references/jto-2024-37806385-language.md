### Evidence, numerical checks and transfer boundaries

- Yale: 101 patients with stage IV EGFR-mutant NSCLC; 23 TP53-mutant/additional-TSG-mutant, 42 TP53-mutant/additional-TSG-wild-type and 36 TP53-wild-type. GENIE: 182 genomic cases, 179 with OS and 155 with PFS. These are different analysis denominators.
- Nine additional suppressors were screened, including STK11, ATM and CDKN2A; the final five-gene co-alteration classifier comprises RB1, NF1, ARID1A, BRCA1 and PTEN. Do not describe it as an STK11 classifier. The STK11 discussion cites other work rather than demonstrating a specific STK11 effect here.
- Yale sequencing was pretreatment in 37 and post-resistance in 64 cases. Paired analysis: 34 patients; 79% classification concordance overall, 89% in the TP53-mutant/TSG-mutant group. No significant pre/post change in tested TSG frequencies. Detection after treatment does not establish acquisition.
- GENIE relative mutation rate is normalized to the TP53-wild-type median, not directly an absolute TMB estimate. Broad platform/ctDNA/tissue differences and mixed sampling times constrain interpretation.
- Primary progression: 21.7%, 6.4%, 8.4%, P=.08; this is numerical, not statistically significant. Progression in at least three organs: 40.9%, 15.8%, 2.9%, P=.0009. No significant difference in the distribution of individual involved organ sites.
- Yale three-group first-line PFS medians: 8.0, 10.6, 12.6 months. TP53-mutant/TSG-mutant versus TP53-mutant/TSG-wild-type HR 2.03 (95% CI 1.12–3.69), P=.006. OS 30.0 versus 33.3 months, HR 1.58 (0.82–3.04), P=.12: do not call that contrast significant. Figure 3 labels the middle OS median 33.2 rather than text 33.3.
- Adjusted combined-status association: PFS HR 2.19 (1.24–3.87), P=.007; OS HR 2.29 (1.23–4.25), P=.009 (Table 2). Table age is displayed categorically but estimates look per-unit; do not reuse that age coefficient without clarification.
- GENIE: PFS 2.5, 7.7, 9.6 months; OS 25.0, 31.6, 54.2 months. OS versus TP53-mutant/TSG-wild-type is nonsignificant, HR 1.30 (0.72–2.36), P=.34. All GENIE patients received first-line erlotinib, not osimertinib; 68/101 Yale patients received first/second-generation first-line TKIs.
- Second-line osimertinib subgroup N=21, with groups 4/8/9. Several reported very small P values coexist with HR confidence intervals crossing one, for example PFS HR 5.93 (0.76–46.33), P=.0004 in Figure 3. Record the reported quantities and distinguish tests; do not silently repair them or use as a clean statistical template. Body and Figure 3 also differ on a first-line PFS P value (<.0001 versus .0006).
- No untreated/non-TKI comparison establishes treatment-specific prediction. Small subgroups cannot isolate each gene's contribution. Functional mechanisms discussed are prior evidence/hypotheses, not experimental results generated here. The study's own limitations distinguish prognostic from predictive effects.
