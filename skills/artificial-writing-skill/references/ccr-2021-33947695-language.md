### Evidence reconstruction and numerical checks

| Evidence unit | Source-grounded reconstruction | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Cohort | 200 pulmonary invasive mucinous adenocarcinomas were reviewed with clinicopathologic and molecular annotation. | Retrospective referral and tissue availability affect representativeness. |
| Testing workflow | Earlier cases used hotspot/KRAS-sensitive assays plus RNA testing; later cases used MSK-IMPACT and MSK-Fusion; unresolved cases underwent WES/WTS. | Platform and assay scope changed over time, so detection opportunity was not uniform. |
| Driver yield | Standard testing identified a driver in 96%; WTS increased the resolved proportion to 97%. | WTS rescue applies to selected unresolved cases, not the whole cohort. |
| Driver spectrum | KRAS alterations occurred in 76%, NRG1 fusions in 7%, and ERBB2 alterations in 6%; fusions accounted for about 51% of KRAS-wild-type tumors. | Percentages have different denominators when restricted to KRAS-wild-type disease. |
| NRG1 phenotype | NRG1-rearranged n=12 versus KRAS-mutant n=104 tumors were larger and more often had extrathoracic spread; survival appeared worse. | Small NRG1 sample, stage imbalance, and lack of definitive multivariable adjustment limit prognosis claims. |
| Novel events | The study reported an NRG2 fusion and one out-of-frame STK11 fusion among rare findings. | A single structural event is not evidence of recurrent STK11 biology. |
| Targeted treatment | Nine patients received matched or biologically directed therapy; responses were heterogeneous, including one complete response to ado-trastuzumab emtansine. | Case-level activity is not a response-rate estimate for a defined molecular subgroup. |

### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. Sequential testing eras create ascertainment differences; later comprehensive DNA/RNA profiling cannot be assumed for every patient.
2. Fusion prevalence among KRAS-wild-type tumors uses a restricted denominator and must not be quoted as prevalence in all invasive mucinous adenocarcinomas.
3. NRG1 survival comparisons are based on 12 cases, with baseline stage and metastatic-pattern differences; causal or independent prognostic claims are unsupported.
4. The nine treated cases received heterogeneous agents and should be narrated as individual evidence.
5. The STK11 out-of-frame fusion is a rare observation, not a basis for classifying this paper as direct STK11 evidence.
6. WES/WTS of unresolved tumors is a rescue workflow, not a uniform whole-cohort multi-omics design.
