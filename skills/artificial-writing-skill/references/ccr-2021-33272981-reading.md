## Research question, design and evidence layers

Question: how DNA-based NGS, targeted RNA-based fusion sequencing, break-apart FISH and RET IHC compare for RET fusion detection, particularly across fusion partners and tumor types.

Design: single-center retrospective diagnostic-assay comparison of MSK clinical testing,2014-01-01 through2020-05-09. Clinical reference cohorts were selectively co-tested, not every specimen receiving every assay. Most positive tumors are lung/thyroid and most partners KIF5B/CCDC6/NCOA4. A small observational treatment subset supports a caution about IHC-negative results; this is not a prospective therapeutic trial.

Safe tags: targeted DNA/RNA sequencing; structural-variant interpretation; kinase-fusion diagnostics; composite reference standard; assay sensitivity/specificity; partner-dependent detection; FISH cytogenetics; IHC localization/H-score; plasma cfDNA; internal threshold-development/validation. Source role: pan-cancer assay-methods reference with a predominant explicit NSCLC cohort, thyroid and other-tumor comparisons. DNA/RNA constitute two targeted molecular layers, not whole-transcriptome or proteogenomic profiling. IHC is not a proteome assay. No direct STK11/LKB1 analysis was identified.

Assays (pp2-3): MSK-IMPACT panels341/410/468genes, all covering RET coding sequence/introns9-11, with introns7-8 added in468gene panel. DNA SV calls manually reviewed. MSK-Fusion is a62gene targeted anchored multiplex PCR RNA assay, covering RET exons8-13; it is not untargeted RNA-seq. MSK-ACCESS plasma targets129genes. FISH cutoff is at least10% of assessed tumor nuclei with defined signal separation/atypical pattern, with at least100nuclei assessed. IHC cloneEPR2871; training-derived positivity is cytoplasmic staining in at least1% tumor cells, not any membranous/background signal. H-score integrates staining fractions and1+/2+/3+intensities; scores can range0-300 by formula. Pathologist scoring was blinded to fusion results, with a second adjudicator for equivocal cases. Confidence intervals use exact binomial limits.

## Source alerts, negative findings and claim limits

1. Introduction p1 describes the chimera with C-terminal coiled-coil/N-terminal kinase orientation, contrary to Methods p2's5'partner/3'RETkinase definition and Figure1C. Treat the introductory phrase as a source orientation conflict; use the Methods/diagram architecture with explicit disclosure, not that sentence as a model.
2. Some RNA-confirmed breakpoints differ from DNA predictions (12/34,including3initial out-of-frame cases); genomic breakpoint prediction is not interchangeable with mature transcript structure. The proposed splicing explanation is inferential.
3. The100%sensitivity estimate has a92.3%lowerCI limit and is conditional on the co-tested cohort and limited RNAreference; it is not proof of perfect real-world sensitivity. The reference RNAassay could miss rare alternative breakpoints; canonical105 cases and many negative controls were not RNAconfirmed.
4. FISH72%specificity/28%false-positive fraction comes from the deliberately chosen25negative controls,including8SVUS. The authors explicitly did not establish overall FISH specificity.
5. Low/noIHC expression did not preclude response in4selected cases. Do not use4/4 to establish unbiased efficacy, a response guarantee, or absence of expression-dependence.
6. Plasma5/16counts DNA structural variants,includingRNAnegative events,under heterogeneous disease status; it is neither overall liquid-biopsy sensitivity nor a ctRNA assay.
7. Other-driver exclusivity is selection logic in treatment-naive controls, not a universal rule. Five of6co-driver positive tumors were previously treated; this supports a resistance hypothesis/context, not proof all were newly acquired by this study's paired longitudinal assay.
8. IHC clone/threshold and partner composition are assay-specific; not allRETantibodies or cancer populations share the reported accuracy. Targeted RNApanel and IHC are not full transcriptomics/proteomics.
9. No standalone Conclusion heading. Final synthesis is Discussion p11. No randomized diagnostic-strategy comparison, independent external IHCvalidation or new systematic safety endpoint.
10. External supplementation remains not_supplied. Administrative p14 states49citedarticles while numbered references run1-54; this is publisher-link-sheet metadata, not a scientific denominator.
