## Safe transfer
Useful for window-of-opportunity design, enhancing versus nonenhancing sampling, free versus total drug, target modulation, noncompartmental PK and explicit negative PD language. IHC is not proteomics; LC-MS drug quantification is not untargeted proteomics. No clinical response or survival efficacy endpoint, and no STK11 evidence. Do not extrapolate the negative result to ALK-positive NSCLC brain metastases or override a different clinical trial population.
