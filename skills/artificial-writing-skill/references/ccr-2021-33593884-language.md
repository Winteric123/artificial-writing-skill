### Evidence reconstruction and numerical checks

| Evidence unit | Source-grounded reconstruction | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Surgical cohort | 604 stage I–III lung adenocarcinomas: KRAS wild type n=374, G12C n=95, other KRAS n=135. | Retrospective, single-institution surgical selection limits generalizability. |
| Recurrence | Three-year DFS was 68.7% for G12C versus 80.0% for other KRAS; unadjusted HR 1.82, P=0.029; stage-adjusted HR 1.84 (95% CI 1.01–3.36), P=0.046. | Borderline precision and limited event count require cautious wording. |
| Recurrence pattern | Cumulative recurrence incidence was numerically higher for G12C but not consistently statistically significant. | Do not convert a trend into a confirmed site-specific recurrence pattern. |
| Pathology within G12C | Lymphovascular and visceral pleural invasion were strongly associated with recurrence; LVI HR 9.57 had a wide CI. | Sparse events can inflate effect estimates. |
| Co-mutations | STK11 and KEAP1 co-occurred in the overall KRAS-mutant landscape; after correction, no gene was uniquely enriched with G12C. | Overall co-occurrence is not G12C-specific enrichment. |
| Clonality | KRAS was clonal in 90/91 evaluable tumors. | Clonality inference depends on purity/copy-number modeling and available tissue. |
| Mutational signatures | APOBEC SBS2 was identified in 16% of G12C versus 6% of other KRAS tumors. | Signature detection is computational and threshold dependent. |
| External cohort | TCGA n=476 showed worse 3-year DFS for G12C, 31.3% versus 54.2%, HR 1.88, P=0.007. | Absolute DFS differs markedly from the discovery cohort; validation supports direction, not transport of absolute risk. |

### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. Overall survival was not mature/evaluable enough for a definitive allele-specific OS conclusion.
2. Few G12C recurrence events yield wide confidence intervals, especially for pathology-stratified analyses.
3. The study predates routine perioperative KRAS G12C therapy and cannot establish adjuvant treatment benefit.
4. STK11/KEAP1 are part of the genomic context but were not shown to be specifically enriched in G12C after multiple-testing correction.
5. Discovery and TCGA cohorts have very different absolute DFS estimates. Do not pool them or present one absolute rate as universally expected.
6. APOBEC SBS2 enrichment is associative and does not prove a causal recurrence mechanism.
