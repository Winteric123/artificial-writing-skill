Claim ceiling: EGFR mutation classes associate with exposure history, molecular context and treatment duration in assembled observational cohorts. Harmonization, missing treatment sequence/timing, variable platforms, small treatment subgroups and possible treatment-induced co-alterations limit inference. Similar VAF distributions do not establish that EGFR and KRAS occur in the same cell or prove co-driver function. Immunotherapy utility remains an open hypothesis, not a demonstrated benefit. No new STK11-highlight designation.

### Abstract background
- `clinical and molecular heterogeneity` — 描述分子亚组差异，不自动意味着不同治疗敏感性。
- `The clinical implications of [variant class] remain incompletely characterized.` — 合成问题句，不能忽略已有研究。
### Abstract methods
- `multi-cohort genomic compendium` — 多个既有队列整合，不是单一前瞻性队列。
- `We compared [molecular classes] using harmonized genomic profiles and separately evaluated treatment-duration associations.` — 合成方法句，保留终点性质。
### Abstract results
- `co-occurring hotspot variants` — 共现与同一细胞内共定位不是同义。

- `These findings motivate further evaluation of [hypothesis] without establishing treatment selection utility.` — 探索性结论，不把关联升为预测标志物。

- `structural and functional variation` — 可用于提出解释层次，不能冒充实测蛋白功能。
