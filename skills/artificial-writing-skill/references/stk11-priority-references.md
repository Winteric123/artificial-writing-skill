These are abstracted writing architectures informed by the reviewed main articles, not verbatim source headings or a prescribed STK11 study protocol. The three CCR PMID sections in [ccr-2026-09-14-atm-smarca4-stk11-priority-language.md](ccr-2026-09-14-atm-smarca4-stk11-priority-language.md) and the four JTO PMID sections in [jto-2026-09-14-stk11-priority-language.md](jto-2026-09-14-stk11-priority-language.md) provide their source-page maps and evidence checks.

When outlining an STK11 manuscript, select and combine only the modules supported by the user's data:

- Introduction: clinical or biological problem → unresolved STK11/genomic-context question → study objective.
- Methods: define cohorts, STK11 variant/functional criteria, assay-specific analysis sets, clinical endpoints, and statistical models that were actually used.
- Results and figures/tables: organize the available evidence into cohort/variant characterization, co-mutation context, molecular or immune phenotype, prognosis/treatment associations, and experimental validation. Use the chosen paper's architecture to improve order; omit modules without data.
- Discussion: connect the main observations, compare appropriate prior evidence, distinguish clinical associations from experimental effects, and state claim-specific limitations.
- Abstract and conclusion: summarize the resulting evidence hierarchy and the actual principal findings.

Before drafting, identify which reference informs each proposed module and map it to the user's corresponding evidence. The framework designation does not authorize adding analyses, experiments, results, or figures that the user has not supplied or requested.

## Mandatory boundaries

- Treat STK11 as a molecular topic within the lung-cancer profile, not as a tumor type.
- Distinguish co-occurrence, mutual exclusivity, statistical interaction, biological cooperation, prognosis, and treatment-effect prediction.
- State the conditioning context for subgroup findings, especially KRAS status.
- Keep mutation status, predicted functional consequence, allelic status, RNA abundance, and protein expression separate.
- Preserve nonsignificant results and confidence intervals that include the null.
- Do not turn retrospective treatment exposure into randomized comparative efficacy.
- Do not reuse the internally inconsistent truncating-ATM estimate reported in PMID 37733794.
- For PMID 41932614, do not describe the adjusted ECD-versus-TKD PFS comparison as statistically significant; its reported CI includes 1 and P=0.06.
- For PMID 41619904, use Figure 4 when assigning exact univariable and multivariable STK11/CDKN2A HRs and disclose the mismatch with the Results prose when exact values matter.
- Do not convert the KRAS/STK11 association under cCRT followed by durvalumab into proof of STK11-mediated durvalumab resistance; the study has no non-durvalumab comparator or treatment interaction.
- For PMID 39864548, distinguish mono-allelic deletion from bi-allelic inactivation, keep the TCGA expression cohorts separate from the treatment cohorts, and disclose 19p13.2-13.3 co-deletion and the cohort-dependent ICI-alone results.
- Do not label the PMID 39864548 deletion association as a validated predictive immunotherapy biomarker; the retrospective non-immunotherapy control does not replace a randomized treatment interaction.
- For PMID 42409117, preserve its operational definition of MTAP loss as homozygous deletion with estimated gene copy number below 0.6; do not transfer that definition to STK11 or to PMID 39864548.
- PMID 42409117 contains no STK11/LKB1 analysis in the supplied main text. Use it only as a structural analogue and never as direct STK11 evidence.
- Keep all four JTO papers out of CCR bibliography, ledger, language-catalog, and corpus counts.
- Do not imply that unprovided supplementary files were reviewed.
