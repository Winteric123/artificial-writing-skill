Question/design: Prospective heterogeneous-treatment cohort of 43 well-differentiated metastatic NENs, 195 longitudinal plasma samples; 40 GEP and only three lung NENs. Lung NEN is not NSCLC or SCLC. sWGS/ichorCNA detects broad autosomal CNAs and estimates tumor fraction; this is genomic liquid-biopsy analysis, not transcriptomic, single-cell, spatial or proteomic profiling. Healthy reference: 100 nonidentifiable women undergoing prenatal testing, not an age/sex-matched cancer-screening cohort. One low-quality sample excluded, leaving 194 analyzed. The tissue classifier used separate public PNEN/PAAD data, not patient-matched tissue for the 43-person cohort.
