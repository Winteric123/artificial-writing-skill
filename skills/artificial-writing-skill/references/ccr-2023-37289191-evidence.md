## Evidence and numerical checks

- Screen of57 SCLC lines:15 IC50<30nM,9 between30–100nM,33>100nM. Highest-dose censoring matters. RB1 loss and cMYC are insufficient classifiers: cMYC high in11/24 sensitive lines but also8 resistant lines.
- RPPA and RNA analysis of57 lines plus81 untreated tumors are expression analyses, not81 treated patients. Continuous BCL2 group contrast P=.06 is nonsignificant; high/low expression association P=.011 uses a different analysis. Neither establishes a clinical cutoff.
- Seven isogenic pairs: BCL2 overexpression increased IC50, P=.004; knockdown restored sensitivity. BCLxL knockdown did not reproduce this in tested SCLC. Polyploidy, caspases, Annexin/PI and protein assays support a model-specific apoptotic mechanism; gamma-H2AX alone is not direct proof of all DNA breaks.
- Bliss delta-AUC is observed minus additive response; negative values indicate enhanced combination activity. Prespecified threshold -.1 reflects experimental variation. Do not reverse its sign or confuse it with HSA.
- AZD2811 nanoparticles in mice differ from free drug in vitro. Initial CDX/PDX experiments used10mg/kg, later25mg/kg; venetoclax100mg/kg. Across12 models,7 showed stasis/regression on AZD2811 alone and8 improved versus the best single agent on combination. Most groups n=3; one control n=2. A Figure4 improvement marker is not a formal interaction test.
- H211 schedule study n=9/arm:7-,5-,3-day venetoclax schedules yielded87%,65%,62% regression. Figure5 displays28% regression for concurrent2-day exposure, while prose emphasizes no added efficacy versus monotherapy. Staggered dosing and AZD0466 experiments support schedule optimization, not formal equivalence or proven reduced human toxicity. No regrowth for30days after stopping therapy is not cure; stable mouse weight is not comprehensive safety.

## Language and safe transfer

Use expression-defined resistance, isogenic perturbation, apoptotic priming, schedule-dependent activity, delayed regrowth, and target engagement. Separate baseline correlations from gene-perturbation evidence, protein abundance from kinase activity, and in-vitro synergy from in-vivo improvement. Reuse preclinical reporting architecture without suggesting an established STK11 therapy or adding a highlight.
