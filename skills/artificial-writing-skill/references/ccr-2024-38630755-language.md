### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. The two cohorts are small and use different PD-L1 inhibitors; replication is directional rather than a pooled confirmatory analysis.
2. Each tumor contributed multiple ROIs. Statistical models used repeated-measure handling, but ROI count must not be mistaken for patient count.
3. YAP1 expression was low and broadly distributed; the SCLC-Y label is method dependent and overlaps with the triple-negative/inflamed state.
4. Target expression was measured at the mRNA level only. DLL3, CD47, B7-H3, and checkpoint transcripts do not prove targetable protein levels.
5. There was no platinum–etoposide-only control arm, so long-term-benefit signatures cannot be called specific predictors of PD-L1 blockade benefit.
6. NDUFB8 results rely on uncorrected P values; do not present them as validated biomarkers.
7. Limited ROIs likely underestimate or distort intratumoral subtype diversity.
