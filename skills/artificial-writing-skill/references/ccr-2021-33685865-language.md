### Evidence reconstruction and numerical checks

| Evidence unit | Source-grounded reconstruction | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Prevalence | 106 of 6,290 NSCLC cases (2%) had EGFR exon 20 insertions, representing about 7% of EGFR-mutant cases. | Institutional testing patterns and referral can affect prevalence. |
| Comparator | Exon 20 insertion cases were compared with classic EGFR mutations and with a 2:1 group lacking a targetable driver. | Different comparisons answer different questions and must not be merged. |

### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. The study reflects a historical period before current exon 20–directed therapies and must not be used as current treatment guidance.
2. Several treatment analyses use time to discontinuation, not RECIST PFS.
3. Immunotherapy and chemoimmunotherapy groups are small, line-of-therapy distributions differ, and treatment assignment was nonrandom.
4. Genomic testing availability selected the cohort and may introduce referral/ascertainment bias.
5. “Lower STK11/KEAP1 frequency” is descriptive and does not establish a distinct functional immune state.
6. Race associations are cohort observations and should not be essentialized as biological ancestry effects.
