The main text reports 153 tTMB-available patients but describes/displays only 78 durvalumab and 48 durvalumab-plus-tremelimumab patients (126 total). CONDOR also had a tremelimumab-only arm, but the main text does not explicitly assign the remaining 27 biomarker-evaluable cases to it; do not treat that arithmetic attribution as verified patient flow. EAGLE bTMB n=247 (84 durvalumab, 83 dual ICI, 80 SoC) was a subset of a negative primary-OS phase III trial (ITT HRs 0.88/1.04). Biomarker-evaluable HRs 0.70/0.75 already differ from ITT, indicating possible selection.
