### Evidence and study map
- Prospective open-label nonrandomized phase2 umbrella HUDSON NCT03334617:941 enrolled/screened overall,324 screened for these modules,268 treated. Modules1,2,3,5, not four randomized arms;79 durvalumab–ceralasertib,87–olaparib,45–danvatirsen,57–oleclumab. Matched88/nonmatched180; contemporaneity not guaranteed, danvatirsen cutoff2020 vs others2022 (pp2–6,15–17).
- Original targeted tissue/plasma DNA, bulk tumor RNA49, blood NanoString770+30 genes48 and TCRβ DNA repertoire; IHC ATM/PD-L1/CD73. No WES, scRNA, spatial RNA, global proteomics or new animal perturbation. Serial blood RNA is targeted expression, not whole-transcriptome RNA-seq. Tumor TIS subset belongs to pooled other regimens, not ceralasertib (pp7–10,16–17).
- Primary ORR13.9%(11/79) vs pooled2.6%(5/189), all confirmed partial responses; PFS5.8(80%CI4.6–7.4) vs2.7(1.8–2.8), OS17.4(14.1–20.3) vs9.4(7.5–10.6). These are descriptive nonrandomized signals, not controlled superiority or 95%CIs (pp3,6,24).
- ATM biomarker-matched23 includes sequence alteration OR low ATM protein; ORR6/23=26.1%,PFS8.4/OS22.8. Fig4 sequence-variant subgroup22 has OS24.1; do not merge with matched23. Alteration definition includes SNV/indel/CNA/rearrangement and/or expression criteria, not synonymous with narrow sequence co-mutation (pp8,15–16,25).
- STK11/LKB1 olaparib-matched21: main report ORR4.8%, embedded ED Fig1 PFS1.4(80%CI1.4–1.8),OS5.8(5.0–10.8). Not evidence that PARP treatment overcomes STK11 resistance. Durvalumab–danvatirsen had no responses in45, so the preclinical STAT3 rescue hypothesis is not established clinically; not a trial exclusively in STK11 mutants (pp3,11,20).
- Fig4 STK11-variant ceralasertib13 vs other26: PFS8.4(5.6–9.5) vs1.7(1.4–3.0);OS20.3(11.0–20.4) vs7.4(6.0–15.0), all80%CIs. No randomized treatment interaction; do not convert to a predictive biomarker claim (p8).
- Tumor TIS STK11-altered5 vsWT38 P=.405; absence of significance ≠ equal immune states, and samples are post-ICI. Histology TIS P=.0517 not significant at.05. Fig3a annotation2732.27 conflicts with plotted2372.28; don't reuse exact score (p7).
- Serial blood signatures use linear mixed models; TCR expansion Fisher/BH q<.01, newly detected clones >5reads, paired Wilcoxon and BH across tests. Clonality changes do not prove tumor antigen specificity or tumor infiltration. Fig5 schematic is proposed mechanism, not direct demonstration (pp8–10,17).
- Grade≥3 TRAEs16/79=20.3% vs57/189=30.2%; TEAEs35/79=44.3% vs97/189=51.3%. Fatal TEAE2/79 and5/189, none judged treatment-related. Do not equate treatment-emergent death with treatment-caused death (pp3,6,27–29).
- Source cautions: Fig1 legend modules1–4 disagrees with1,2,3,5; primary/acquired counts30/49 in Table1 vs28/51 in Fig4 use different classifications and need clarification; Fig5TCR plot64baseline vs paired62 in text; ED Fig2 PD-L1 counts16/18 conflict with main8/24. Methods equates Simpson clonality with1−Pielou evenness, which should not be reused as a mathematical definition. Report exact assay metric without propagating that equality.
- Writing logic: mechanistic rationale → biomarker assignment → prespecified efficacy/safety → negative modules → exploratory serial biomarkers → nonrandomization/assay heterogeneity → need for controlled validation. Link with CD2021 STAT3 paper as preclinical-to-clinical discordance, not a single concordant efficacy story.
