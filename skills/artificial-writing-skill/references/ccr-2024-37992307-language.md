# Timing-dependent chemotherapy–checkpoint interactions

## PMID 37992307

Mariniello et al., Platinum-Based Chemotherapy Attenuates the Effector Response of CD8 T Cells to Concomitant PD-1 Blockade. CCR2024;30:1833–1845; DOI10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-23-1316. Online November22,2023; issue May1,2024. Translational Cancer Mechanisms and Therapy. Original basic immunology/treatment-scheduling experiment motivated by NSCLC; actual model is chronic/acute LCMV infection in mice, not lung tumors or patient efficacy. The related commentary is not included.

Coverage: Abstract/Introduction pp1–2, Methods pp2–3, Results pp3–9, Discussion pp8–11, TR p2; all six figures pp4,5,7–10 visually read, regimen table embedded in Fig1. References pp12–13; supplements cited but not read. Main reading complete, not acceptance rechecked.

Methods: female C57BL/6J,clone13 chronic infection with transient CD4 depletion, treatment≥45days postinfection; cisplatin2.5mg/kg+pemetrexed300mg/kg and anti-PD-L1 clone10F.9G2 10mg/kg, intraperitoneal every3days for5doses. Anti-PD-L1 blocks the PD1 pathway; do not relabel as an anti-PD1 antibody. Flow cytometry/tetramers Gp33/Gp276; TCF1/TIM3 stem-like, CX3CR1 or CD101−TIM3+ transitory, CD101+TIM3+ terminal subsets; Ki67 proliferation, peptide-stimulated IFNγ, Vero plaque assay viral titers. Independent experiments2–3 with3–6mice/group; log10 counts, ANOVA/multiple comparisons, t test for scheduling comparison. Each dot animal, not a technical replicate. Sequential5chemo doses then5antibody doses starting2days after chemo; contemporaneous comparison described with PBS during initial interval.

Findings: concomitant chemo restricts antibody-induced absolute CD8 expansion and proliferating transitory effectors; quiescent stem-like counts relatively spared by chemo alone, but not universally protected after activation. Some proportions remain similar despite lower absolute counts. IFNγ-positive absolute count combination versus antibody alone is a trend(P.07), not significant, while relative proportion decreases. Combination splenic viral titers2.8-fold higher than antibody alone(P.03). Sequential versus concomitant improves cell expansion, IFNγ-positive numbers5.3-fold(P.01), and viral control(2.8-foldlower,P.0001). These are infection-model immune readouts, not tumor response/OS. Acute antigen-expansion experiment is described in body but its supplement panels are not directly checked.

Interpretive limits: quiescence/genome-protection mechanisms are partly inferred, not demonstrated by lineage tracing or DNA-repair perturbation. Two-week window does not establish long-term stem-cell maintenance. Removing tumor antigen-release effects clarifies one mechanism but prevents evaluation of the net antitumor benefit of chemo-ICI. No clinical recommendation to stop pemetrexed, shorten chemotherapy, or replace concomitant therapy follows from this experiment. No omics assay performed here; cited single-cell studies are background.
