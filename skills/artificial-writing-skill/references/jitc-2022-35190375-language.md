### Evidence and numerical checks

Post-hoc retrospective exploratory genomic subgroup analysis of randomized open-label phaseIII trial. 1202 randomized; this paper's ITT1047 excludes EGFR/ALK, rather than equating ITT with all1202. Mutation-evaluable920, SP263 PD-L1-evaluable774, intersection684. Follow-up39.4months, cutoff2019-09-13. Baseline plasma ctDNA targetedNGS, not WES or tumor-tissue sequencing; includes known/likely/unknown functional-impact variants but excludes synonymous variants. A detected mutation is not demonstrated LKB1 functional loss. PD-L1 is targeted tissue IHC, not proteomics. There is no new single-cell/spatial sequencing or functional wet-lab mechanism here.

Treatments: ACP atezolizumab+carboplatin/paclitaxel; ABCP additionally bevacizumab; BCP bevacizumab+carboplatin/paclitaxel. Unstratified Cox HRs and Kaplan–Meier outcomes compare each atezolizumab arm to BCP, not a powered direct ABCP-versus-ACP hypothesis. VEGF immunomodulation is cited background, not measured causal mediation. Baseline liver metastases are imbalanced across some subgroups.

Limitations: post-hoc subgrouping; underpowered small groups; plasma-detection sensitivity and missing tissue mutation calls; variant functional heterogeneity; prognostic imbalances; no functional-loss assay; overlapping genes and PD-L1 differences. The paper's predictive wording is hypothesis-generating, not validated interaction evidence. Safety is described as similar in supplementTableS1, which was not supplied: no detailed safety validation is claimed. This source supports context-aware comparison with KEYNOTE-042 and other STK11 trials, not universal immunotherapy resistance or a regimen recommendation.
