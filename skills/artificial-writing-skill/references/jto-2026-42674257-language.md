### Evidence and study map
- Question: can DNA and RNA EGFR allele fractions refine recurrence-risk heterogeneity within EGFR-mutant LUAD, and what does TP53 add? Taiwan initial191 cases led to RFS133 untreated stageI–III cases and a separate TKI analysis48 (recurrence32, adjuvant16). Singapore initial86 led to RFS54 and TKI17. Do not assume every analysis set is a nested subset without the missing flowchart (pp8,11–13).
- WES uses paired tumor/normal; EGFR exon19 deletion or L858R prioritized when multiple variants occur. TP53 truncating and nontruncating classes are distinguished. RNA quantification uses Salmon, TMM/edgeR and mutant-aware transcript deconvolution; RNA VAF is mutant transcript counts divided by mutant plus wild-type transcript counts, not total gene expression or a direct clone-size measurement. PUREE estimates purity; CIN is inferred from copy-number profiles (pp8–10).
- Statistical chain: distribution-appropriate group tests → KM/log-rank → adjusted Cox with age/sex/stage/EGFR type/TP53/purity → linearity/collinearity checks → parsimonious and Firth sensitivity fits → LASSO selection and unpenalized refitting. Model checks and sensitivity outputs partly reside in unsupplied supplements; post-selection estimates and optimized thresholds remain exploratory (pp9–12).

Discordant DNA/RNA classifications occurred in34/133 (25.6%); RNA VAF averaged14.8 percentage points above DNA VAF, P<.001.
