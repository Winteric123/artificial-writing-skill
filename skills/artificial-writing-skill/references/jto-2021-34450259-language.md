# KEAP1/TP53 genomic, evolutionary and immune subtypes

- Direct STK11 co-mutation context, but primary four-group classifier is KEAP1/TP53. Sequence-level cross-gene co-occurrence, evolutionary inference, immune-treatment outcome analysis. KEAP1 single-mutant means TP53-wild-type, not absence of STK11/KRAS/other mutations. Tumor-suppressor mutation is not uniformly validated functional loss: study did not apply variant pathogenicity prediction.
- Multi-cohort computational/reanalysis study: GENIE targeted DNA n6208; WES n667; TRACERx multiregion WES n60; TCGA bulkRNA n506; public ENCODE A549 ChIP-seq/DNase-seq/CAGE/RNA and K562 ChIA-PET; CCLE and immune signatures. Distinct datasets/models, not matched multi-omics on all6208 patients. No original scRNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, global proteomics or functional knockout/rescue established.
- Clinical identification set OAK/POPLAR atezolizumab bloodNGS n304; tissue validation Rome/MSKCC/DFCI n343; pooled647. No randomized chemotherapy-arm comparison performed in this analysis. Prognosis within ICI-treated cohorts is not established predictive treatment interaction.
- Figure1: KEAP1-SM enriched for STK11 (71% versus36% in double-mutant figure panel), KRAS and SMARCA4. Co-occurrence/mutual-exclusivity used top15 genes; neutral statistical interaction is not biochemical independence. CNVs and synonymous variants excluded from survival grouping.
- Figure2: MATH across four groups overall Kruskal-Wallis P<.001; VAF co-clustering suggests, but does not prove, KEAP1/STK11 same-cell clonality. Purity/copy-number and assay heterogeneity matter. REVOLVER multiregion trajectories remain inferred rather than longitudinal cell tracking.
- Figures3–4: four-group OS separation; KEAP1-SM worst and double-WT best. However KEAP1-SM versus KEAP1/TP53-DM pairwise P=.065 in blood cohort and .174 in tissue cohort, nonsignificant; pooled comparison P=.040. Do not turn the descriptive ranking into significant pairwise superiority in both validation cohorts. Tissue Kaplan–Meier baseline n333 (37+40+143+113), whereas caption cohort343; model evaluable counts also differ. Report assay/outcome-specific denominators, not every analysis as343.
- Figure5: AKR superenhancer inference uses cross-cell-line public tracks; a K562 chromatin loop is not a directly measured lung-tumor loop. PROGENy estimates pathway activity from bulk transcripts, not biochemical activity measurement.
- Figure6: IFN-gamma response NES−1.59/FDR.057 (not FDR<.05); KRAS signaling NES1.83/FDR.028. Inferred immune desert/signature scores are not directly counted cell populations. Predicted neoantigen burden is not validated antigen presentation.
- Reusable STK11 logic: four mutually exclusive genotype groups → co-occurrence scan → VAF/heterogeneity/evolution → external clinical validation → regulatory/transcriptomic/immune integration. Limitations: retrospective treated-only cohorts, unequal panel coverage, missing histology/covariates, small genotype groups, public cross-dataset mechanism inference.
