## Evidence and denominators
Of 168 eligible randomized afatinib ± cetuximab participants, 106 had baseline plasma, 98 cycle-3-day-1 plasma and 77 progression plasma. These are overlapping time-point populations, not 106 fully serially sampled patients. Baseline EGFR ctDNA was detected in 82/106. Among 81 paired baseline/8-week patients, 62 were baseline positive: 47 cleared and 15 remained positive. The body calls 15 “19%,” using all 81 rather than 62; Figure 1 correctly displays 15/62=24%. Another baseline-negative patient became positive, accounting for Table 1's 16 C3D1-positive paired cases. All-three-time-point population is 36; C3D1/progression paired population 46. Table 1's orange caption says BL/PRG but displayed columns are C3D1/PRG. Record rather than silently reconcile.

Methods pp3–5: archived plasma Guardant360 NGS, central tumor pyrosequencing in 89 available cases, probe-level CNG normalization and eight-week landmark analyses. Variant-class detection limits differ; per-position specificity is not patient-level sensitivity. Driver MAF and multi-variant PMR are different biomarkers. PMR uses proprietary filtering and can retain CHIP contamination; TP53 analysis explicitly did not discriminate tumor versus CHIP origin. No matched-leukocyte validation should be invented.

Results pp6–8, Figure 2 p14: among the 62 baseline-positive paired cases, clearance associated with PFS HR 0.23 (0.12–0.45), medians 15.1 vs 4.6 months; OS HR 0.44 (0.21–0.90), medians 32.6 vs 15.6. Figure axes explicitly measure months from day-60 landmark, not directly from randomization. This is prognostic/on-treatment association within treated patients, not a treatment-by-biomarker interaction proving treatment selection. RECIST response at C3D1 did not distinguish PFS (HR0.97), although OS association was significant; do not say imaging predicts no outcome whatsoever. Table 3 responses 9/14 vs24/45 at C3D1 use measurable-disease subset rather than all62.

Baseline binary detectability loses conventional significance after adjustment (OS HR2.23, CI0.97–5.15, P.06; PFS HR1.30, P.38), while continuous log-MAF associations adjusted for CNV were significant. PMR complete response associations nonsignificant for PFS (HR.67, P.16) and OS (.83, P.60). CNG occurred in25/106, not24% of baseline-positive82 as Discussion implies. CNG-associated OS HR1.74, CI.99–3.06, P.05 is borderline, despite stronger narrative; PFS nonsignificant. MAF medians/ranges differ when zero-including, positive-only and paired populations change; preserve population qualifiers. TP53 clearance30/46 versus persistent16/46 associated with PFS HR.44, but not necessarily tumor-specific TP53 clearance.

## Interpretation and language use
Introduction pp2–3 moves from serial biomarker measurement to therapy-specific uncertainty. Discussion pp8–11 treats low shedding, CNG-inflated MAF, CHIP, missing blood draws and afatinib rather than modern first-line extrapolation. No clinical intervention based on clearance was tested. Conclusion proposes intervention trials; it does not establish a validated surrogate endpoint. Translational Relevance p2 and result paragraph structure support cautious biomarker reporting. No direct STK11 analysis or new highlight designation. Genomics/liquid-biopsy/bioinformatics/targeted-therapy/statistics facets, not single-cell or spatial profiling.
