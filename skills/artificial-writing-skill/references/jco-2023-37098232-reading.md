## Question, design and analysis boundaries

This report asks whether responses to sotorasib remain durable with approximately two years of follow-up and whether baseline markers distinguish long benefit from early progression. CodeBreaK 100 is a multicenter, open-label, single-group phase I/II trial (NCT03600883). The pooled 960-mg once-daily population contains 174 patients: 48 phase I and 126 phase II. Phase I's primary endpoint was safety/tolerability; phase II's primary endpoint was blinded-independent-central-review ORR. This report is an extended pooled analysis, not a new randomized comparison.

At the February 22, 2022 cutoff, median treatment duration was 5.6 months (0.2-35.9); 13 patients remained treated. Prior therapy included PD-(L)1 inhibitors in 157/174 (90%) and both platinum chemotherapy and PD-(L)1 inhibitors in 144/174 (83%); the table does not establish that all such patients received concurrent chemoimmunotherapy. Table 1 includes two phase-I patients with zero prior metastatic lines, so avoid saying every participant had an identical pretreatment history.

The efficacy set was 172, whereas OS used 174. Biomarker availability was narrower: 114 phase-II patients had centrally measured PD-L1 and/or genomic data. Long benefit was PFS >=12 months; early progression was PFS <=3 months **without a PR/CR**. Patients between those extremes are not in the Figure 2 comparison. Therefore Figure 2 percentages are not all-patient genotype-specific response rates or probabilities of benefit.

## What the paper supports for STK11 writing

Sotorasib can produce durable benefit in some KRAS G12C NSCLCs harboring STK11 alterations. The STK11-only/KEAP1-WT contrast is materially different from KEAP1-altered disease. This challenges a blanket claim that all STK11-mutant tumors are intrinsically refractory to every systemic therapy, but does not establish STK11 as a favorable predictive marker or prove STK11-mutant/WT equivalence.

The study has no contemporaneous untreated or alternative-treatment comparator. Small exploratory biomarker subsets, extreme-outcome selection, tissue/plasma differences and overlapping alterations limit causal/predictive interpretation. Historical chemotherapy comparisons and CodeBreaK 200 results in the Discussion are contextual literature, not randomized evidence generated by CodeBreaK 100. No molecular perturbation experiment establishes why a subgroup benefits.
