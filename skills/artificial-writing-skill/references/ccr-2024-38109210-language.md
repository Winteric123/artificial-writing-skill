Supplied14-page HHS author manuscript; header final-edited date February16,2024, repository availability August16 not publication date. Existing official category Precision Medicine and Imaging. Methods: digitized waterfall/swimmer data from10 published subprotocols,435 patients; cutoffAugust2022, two crizotinib subprotocols excluded for low combined accrual9. Tumor types with<3 or miscellaneous labels not tested. Monte Carlo10^7 resamples, one-sided tumor-type sensitivity question, mean tumor-size change primary and PFS HR secondary; BH FDR.25. Comparator is all treated patients in each subprotocol, not untreated controls. Non-evaluable patients imputed to mean or maximum progressive-disease change for two sensitivity analyses. PFS influenced by histology growth rate, so no efficacy conclusion from PFS alone.

Findings: seven signals in six/ten subprotocols, four previously below aggregate ORR threshold16%. Dabrafenib/trametinib BRAF V600E gynecologic6(mostlyLGSOC) and biliary4; capivasertib AKT1E17K breast15; AZD4547 FGFR urothelial5; trametinib BRAF non-V600 lung7; weak afatinib HER2 ER+breast10 and binimetinib NRAS biliary6. Lung mean change−22% versus about−4/−5% overall,P.026, three/seven>30% target-lesion reduction is not confirmed RECIST ORR. No heterogeneity detected in four other arms is not equivalence/tumor-agnostic proof. Weak binimetinib signal disappears under worst-case imputation. Only gynecologic dabrafenib/trametinib signal also has PFS support by the study's permissive multiplicity rule; HR.50/P.056 is not nominalP<.05.

Boundaries/source alerts: statistical superiority versus a poor overall cohort can lack meaningful absolute activity (−3%/−10% signals). FDR.25 is not per-comparison alpha.05 or a posterior probability each finding is true. Methods incorrectly defines a P value as probability the null is true given data; never learn that sentence. Multiplying observed discoveries by the FDR target is not an exact realized false-positive count, nor proof that all-false probability<.0001. 'Tumor volume' digitized from RECIST plots must not be assumed volumetric imaging. Prose FDA capivasertib/paclitaxel approval claim and current-guideline statements are unverified historical assertions, not reusable current guidance. Biological tissue context and mutation-spectrum composition remain confounded.
