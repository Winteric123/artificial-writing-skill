### Evidence and transfer boundaries

- S1400 screened 1864; 1841 submitted tissue; 1672 successful NGS (90.8% of submitted), from 436 sites. Previously treated stage IV/recurrent SqCC, 2014–2019. The 750+ sites refers to sites having the study open, not 436 enrolling sites. Trial-screening genomic analysis is not itself a randomized efficacy comparison.
- 313-gene research panel; 36,677 total variants. SV here means short variants, NOT structural variants; rearrangements are a separate category. TP53 SV 90.0%, LRP1B 41.4%, MLL2 35.6%, CDKN2A 26.8%, FAT3 25.8%. Panel analyses include variants of varying functional certainty; do not label all as pathogenic drivers.
- TCGA comparator 495 largely earlier-stage SqCC; gene-level SV prevalence correlation r=.92, with higher rates in S1400 (paired test P=7.95e-18). Stage, prior therapy, assays and germline filtering may differ; correlation is not assay interchangeability or evidence therapy caused mutations.
- Biomarker-defined alterations in 1325/1672 (79.2%); 480 (28.7%) potentially eligible for multiple substudies if concurrently open. This is theoretical biomarker eligibility, not actual enrollment, approved actionability or response. Figure 2 survival groups are overlapping mutation-defined sets, not randomized treatment arms; x-axis days.
- 1219 archival and 453 fresh biopsies; no significant differences on measured sequencing properties/OS, not an equivalence study. Current/former/never smokers 34.6/61.4/4.0%.
- MEGSA analyzed 71 genes with prevalence >6%: NFE2L2/KEAP1/PARP4 FDR4.1%; CDKN2A/RB1 FDR13.1%. Report the latter's permissive 15% discovery threshold, not FDR<5%. SELECT separately analyzed 336 events (261 short variant, 69 amplification, six deletion) >1%; 41 mutually exclusive and 95 co-occurring pairs at FDR<5%. Chromosome-local CNAs merged to avoid trivial co-occurrence. This is a different algorithm/event unit/threshold.
- KRAS/STK11 co-occurrence P=1e-7 appears in text among S1400-specific discoveries, but not the subset drawn in Figure 4. This is directly relevant to SqCC co-alteration language, not an STK11 treatment-response effect or same-cell/phased proof. Report P versus FDR separately.
- NFE2L2-or-KEAP1 SV associated with poorer OS (P=.016), detailed survival curve in an unavailable supplement. Do not invent an HR/CI. Mechanistic KEAP1/NRF2 discussion references prior work.
- PARP4 altered in 247 patients (14.8%); 409 SV observations, of which 284 (69.4%) at I1039T/Q1059R/T1170I, occurring in 44/112/128 cases. Counts overlap; 30 tumors contained all three and 59 contained Q1059R+T1170I. Do not sum event counts into distinct patients or infer allelic phase.
- AlphaFold proximity and cross-species conservation generate functional hypotheses, not demonstrated protein dysfunction. PARP4 absent from the commercial assay discussed; only 240/313 research genes overlap. TCGA PARP4 15/495 (3.0%), East Asian LUAD16/305 (5.2%); different ancestry/histology/stage cannot establish acquired platinum resistance.
- Source caution: Figure 1D S1400 stage cells 8+60+288+1313=1669, while displayed total is1672. Missing stage explanation is not clear in main text. The prose/legend alternate original-diagnosis versus biopsy-time stage; do not silently harmonize. Figure 2 category labels/counts should not be used as treatment assignment counts. Functional/germline filtering and matched-normal details are insufficient to independently adjudicate recurrent PARP4 variants; retain this uncertainty.
