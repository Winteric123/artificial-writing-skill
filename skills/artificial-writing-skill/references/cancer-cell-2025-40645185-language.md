# PMID 40645185 — language-retention note

## Section-aware vocabulary and collocations

- **replication-stress vulnerability** — Abstract/Discussion term for susceptibility rooted in fork/DDR stress.
- **compensatory ATR-CHK1 activation** — Results phrase for KEAP1-loss signaling; distinguish pathway activity from drug response.
- **genotype-dependent ATR-inhibitor sensitivity** — Results wording that prevents universalizing across KRAS/STK11/KEAP1 states.
- **isogenic genotype series** — Methods phrase for K, KL, KK, and KLK comparisons.
- **convergent dependency through distinct mechanisms** — Discussion synthesis for LKB1 and KEAP1 loss.
- **immune-cold baseline state** / **treatment-associated immune remodeling** — keep baseline and post-treatment observations separate.
- **heterogeneous active-control regimens** — Limitations wording for the HUDSON comparator.
- **exploratory clinical signal** — correct label for post hoc PFS/OS findings.

## Synthetic sentence frames

1. “Loss of **[gene A]** increased basal replication stress, whereas loss of **[gene B]** enhanced compensatory **[checkpoint]** signaling; both states converged on ATR dependence.”
2. “The isogenic series separated the individual and combined effects of **[alterations]**, with the largest response observed in **[genotype]**.”
3. “Activity in **[genotype 1]** but not **[genotype 2]** argues against using **[single alteration]** as a context-free biomarker.”
4. “The post hoc cohort showed longer PFS, whereas the OS confidence interval crossed the null; the result is clinically suggestive but not confirmatory.”
5. “Immune remodeling was demonstrated in murine tumors and should not be presented as a measured human on-treatment effect.”

## Paragraph logic

For a vulnerability paragraph: genotype-defined stress state → pathway readout → isogenic perturbation → pharmacologic response → in vivo replication → negative genotype/context → human correlative support. For a clinical bridge: define post hoc population and comparator → report PFS with CI → report OS and negative interval → state small subgroup → deny predictive validation.

## Safe transfer

- Preserve KRAS, STK11/LKB1, and KEAP1 genotype jointly; “STK11-altered” alone is too broad for several key results.
- Separate original preclinical experiments, TCGA/ICON tissue reanalysis, and HUDSON post hoc clinical evidence.
- Use “associated with” for HUDSON and human proteomics; reserve causal verbs for isogenic or knockout experiments.

## Unsafe transfer

- Do not state that STK11 or KEAP1 alteration is a validated ATR-inhibitor biomarker.
- Do not call the OS result positive when its confidence interval includes 1.
- Do not generalize KLK anti-PD-1 combination benefit to KL, where the corresponding comparison was negative.
- Do not infer acceptable human toxicity from cell-line or mouse experiments.

## Indexed language units

- `Loss of [gene A] increased basal replication stress, whereas loss of [gene B] enhanced compensatory [checkpoint] signaling; both states converged on ATR dependence.`
- `Activity in [genotype 1] but not [genotype 2] argues against using [single alteration] as a context-free biomarker.`
- `genotype-defined stress state → pathway readout → isogenic perturbation → drug response → in vivo validation → negative context → exploratory human bridge`
