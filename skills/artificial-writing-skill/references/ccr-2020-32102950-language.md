### Evidence reconstruction and numerical checks

Question: does pretreatment plasma TMB associate with response and survival during first-line pembrolizumab-based treatment, and can selected mutation findings refine the observed stratification? Prospective enrollment does not make the derived cutoff or exploratory composite a prospectively validated predictive test.

Statistical reconstruction (p4): nonnormal pTMB was compared using rank-based tests or nonparametric bootstrap tests of medians; response used logistic regression; continuous-pTMB linearity was assessed using a restricted cubic spline. Survival used Kaplan-Meier/log-rank and Cox models, with proportional-hazards assessment using Schoenfeld residuals. Potential confounders were examined individually alongside binary pTMB; this is not evidence of one model jointly adjusting for every listed factor. The DCB analysis reports no covariate changing the OR by more than 15% and therefore no adjusted OR calculation (p5). Two-sided P<.05 was the stated threshold; a multiple-testing correction is not reported for the exploratory mutation/composite analyses. No detailed safety analysis is supplied in the main article.

### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. The p5 week-9 response statement prints OR 1.09 with 95% CI 1.02-1.08 and P=.018. The point estimate lies outside the printed CI. This is an unresolved source inconsistency, visually confirmed; do not silently correct the estimate or CI, or reuse this numerical pair as validated.
2. The Discussion p7 prints 52/66 as 79.8%, whereas the Abstract p2 and Results p5 print 78.8%. Preserve 52/66 and identify the conflicting percentage; do not propagate 79.8%.
3. Methods p3 and Results p5 define DCB as CR/PR/SD lasting more than six months, whereas Figure 1B caption p12 says CR/PR/SD as of six months and describes NDB as progressive disease. The time-boundary and SD wording differ. Use the formal Methods definition with an alert when reconstructing endpoints; do not treat the caption as identical.
4. Figure 2A negative-mutation/DCB P=.060, mutations-alone/PFS P=.110, composite/PFS P<.001 and composite/OS P=.009 belong to different analyses. Neither nonsignificant molecular comparison is rescued by the composite's significance.
5. The source title and some Discussion wording use predictive language. This single-center nonrandomized treated cohort establishes outcome associations and a candidate stratification hypothesis; it does not distinguish prognostic from treatment-specific predictive value. Regimen assignment by PD-L1 prevents treating monotherapy versus combination as a randomized comparator.
6. A matched clinical tissue-TMB analysis was unavailable; immune competence, tumor microenvironment, MHC status and microbiome were not studied. Proposed biological explanations and cited studies do not become new assays in this article. Separate supplements remain unsupplied.
