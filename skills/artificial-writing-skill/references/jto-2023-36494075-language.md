### Evidence, discrepancies and transfer boundaries

- Single-center retrospective cohort327, with252 osimertinib alone and75 combinations (57 chemotherapy/anti-VEGF,16another EGFR TKI,2ICI). Median follow-up23.9months (IQR13.6–33.9), rwPFS13.7(95%CI12.6–16.8), OS35.2(32.4–38.5), TTD16.4(13.8–18.4). PRISSMM-derived real-world progression is not RECIST central review. TTD exceeds PFS partly because therapy continued beyond progression.
- Baseline NGS283: TP53/RB1 co-altered50, TP53-only141, neither92;44unknown not wild type. Corresponding rwPFS10.5,13.5,18.8months. Adjusted association for combined TP53/RB1 P=.015; atypical EGFR P<.001. Main text does not give adjusted HR/CI: do not invent from P values. AtypicalEGFR29 with median5.8months; classicalL858R113/ex19del185, medians16.4/15.2; P=.8 for classical contrast.
- Postprogression-biopsy95 is a selected subset of250progressors, not all327. 50/95 known resistance (~52.6%; text53%, abstract52%); off-target24, transformation14, on-target12, unknown45. METamp9, EGFRamp7, C797S3, G724S2; classifications of amplification versus mutation versus histologic change must remain distinct.
- Transformation reporting is internally inconsistent: p6 lists six pureSCLC, four pureSqCC, twoLCNEC, oneLCNEC/SCLC, oneLCNEC/SqCC (14total); subsequent p7 reports sevenSCLC plus onecombined with baselineTP53/RB1. Earlier p6 mentions15/50 TP53/RB1 cases transformed, exceeding the14postbiopsy transformation count. Figure2 groups mixed histologies differently (SCLC7,SqCC5,LCNEC2). Preserve source context and do not reconcile or derive gene-specific transformation risk without supplement/data clarification.
- Table1 paired/post-only biopsies74/21, whereas Figure2 table displays67/28. Atypical postbiopsy text15 (8paired/7post-only) differs from Table1 six+five. These discrepancies limit granular subgroup claims. Table2 has printed H7773R nomenclature; do not silently normalize to a specific mutation without sequence evidence.
- Biopsy89/95 from progressive sites; five of six nonprogressive-site biopsies had no mechanism identified. This reflects sampling sensitivity/selection, not proof that unknown mechanisms are absent.
- Postprogression resistance-category survival P=.07; no significant difference does not mean categories lack prognostic value. Figure3 y-axis is labeled progression-free survival although caption/text describe postprogression survival; use the actual analysis context and flag the label.
- Tailored second-line26, nontailored59, no subsequent10. Time-dependent Cox reports24-monthHR.09 versus nontailored (abstractP=.006); no main-text CI supplied. Avoid claiming a constant HR across time or causal survival benefit: confounding by performance, actionable findings, access and treatment selection remains despite time-dependent modeling.
- RNAfusion testing40/95:25unknown-mechanism,15known. Three fusions of uncertain significance in unknown group are not established resistance drivers. Serial single case ERBB2amp->METamp->CD74-ROS1 illustrates evolving heterogeneous findings; normalized relative allele frequency is a clonality proxy, not exact single-cell lineage proof.
- Signature/TMB analysis39paired sufficiently pure samples. APOBEC dominance10%pretreatment versus28%post, P=.07 (nonsignificant); TMB4.4->5.3mut/Mb,P=.008. WGD61->71%,P=.3 and FGA P=.12 nonsignificant. Signaturecalling requires >=5SNVs and purity>=20%; low-count cases shown separately in Sankey, not all classified by a robust dominant signature.
- Only four post-treatment tumorsTMB>=10, three transformed; this does not demonstrate immunotherapy benefit. Retrospective selection, heterogeneous combinations and incomplete tissue follow-up prevent causal claims about treatment-induced transformation or improved survival from tailored care. Trial/status and treatment recommendations are historical, not current clinical guidance.
