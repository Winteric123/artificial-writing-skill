## Synthetic sentence frames

1. **Genotype interaction:** “The combined **[alteration A/B]** state showed a stronger **[pathway phenotype]** than either single alteration, supporting cooperative rather than merely additive reprogramming.”
2. **Nutrient dependency:** “Sensitivity to **[nutrient withdrawal/inhibitor]** was enriched in **[genotype]** models, although **[exception]** did not follow the dominant pattern.”
3. **Isogenic causality:** “Re-expression of **[tumor suppressor]** or suppression of **[effector]** shifted the dose–response curve, and the dual perturbation produced the largest loss of sensitivity.”
4. **Mechanism:** “Inhibition reduced **[ATP/GSH/NADPH]** and increased **[ROS]**, linking the growth phenotype to both bioenergetic and redox disruption.”
5. **Rescue logic:** “Rescue by **[metabolite]** was model dependent, indicating that the dependency could not be assigned to a single downstream branch across all cell lines.”
6. **Translational ceiling:** “The genotype enriched for response in xenograft and PDX models, but prospective clinical predictive value remains untested.”

## Paragraph architecture

### Genotype-to-vulnerability paragraph

Begin with human co-occurrence, separate the four genotype groups, describe pathway enrichment, then move to isogenic perturbation and finally drug response. This progression distinguishes human association from causal model evidence.

### Metabolic-mechanism paragraph

Order evidence as nutrient withdrawal or pharmacologic perturbation → growth/cell-cycle effect → redox and energetic readouts → metabolite rescue → model-specific exception. The rescue data should narrow, not over-simplify, the mechanism.

### Preclinical-translation paragraph

Report dose and model genotype, state the responsive and nonresponsive comparators, and close with the absence of patient-level efficacy. A selective mouse response is evidence for model stratification, not a validated treatment biomarker.

## Unsafe transfers

- Do not write that every STK11-mutant tumor is glutamine dependent; KEAP1/NRF2 context and model exceptions matter.
- Do not describe CB-839 as clinically effective on the basis of xenografts or PDXs.
- Do not omit the nonresponsive KP PDX/A549-KEAP1 controls or the H1355 exception.
- Do not convert partial metabolite rescue into proof of a single causal pathway.
- Do not repeat the Figure 2 caption's “higher NRF2 with LKB1” direction without noting that it conflicts with the Results text and visual data.
