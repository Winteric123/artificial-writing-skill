### Evidence reconstruction and numerical checks

| Evidence unit | Source-grounded reconstruction | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Preclinical rationale | Low-dose gemcitabine increased camonsertib activity in isogenic ATM-WT/KO cells, a BRCA1-deficient breast line, and ATM-deficient lymphoma/BRCA1-mutant breast xenografts. | Models were not lung-cancer models and do not establish tumor-agnostic efficacy. |
| Trial design | TRESR module 4 was a phase I/IIa subpart; 76 molecularly selected, heavily pretreated patients received multiple doses and schedules. | The analysis is nonrandomized and designed primarily for dose/safety characterization. |
| Population | Ovarian n=41, pancreatic n=8, breast n=6; “other” included NSCLC n=3. Frequent enrollment genes were BRCA1 n=30, BRCA2 n=20, and ATM n=14. | Lung cases were too few and not separately analyzed. |
| Safety | Neutropenia occurred in 64.5% overall and 50.0% at grade 3+; anemia 59.2%/31.6%; thrombocytopenia 35.5%/13.2%. DLTs occurred in 23/63 (36.5%). | Safety denominators differ between all treated and DLT-evaluable patients. |
| Schedule | A 28-day, one-week-on/one-week-off strategy reduced neutropenia-driven dose modification relative to the heterogeneous 21-day cohorts; DLT rate at the proposed expansion dose was 7/26 (26.9%). | Cross-schedule comparisons are not randomized and combine different doses. |
| Activity | Of 67 efficacy-evaluable patients, ORR by RECIST or tumor marker was 8/67 (11.9%); RECIST PR was 7/66 (10.6%), including six confirmed. CBR was 30/67 (44.8%); median PFS was 19 weeks. | Tumor-marker and RECIST response definitions must not be merged without labeling. |
| Gynecologic subset | ORR was 7/40 (17.5%), CBR 20/40 (50.0%), median PFS 32.6 weeks; at the proposed expansion dose ORR was 2/24 (8.3%). | This is a selected subgroup and not applicable to NSCLC. |
| ctDNA | The paper states that 35 patients had paired plasma samples but reports molecular response in 22/36 (61%), an internal denominator conflict. | Preserve the conflict; do not silently repair 35 versus 36. |

### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. This is not lung-specific evidence. The three NSCLC participants are listed only in the broad “other” group, without an NSCLC response estimate.
2. Dose and schedule cohorts evolved during the study. Avoid causal comparisons between arms or schedules.
3. The proposed expansion schedule improved tolerability but had suboptimal activity, and the authors state that further optimization is needed; do not call it a settled RP2D.
4. Hematologic toxicity persisted even at low gemcitabine doses. “Low dose” does not mean low risk.
5. The ctDNA methods/results contain a 35-versus-36 denominator discrepancy for molecular response.
6. Responses at higher starting doses could partly reflect gemcitabine exposure or a priming effect; the combination contribution is not separable in this single-arm study.
7. Reversion detection is limited by assay coverage and structural-variant ascertainment. A nonsignificant comparison is not evidence that reversions are irrelevant.
