### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. The clinical gene panels did not include KEAP1 (p11). The patient association cannot establish a KEAP1-adjusted STK11 effect or clinical STK11/KEAP1 co-mutation interaction. The KEAP1/NRF2-dependent mechanism is supported in experimental models, not measured in those patients.
2. Figure1 LRR/DM panels use wild-type n150 plus mutant12, whereas DFS/OS use152+12. Figure2 KP is20 for DFS and19 for LRR. Reasons for every endpoint exclusion are not resolved in the supplied main text; retain endpoint-specific denominators.
3. Figure3G and Figure5A–C display MitoSOX, whereas Methods describe DCFDA with excitation/emission labels written as nM (pp4–5). These probes and units are not interchangeable. Do not claim a single fully reconciled ROS protocol; the wavelength-unit typo must not be copied as a concentration or silently treated as validated methodology.
4. p9 says KEAP1 knockdown in a paragraph/figure about re-expression; Figure3G labels the restoration experiment. The direction of a knockdown-specific redox claim is unresolved. Use model/construct labels and do not copy the contradictory sentence.
5. The p10 prose gives LKB1-restored CB-839 DER.8, while Figure5I displays.9 (KEAP1 panel.8). Do not reuse the exact restored-LKB1 value without source clarification; the supported transferable observation is attenuation of radiosensitization. Figure3D also prints P=.022 whereas prose reports .023; avoid pretending exact agreement.
6. Figure4 caption describes7–8/group and KEAP1 n7/group, but the KEAP1 control risk table starts at8; Methods irradiation volume250–300mm³ differs from caption250–350mm³. Preserve this experimental reporting uncertainty rather than fabricate one reconciled protocol.
7. Table1 mutant median age is printed58.5(range78–73), an internally impossible range. Do not correct it by guessing. Tables2–4 label a T-stage contrast with≤7/>7 whereas Table1 groups T0/T1/T2 versusT3/T4; do not translate the former into verified centimeter thresholds or restage cases from it.
8. Figure3H shows viable-cell fractions; LKB1 contrast P=.0603 is nonsignificant, KEAP1 contrast P=.0164 is significant. Figure5D combination-versus-RT P=.119 is nonsignificant. Increased redox signal, viable-cell loss and clonogenic radiosensitization are related but separate readouts.
9. The cohort lacks a non-RT treatment comparator and treatment-by-biomarker interaction analysis. Adverse outcomes after RT alone do not validate an RT-specific predictive marker. Clinical treatment predates routine durvalumab; immunotherapy resistance and immune mechanisms in Discussion are cited context, not this cohort's immunotherapy results.
10. CB-839+RT is a preclinical hypothesis. A cited earlier mouse drug study must not be attributed to the present experiments. Independent supplements and acceptance source recheck remain outstanding.
