## Question and experimental logic

The authors test whether STK11/LKB1-deficient tumor cells inhibit dendritic-cell antigen handling and T-cell priming via a secreted factor. They combine reanalysis of prior K/KL mouse scRNA-seq, newly engineered Stk11-knockout LLC lung and Panc02 pancreatic allografts, DC recruitment/reconstitution, human lung-cell secretomics, AGR2 perturbation, FOXA1 regulation and DC ARG1 biochemical assays. The proposed axis is LKB1-AMPKalpha-dependent control of FOXA1/AGR2 in tumor cells, followed by secreted AGR2-mediated DC dysfunction with ARG1 stabilization.

Direct STK11 functional evidence is present. LLC lung, Panc02 pancreatic and human A549/H460 in vitro systems must remain separate. The therapeutic efficacy experiments are mouse anti-PD-1 plus tumor FLT3L overexpression or ovalbumin-loaded DC delivery, not a human DC-vaccine or AGR2-inhibitor trial. ARG1 inhibitor CB-1158 rescue is an in vitro experiment. Public human survival/expression analyses are contextual associations, not a validated STK11-stratified treatment predictor.

## Actual assay provenance and methods

Original: Stk11 CRISPR-knockout tumor models; human LKB1 restoration and AGR2 knockdown; DC uptake/migration and antigen-specific co-culture; flow cytometry/ELISA; tumor-secretome DIA mass spectrometry on Orbitrap Astral; recombinant AGR2 pull-down/interactome LC-MS on timsTOF; BMDC bulk RNA-seq after AGR2 versus IgG; original ChIP-qPCR; immunofluorescence and protein-turnover assays. Secretome and DC-interaction proteomics have different sample sources and cannot be merged as a tumor tissue proteome. No original spatial transcriptomics or scRNA-seq is generated here. The scRNA-seq is reused GSE180963, also generated by PMID36512628 in this same intake batch; these are not independent data cohorts.

Bulk RNA-seq DEG criterion absolute log2FC>=1 and FDR<0.05. Secretome nomination uses P<0.05 and absolute log2 change>1; IP-MS uses P<0.05 and fold change1.5. Do not transfer RNA FDR control to all proteomic comparisons. Public TCGA/GTEx/GEPIA/CIDE and immune-deconvolution analyses are reanalyses, and public FOXA1 ChIP-seq only motivates the direct ChIP-qPCR experiment.

Mouse tumor implantation 2-5 million cells subcutaneously; Methods state randomization/blinding. BMDC generation uses GM-CSF2 ng/mL plus FLT3L200 ng/mL. LPS maturation concentration differs across stated assay protocols (100 ng/mL in general culture versus10 microg/mL in the RNA-seq description); do not homogenize them into a single universal protocol. Anti-PD-1 is200 microg/dose, but dosing-day descriptions conflict as noted below.

## Source alerts and claim ceiling

1. Results p.7 and Fig.3B-C p.9 conflict on TCF1/TIM3 subset direction. Preserve plotted nonsignificant stem-like fraction and increased terminal-like fraction as displayed, and exclude the conflicting prose statement from language reuse pending clarification. Do not label the discrepancy resolved by choosing a preferred narrative.
2. Results p.13 describes increased ARG1 accumulation, whereas Fig.7D p.14 MFI quantification decreases with AGR2 (P=0.0068). Other images/CHX assays support the proposed model but do not resolve that specific quantitative contradiction. Withhold a direction-specific Fig.7D accumulation claim.
3. General Methods p.3 anti-PD-1 schedule is days1/4/7/10, whereas Fig.4A p.10 scheme shows days5/7/9/11. Record protocol-specific conflict; do not synthesize a single exact dosing schedule without source clarification.
4. Background p.2 overcompresses an ARG1-to-kynurenine route; Discussion p.15 invokes ARG1/IDO1 interplay. ARG1 catalyzes arginine-to-ornithine metabolism, whereas kynurenine is a tryptophan-pathway product. No isotope-flux result here establishes direct ARG1 production of kynurenine; exclude that causal phrasing.
5. Antigen uptake, migration, MHC-II markers, SIINFEKL-H2Kb display and T-cell priming are distinct endpoints. Unchanged peptide-MHC-I display and several maturation markers forbid a blanket statement that antigen presentation/maturation is entirely abolished.
6. The compound C effect on FOXA1 abundance is not reproduced by PRKAA1 knockdown; the convergent promoter-occupancy effect is narrower. Avoid declaring all compound C effects AMPK-exclusive.

## Limitations and safe Chinese reuse

The source supports a tumor-secreted AGR2/DC interaction model through loss/rescue experiments, but human-cell/mouse DC systems, artificial OVA antigen, subcutaneous models, broad drug effects and unresolved figure/text inconsistencies limit translation. Genetic FLT3L expression is not identical to an available FLT3L regimen; OVA-loaded DC rescue is not a demonstrated clinical STK11-directed vaccine. RNA/protein associations cannot establish the cell source without supporting experiments. No evaluated AGR2-targeted clinical treatment is shown.
