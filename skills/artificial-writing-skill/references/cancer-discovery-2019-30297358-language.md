# PMID 30297358 — language-retention note

## Vocabulary and collocations retained

- **attenuated cytosolic-DNA sensing** — calibrated pathway-level description.
- **epigenetically silenced STING state** — use only where methylation/chromatin evidence is shown.
- **restore pathway competence** — preferable to “reverse immune resistance” when only signaling output is restored.
- **reconstitute interferon-related output** — appropriate for IFNβ/chemokine induction assays.
- **immune-cell migration in a spheroid model** — keeps the assay platform visible.
- **selected human-tumor subset** — useful warning when correlative panels are smaller than the source cohort.
- **pharmacologic delivery constraint** — separates target biology from compound feasibility.
- **mechanistic rationale rather than clinical validation** — safe translational boundary.

## Sentence frames

1. “Compared with **[molecular comparator]**, **[genotype]** tumors showed reduced enrichment of **[pathway]** and lower expression of **[key component]**.”
2. “Re-expression of **[gene]** restored **[pathway component]** in **[state A]**, whereas **[epigenetic intervention]** was additionally required in **[state B]**.”
3. “Upon **[stimulus]**, restoration of **[component]** increased phosphorylation of **[signal proteins]** and induction of **[effector genes]**.”
4. “This assay demonstrates pathway competence in vitro; it does not by itself establish immune-mediated tumor control in vivo.”
5. “The inability of **[compound]** to reach the relevant cellular compartment highlights a delivery limitation distinct from target validity.”

## Paragraph architecture

For immune-mechanism writing: establish the genotype-pathway association → distinguish epigenetic states → show stimulus-response rescue → connect to immune-cell or tumor-cell phenotype → state delivery/model limitations → avoid jumping to clinical efficacy.

## Unsafe transfers to avoid

- Do not describe the study as a clinical efficacy study.
- Do not claim that STING-low status predicts checkpoint-inhibitor response.
- Do not collapse STING-low and STING-absent models into a single reversible state.
- Do not imply that the tested agonist achieved effective intracellular delivery.

## Indexed language units

- `Re-expression of [gene] restored [pathway component] in [state A], whereas [epigenetic intervention] was additionally required in [state B].`
- `Upon [stimulus], restoration of [component] increased phosphorylation of [signal proteins] and induction of [effector genes].`
- `The inability of [compound] to reach the relevant cellular compartment highlights a delivery limitation distinct from target validity.`
