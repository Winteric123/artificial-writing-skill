# PMID 28538732 — language-retention note

## Vocabulary and collocations retained

- **maintain pyrimidine pools** — useful for describing a metabolite-supply function without overstating pathway exclusivity.
- **sustain DNA synthesis** — connects metabolic availability to a functional cellular output.
- **noncanonical nitrogen utilization** — concise framing when a familiar enzyme is used outside its canonical tissue pathway.
- **selective metabolic dependency** — suitable only when comparator models show materially weaker dependence.
- **channel ammonia-derived nitrogen toward pyrimidine synthesis** — mechanistic verb phrase for isotope-supported flux.
- **induce replication stress** / **shorten replication-fork tracts** — distinguish molecular consequence from generic growth inhibition.
- **rescue the growth defect** — use only where supplementation or re-expression causally reverses the phenotype.
- **retrospective prognostic association** — protects against mislabeling a survival correlation as treatment prediction.

## Sentence frames

1. “Loss of **[tumor suppressor]** was associated with increased **[enzyme]** expression, and re-expression of wild-type—but not **[inactive mutant]**—reversed this phenotype.”
2. “Stable-isotope tracing indicated that **[substrate-derived atom]** was incorporated into **[product]**, supporting a role for **[enzyme/pathway]** in **[biosynthetic process]**.”
3. “Genetic depletion reduced **[metabolite pool]**, increased **[stress marker]**, and impaired **[functional readout]**; supplementation with **[specific metabolites]** partially rescued these effects.”
4. “Although high **[marker]** expression was associated with shorter survival, the small retrospective subgroup supports a prognostic, rather than predictive, interpretation.”
5. “The combination enhanced antitumor activity in xenografts, but clinical benefit and biomarker utility remain untested.”

## Paragraph architecture

Mechanistic result paragraphs can follow: phenotype discovery → genetic directionality → isotope or rescue evidence → functional consequence → model boundary. Translational paragraphs should then separate human prevalence/prognosis from preclinical intervention evidence and end with an explicit non-predictive caveat.

## Unsafe transfers to avoid

- Do not convert technical tumor fragments into independent patient counts.
- Do not call CPS1 a validated clinical target or CPS1-high status a cisplatin-predictive biomarker.
- Do not describe the noncanonical pathway as a complete, functional urea cycle.
- Do not transfer exact survival thresholds to another cohort without re-estimation.
