### Evidence reconstruction and central-result checks

- Question/design/population: investigator-initiated, prospective, single-center, open-label phase I/II study of intravenous CVA21 plus pembrolizumab in heavily pretreated advanced NSCLC. - Analysis sets: safety was the prespecified primary endpoint; RECIST 1.1/irRECIST activity and an immune-cell-rich tumor microenvironment were secondary. WGS included nine tumors; MIBI included eight paired biopsies, 47 fields of view, and 69,784 classified cells. Numerous nominal tests were run without a multiplicity plan.

### Evidence reconstruction and central-result checks

- Question/design/population: post hoc clinico-genomic/transcriptomic analysis of previously treated advanced KRASG12C-mutant NSCLC receiving single-agent adagrasib 600 mg twice daily in KRYSTAL-1 expansion cohorts (parent n=129). This is a single treatment cohort with no untreated, chemotherapy, or alternative-KRAS-inhibitor comparator.
- Analysis sets: pretreatment tissue NGS was available for 121, but gene-specific panel coverage produced different denominators (STK11 n=100; KEAP1 n=91; joint KEAP1/STK11 groups n=89). Pretreatment HTG EdgeSeq expression was available for 68; the composite KEAP1/STK11/NRF2 analysis used 50 with all required data. RECIST 1.1 ORR, PFS, and OS were compared by chi-square/log-rank/Cox. No multivariable clinical model, formal treatment-by-biomarker interaction, or multiplicity plan for the many genomic/signature/subgroup analyses was reported.
- Preclinical chain: KRASG12C cell lines, a genotype-stratified CDX/PDX panel, an approximately 1,000-gene in vivo CRISPR screen, two engineered LU99 KEAP1-knockout clones, and drug-combination assays were used. These experiments can support model-specific biologic plausibility but cannot convert the single-arm human associations into a clinically predictive biomarker.

- Source inconsistency: the Discussion says NRF2-high/continuous score was negatively correlated with both PFS and OS “even in the absence of KEAP1mut” (p9), yet the main Results and Fig. 5E–F show KEAP1wt PFS r=−0.39, P=0.022 and OS r=−0.24, P=0.165. The broad KEAP1-independent OS claim is withheld; only the displayed PFS association is retained. A smaller supplementary median-split subgroup, which was not supplied, does not override the main continuous-analysis null.
- Figure-label boundary: Fig. 6 abbreviates the red comparator as `KMUT/SMUT/NHIGH`, whereas the Results define it as patients harboring KEAP1mut, STK11mut, **and/or** NRF2-high. It is treated as “at least one adverse feature,” not a triple-positive group.
- Prognostic-versus-predictive boundary: all human patients received adagrasib, so clinical associations cannot distinguish general disease prognosis from relative adagrasib benefit. KEAP1 perturbation supports a drug-response mechanism in specific models but does not supply the missing human treatment interaction; STK11 experimental results are explicitly mixed.
- Limitations: post hoc single-arm analysis, incomplete and gene-specific NGS coverage, local assay heterogeneity, small joint-genotype groups, no adjusted clinical model, no treatment comparator/interaction, numerous nominal genomic/signature/cutoff comparisons without multiplicity control, transcriptome n=68/composite n=50, weak continuous correlations, median/tertile/quartile exploration, same-cohort composite derivation and evaluation, no external clinical validation, selected cell-line/PDX/CDX contexts, mixed STK11 perturbation results, no clinical combination or safety data, sponsor involvement, and unsupplied supplements.
- Claim ceiling: KEAP1, STK11, and NRF2 features stratified survival within adagrasib-treated KRASG12C NSCLC; KEAP1 loss also produced adagrasib resistance phenotypes in selected models. These findings are treatment-context/prognostic and mechanistically hypothesis-supporting, not validated human predictors of relative adagrasib benefit. STK11 specifically was associated with PFS/OS but not ORR, and its direct resistance mechanism remains unresolved.
