### Evidence reconstruction and central-result checks

- Question/design/population: prospective, multicenter, open-label, single-arm phase II study at 16 Japanese institutions. Fifty patients with untreated, unresectable stage III NSCLC were enrolled before concurrent chemoradiotherapy (CCRT); 47 formed the efficacy/safety set after one eligibility violation and two surgical-treatment deviations. Eligible patients were age 20–74, ECOG PS 0–1, able to receive 60 Gy/30 fractions, and excluded for definitive interstitial pneumonia, autoimmune disease, or continuous corticosteroids at least 10 mg.
- Treatment/analysis sets: the strategy was two cycles of platinum-doublet CCRT followed by durvalumab 10 mg/kg every two weeks for up to 12 months, intended from the next day through day 5 after radiation. Forty-two of 47 received durvalumab; five did not because of COVID-19 pneumonia, pneumonitis/febrile neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, prolonged radiation interruption with pneumonitis, or myelosuppression. Forty of 42 were evaluable for PFS from durvalumab start, whereas all 42 contributed OS from durvalumab start.
- Statistical design: the primary endpoint was IRC-assessed one-year PFS from registration before CCRT, not from durvalumab initiation. The historical threshold was 50%, expected value 63%, power 80%, and one-sided alpha 20%; the design used a 60% CI and also reported a 95% CI. The planned 42 patients became 47 for ineligibility/dropout allowance. There was no randomized or contemporaneous delayed-start control.

- Source alerts: (1) the primary endpoint begins at registration before CCRT and includes patients who never received durvalumab; (2) the one-sided alpha was 20% and the planned decision interval was 60%, although the 95% CI also excluded 50%; (3) 42/47 received durvalumab and 40/42 contributed PFS from its start; (4) one participant started on day 8, outside the stated next-day-to-day-5 window; (5) the Results prose reports the IRC median-PFS lower CI as 13.2 months, whereas the abstract, Table 2, and Figure 1 report 13.4 months; (6) ORR spans CCRT plus consolidation and is not a durvalumab-specific response rate; (7) AEs were collected from registration and cannot be assigned solely to durvalumab; (8) PACIFIC comparisons are nonrandomized and use different clocks; and (9) supplementary treatment, pneumonitis-onset, PD-L1, and comparative tables were not supplied.

- Claim ceiling: this study met a prespecified single-arm one-year PFS benchmark for a selected CCRT-to-early-durvalumab strategy and described its short-term toxicity. ### Evidence reconstruction and central-result checks

- Question/design/population: retrospective, multi-institutional biomarker cohort of advanced NSCLC patients treated with PD-1-axis blockers. Investigators collected 179 pretreatment whole sections from resections and core biopsies across five centers; four failed quality review, leaving 175 for pathologist visual classification.
- Assay/method chain: centrally performed multiplex immunofluorescence measured cytokeratin, CD4, CD8, and CD20 over entire sections. Single-cell segmentation assigned cells to tumor or stroma. For each FOV and lineage, the tumor-to-stroma ratio of within-compartment lymphocyte proportions was binned, and unsupervised K-means clustering used lineage-specific ratio distributions. FOVs without the lineage were excluded: 5.77% for CD8, 9.37% for CD4, and 50.70% for CD20; 29 patients had no detectable CD20 cells.
- Statistical chain: the Methods state that specimens with only tumor or stroma or with less than 1 mm² tumor were excluded from objective analysis (n=24). Survival was reported as five-year OS after ICI using Kaplan–Meier/log-rank comparisons; the main Methods do not explicitly define its time origin. Binary pooled comparisons were exploratory and selected after similarity of survival patterns. No multivariable adjustment, treatment interaction, multiplicity correction across lineages/groupings, response analysis, or untreated control was reported.

### Evidence reconstruction and central-result checks

- Question/design/population: secondary, hypothesis-generating reanalysis of published NCI-MATCH basket-trial subprotocols to ask whether particular histologies were more sensitive than other histologies receiving the same genotype-matched treatment. Ten of 12 subprotocols published through August 2022 were included; two crizotinib subprotocols with only nine total patients were excluded. The abstract reports 435 patients across the analyzed subprotocols.
- Data/method chain: individual tumor-volume change (TVC) and PFS data were digitized from published waterfall and swimmer plots, not obtained as trial source data. Tumor types followed primary reports with stated exceptions. For each histology, 10 million Monte Carlo samples generated a one-sided null distribution for greater sensitivity relative to the pooled trial. TVC was primary; PFS was secondary and interpreted only when concordant with TVC.
- Multiplicity/missingness: Benjamini–Hochberg control used FDR 25% across 39 subgroup tests. The authors approximate two false discoveries by multiplying the seven observed calls by the FDR target. That calculation is not an exact realized false-positive count, a per-finding posterior probability, or a general consequence conditional on seven discoveries; the review does not endorse it or the source's all-false-probability argument. Post hoc sensitivity analyses imputed nonevaluable TVC as the mean or worst evaluable progressive-disease TVC, but their figures/tables were in the unsupplied supplement.

### Evidence reconstruction and central-result checks

The question is whether targeted FFPE expression can approximate the PD-1-high dysfunctional TIL phenotype and classify durable disease control in nivolumab-treated NSCLC. The 152 candidate patients had stage IV NSCLC receiving second- or later-line nivolumab; known sensitizing EGFR and ALK-positive cases were excluded. Disease control at 12 months includes stable disease and is not ORR.

NKI training flow was 94 candidates → 66 after RNA exclusions → 58 after profiling QC → 41 extreme-phenotype training cases (12 IHC-high/DC12 plus 29 IHC-low/PD); 17 IHC-high/PD tumors were omitted from model training.
