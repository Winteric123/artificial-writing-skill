CCR issue year 2023; DOI `10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-22-2323`. Registered `author_manuscript`, 19 physical pages; SHA-256 `990caba69198a668c127a8e2fc5aebf1391c3385d3779c12b6c8696847ae5160`. Supplement `not_supplied`.

This is a retrospective computational resampling study, not a new phase Ib/II efficacy trial. The parent IMpower150 population was 1,202, while the modeled analysis used patients with longitudinal sum-of-longest-diameter data from ABCP (`n=392`) and BCP (`n=393`). Samples of 15, 30 or 40 patients per arm were drawn with replacement 500 times and administratively truncated at 6, 12 or 24 weeks after the last patient entered. The BCP-versus-BCP null comparison came from the same control pool and is an operating-characteristic construct, not an external failed trial.

The nonlinear mixed-effects model estimated tumor growth rate (`KG`), shrinkage rate (`KS`), time-specific tumor-size ratio and time to regrowth. Geometric mean ratios are not hazard ratios. Patients with baseline-only scans were retained but supply no longitudinal dynamic information; empirical Bayes estimates may shrink toward population values. The TGI–OS model was trained on six historical trials with IMpower150 omitted, then conditioned on resampled baseline covariates and `KG`.
