## Evidence, result, and transfer recheck

| Item | Source-grounded disposition |
|---|---|
| Evidence chain | Autochthonous KRAS/TP53-background lung models, tumor-specific `Lifr` and `Sox17` perturbations, delayed anti-LIF intervention, and T-cell depletion support a model-specific causal chain. Human snRNA, IHC, TCGA expression, and survival analyses are concordant observational layers. |
| Central results | Mouse scRNA used `n=2/condition`; human snRNA included KRAS `n=14` and KRAS/LKB1 `n=4` (Figure 1 pp3-4). The TCGA analysis classified 510 of 515 cases (73 mutant, 437 wild type) (Figure 2 p6). Lif/Lifr perturbation reduced tumor burden without a corresponding autonomous in-vitro proliferation effect; T-cell depletion attenuated benefit (Figures 2-3 pp6-9). Delayed anti-LIF treatment reduced burden and Arg1-positive myeloid states, while total stromal macrophage abundance did not show the same decrease (Figure 6 p13). |
| Unit and source limits | ExCITE/TCR data pooled two mice per condition; cells and tumors are not independent animals. A general Methods statement of at least three mice conflicts with specific `n=2` single-cell captions, so the figure-specific count governs reuse. Trp53-wild-type support and several detailed results appear only in unsupplied supplements as described by the main text. |
| Claim ceiling | The pass supports a preclinical LIF/SOX17 state-maintenance model and rationale for further evaluation. It does not establish human anti-LIF efficacy, anti-PD-1 combination benefit, a treatment-predictive interaction, or that pseudotime proves lineage direction. |
| Safe transfer | Model-specific depletion language is safe when the actual intervention and immune group are named. Bulk RNA may support an association with a myeloid feature, not a claim that LIF causally induced clinical immunotherapy resistance. Synthetic frames are not quotations. |
