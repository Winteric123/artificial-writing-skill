- Positive findings: ZW191 showed mechanism-consistent FRα binding/internalization, in-vitro bystander activity, antitumor activity in selected CDX/PDX models, additive activity with tested partners, and a 60-mg/kg nonclinical NOAEL/HNSTD in cynomolgus monkeys.
- Negative/uncertain findings: one ovarian PDX was inactive; only subsets of NSCLC/endometrial/TNBC models responded; target-expression relationships were inconsistent and exploratory; low group sizes limit between-ADC comparisons; in-vivo synergy was not tested; Figure 7 is ecological; no patient outcomes are presented.
- Safety findings: all reported NHP changes were judged non-adverse and reversible, with no ocular or lung toxicity, but biologic changes occurred. Carboplatin doses were omitted for six mice because of body-weight loss. These data do not support a human “safe” or “better tolerated” claim.
- Source alerts: (1) OV-90 group size is n=7/group in Methods p13 but n=6/group in the Figure 4 legend p33. (6) all authors were current or former sponsor employees and the sponsor held relevant patents/stock interests.
- Limitations: sponsor-controlled preclinical study; small model-specific group sizes; no clinical cohort; research-level IHC; heterogeneous model panels; multiple pairwise tests without a study-wide multiplicity plan; incomplete supplement; single-dose combination comparisons; unresolved OV-90 denominator; ecological cross-ADC analysis.
- Claim ceiling: ZW191 has a differentiated preclinical pharmacology profile, activity in selected FRα-expressing models, and a bounded NHP toxicology profile. The paper does not establish clinical efficacy, clinical safety, superiority or “best-in-class” status, a patient-selection cutoff, broadened eligibility, or causal translation of payload potency into clinical dose.

- Positive findings: GPC2 was prevalent in the sampled neuroblastomas; D3-GPC2 CAR T cells showed target-dependent activation and killing; several neuroblastoma and two SCLC xenograft models showed tumor control; no GPC2-specific pathology was identified in examined mice.
- Negative/uncertain findings: GPC2-low cells were largely resistant; one neuroblastoma PDX showed only modest activity comparable with GD2 CAR; post-treatment expression and genomic correlations were descriptive; normal-tissue coverage was incomplete; mouse “PFS” and multiplicity methods were incompletely reported; most animals developed xGvHD-type lesions.
- Safety findings: nine tested primary-cell types did not trigger measurable activation, and blinded necropsies found no GPC2-specific lesion pattern. However, neural/testicular cells were absent, two control mice died/declined without necropsy, group denominators changed, and NSG/xGvHD models cannot establish human safety.
- Source alerts: (1) “PFS” is repeatedly plotted without a defined progression event or reported survival-comparison method/effect estimate. (2) numerous two-sided t tests and model/timepoint comparisons have no multiplicity correction. (3) the normal-cell panel does not cover neural or testicular tissue, both relevant to the biological expression discussion. (4) two PSMA controls were prematurely lost and not necropsied; toxicology denominators differ at days 16 and 27. (5) xGvHD lesions and late weight loss occur across arms, limiting toxicity attribution. (6) multi-donor replication, normal-tissue expression, and several safety/efficacy panels reside in an unsupplied supplement. (7) SCLC patient expression data come from prior work, not a human SCLC cohort in this paper. (8) the authors acknowledge that immunodeficient NSG models underestimate host immune modulation.
- Limitations: preclinical only; selected high/moderate-GPC2 models; small mouse groups; many in-vitro results from one donor; no comprehensive normal-tissue panel; immunodeficient xenografts with xGvHD; changing toxicology denominators and unexamined early losses; unspecified PFS event/test; no study-wide multiplicity control; supplement not supplied; no patient efficacy/safety data.
- Claim ceiling: D3-GPC2 CAR T cells showed target-dependent preclinical activity in selected neuroblastoma and SCLC systems and no GPC2-specific toxicity signal in the tested normal-cell/mouse assays. The study does not establish human safety, clinical efficacy, a therapeutic index, a GPC2 selection threshold, SCLC-wide applicability, or superiority over another CAR platform.
