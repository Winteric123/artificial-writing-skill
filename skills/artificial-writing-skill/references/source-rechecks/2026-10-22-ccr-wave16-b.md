CCR issue year 2021; DOI `10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-21-0423`; registered `author_manuscript`, 25 physical pages; SHA-256 `7c6dd270d83d7134bf0e9897197bcdbe7175379ac5628493019624e45bf5e60a`. Supplement status: `not_supplied`.

The retrospective cohort contained 200 pulmonary invasive mucinous adenocarcinomas. Standard testing identified a driver in 96%; whole-transcriptome sequencing found an additional NRG2 fusion and raised the reported yield to 97%. KRAS alterations occurred in 151/200, and fusions occurred in 24/49 KRAS-wild-type tumors. The source nevertheless labels the latter as 51%; because 24/49 is 49.0%, the exact count and denominator are retained and the inconsistent percentage is withheld. NRG1-positive tumors were larger, more often metastatic outside the thorax and had worse unadjusted OS/RFS than the selected KRAS comparator, but the NRG1 group was small, baseline stage differed and no adjusted survival model was supplied. Nine potentially targetable cases received heterogeneous agents; isolated responses and nonresponses are case-level evidence, not a subgroup response estimate.

Ceiling: a broad descriptive driver map supporting RNA fusion testing in KRAS-wild-type invasive mucinous adenocarcinoma, with exploratory clinicopathologic and survival differences; no validated prognostic model or treatment-effect estimate.

Under the stated common-variant filters, HapMap WES precision exceeded .98 and recall exceeded .87. Tumor mutation intersample agreement had a median of .81, with 96.1% above .70, but agreement is not accuracy because a complete tumor truth set was absent.

For RNA, medTIN greater than 50 retained 91/150 samples and met the prespecified rho greater than .94 criterion. A threshold greater than 30 retained 134 samples and correlations exceeded .90 but did not meet .94, even though samples continued to cluster by patient. The lower threshold therefore did not pass the primary criterion. The cohort-derived DV200 rule, moderate repertoire/clonality correlations, seven off-target HLA calls and inferred neoantigen analyses remain bounded technical findings, not universal QC or immune-function validation.

Ceiling: a small multisite analytical study supporting specified WES/RNA-seq QC rules under a controlled workflow; no universal cutoff, tumor truth accuracy, clinical utility or predictive validation.
