One Methods line prints nivolumab as 240 mg/kg, conflicting with the fixed 240-mg dose elsewhere; it is quarantined from protocol reuse.

Ceiling: early-phase evidence of limited overall activity plus a hypothesis-generating pharmacodynamic/targeted-expression chain; no validated predictive biomarker, NSCLC efficacy, treatment interaction or external validation.
