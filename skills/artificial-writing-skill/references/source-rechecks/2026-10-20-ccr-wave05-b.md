- Question/design/population: first-in-human, multicenter, open-label phase I trial of single-agent tobemstomig. Dose escalation enrolled 35 patients with heterogeneous advanced/metastatic solid tumors across 50–2100 mg Q2W. Expansion enrolled 41 CPI-experienced melanoma patients, 20 CPI-experienced NSCLC patients, and 8 CPI-naive ESCC patients; all 104 treated patients comprised the relevant safety population.
