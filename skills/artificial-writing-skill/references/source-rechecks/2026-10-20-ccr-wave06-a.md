### Evidence reconstruction and central-result checks

- Question/design/population: multicenter retrospective cohort of 473 patients with stage IV nonsquamous NSCLC harboring exon 19 deletion or L858R, all consecutively treated with first-line osimertinib monotherapy across five institutions from 2014–2025. Baseline tissue NGS defined EGFR amplification as copy number ≥6; liquid-biopsy results were excluded. Eighty-one patients were EGFR-amplified and 392 were not.
- Comparator/analysis sets: amplification-defined groups within one treatment context; no untreated, alternative-TKI, or randomized regimen comparator. The primary endpoint was investigator-assessed PFS from osimertinib initiation; ORR and OS were secondary. Response lacked formal central RECIST assessment. Allele of origin was computationally inferred for 78 amplified cases. The resistance analysis selected 113 patients who first responded or had stable disease ≥6 months, then progressed and underwent tissue NGS before subsequent treatment.
