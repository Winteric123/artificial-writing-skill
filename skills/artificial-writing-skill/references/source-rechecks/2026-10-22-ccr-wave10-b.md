### Evidence and result reconstruction

The question is whether a human anti-TIP1 antibody can be selected, engineered, characterized and radiolabeled for preclinical radiation-guided immuno-PET. This is antibody discovery/manufacturing and animal imaging, without patient PET, diagnostic-accuracy, antitumor-treatment or survival evaluation. PBMCs from 54 healthy donors supplied a nonimmune scFv library of 3 x 10^9 members; eight selected scFvs were reformatted as human IgG1. L111 was selected by recombinant-protein ELISA/SPR and cancer-cell binding. A549/H460 NSCLC models, other tumor cell lines, and one lung-adenocarcinoma PDX were used. Native MS/glycan analysis characterizes the antibody and is not tumor proteomics.

### Evidence and result reconstruction

This is a post hoc correlative study nested within the negative phase III randomized S1400I nivolumab versus nivolumab/ipilimumab trial in chemotherapy-pretreated, ICI-naive advanced squamous NSCLC. Parent trial n=252 (127/125); biospecimen cohort n=160. No result here reverses the parent trial's overall negative treatment comparison. Assays have different sets: mIF reported n=82 but the Methods arm counts 38+42 sum to80; targeted NanoString n=38 (23/15); WES n=50 (28/22); Olink 92 proteins in 561 longitudinal sera from160 patients. Joint Olink/mIF random forest used81. Therefore this is not 160 complete matched multi-omes, whole-transcriptome RNA-seq, single-cell sequencing or unbiased whole-proteome profiling.

Methods include compartment-specific mIF densities, nearest-neighbor distances, spatially varying marked-point probabilities and clusters of at least10 cells within20 micrometres; WES/pBMC germline controls and consensus CNVs supported by at least2 of3 callers; NanoString deconvolution estimates; relative Olink log2 NPX; median splits and survival models, multiplicity correction in specified analyses, mixed/joint longitudinal-survival models and exploratory trees/forests. The text reverses predictor/outcome wording for the mixed model; that exact statistical specification is withheld.

### Evidence and result reconstruction

Question: do historical YAP1-defined SCLC cell models actually represent SCLC? This integrative cell-line reclassification study uses public/institutional genomics, bulk transcriptomics and proteomics; authenticated cultures, xenograft morphology/IHC and sequential blinded review by five thoracic pathologists; and six primary SMARCA4-UT samples. It tests model identity rather than response to treatment, prognosis or immunotherapy benefit. The pathology vignette is standardized experimental context, not clinical history for every donor.

Claim ceiling: orthogonal molecular/pathologic evidence supports reassigning specific historical SCLC-Y cell lines and using selected models as resources for SMARCA4-deficient malignancies. It challenges YAP1 as a lineage-defining SCLC subtype factor without denying genuine SCLC state plasticity. Limits: small historical cell-line set, incomplete original biopsies, xenografts as diagnostic surrogates, dataset/batch effects, heterogeneity among reassigned diagnoses, six patient UT cases, no tested therapy.
