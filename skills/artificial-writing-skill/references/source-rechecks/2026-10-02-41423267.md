## Evidence, result, and transfer recheck

| Item | Source-grounded disposition |
|---|---|
| Design and assay layers | Retrospective DFCI/MSKCC NSCLC cohort (`N=4,157`; immunotherapy 2,768, chemo-immunotherapy 1,389), not a smoking intervention or randomized trial. PD-L1, dNLR, mIF, plasma protein, and bulk-RNA subsets have different denominators. Panel-derived tobacco mutational signature, Olink targeted proteins, mIF spatial protein counts, and bulk-RNA deconvolution are not interchangeable or single-cell assays. |
| Central clinical results | In the immunotherapy cohort, ORR was 8.7%/21.6%/27.5% for never/former/current smoking; adjusted PFS HRs were 0.67 and 0.64 for former/current (Figure 2 pp4-5). In chemo-immunotherapy, ORR differences were nonsignificant by smoking status/intensity, while selected PFS associations remained (Figure 3 pp5-6). First-line PD-L1-high never smokers had higher ORR and PFS with chemo-immunotherapy but no significant OS difference; ever-smokers had no significant PFS or OS difference (Figure 4 pp6-7). Different subgroup P values do not establish treatment interaction. |
| Genotype and multi-omics | With immunotherapy, STK11 was associated with shorter PFS/OS but not ORR; KRAS/STK11 and KRAS/KEAP1 were adverse in chemo-immunotherapy analyses (Results pp5-6). TSMS, TMB, PD-L1, dNLR, mIF, protein, and RNA results remain assay-specific (Figures 5-7 pp7-9). Exact protein identities/effect types affected by source conflicts are withheld from unqualified reuse. |
| Source qualifications | The smoking counts, mIF 691/619, current-smoker ORR 43%/45%, TSMS denominator wording, Figure 6 versus global-null language, protein-list/effect-type labels, and pack-year boundaries contain source inconsistencies.
