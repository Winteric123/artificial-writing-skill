## Evidence, result, and transfer recheck

| Item | Source-grounded disposition |
|---|---|
| Design | Single-arm, open-label, multicenter phase I/II follow-up in pretreated KRAS G12C NSCLC (`N=174`), with different safety, efficacy, CNS, and exploratory biomarker analysis sets. It is not a randomized treatment comparison. |
| Central efficacy/safety | Efficacy-evaluable `n=172`: ORR 41% (95% CI 33.3-48.4), DCR 84% (77.3-88.9), DOR 12.3 months (7.1-15.0), PFS 6.3 months (5.3-8.2). OS used `n=174`: 12.5 months (10.0-17.8); 24-month OS 32.5% (25.0-40.2). Any-grade treatment-related adverse events occurred in 121/174; grade 3/4 in 34/2, with no grade 5 event. The late-onset analysis is limited to 45 patients treated longer than one year (Results p2; Table 1 p3; Figure 1 p4). |
| Exploratory biomarkers | Long benefit/early progression were 40/172 and 62/172. KEAP1: 3/19 versus 16/19, OR 0.22 (0.06-0.87). STK11 mutant versus wild type: 8/25 versus 16/40, OR 0.71 (0.25-2.02) (Figure 2 pp5-6). The STK11 result is imprecise and does not establish equivalence; extreme-outcome selection and missing biomarker data restrict inference. |
| Claim ceiling | A subset had durable benefit, including some STK11-altered tumors; KEAP1 was associated with less long benefit in this exploratory analysis. Without a comparator or interaction test, neither alteration is a validated treatment-predictive biomarker. CNS control (`14/16`) must not be confused with all 40 patients with baseline brain metastases. |
| Safe transfer | A safe frame states the single-arm cohort, analysis-set denominator, endpoint estimate/CI, and exploratory biomarker uncertainty. It must not say that STK11 predicts sensitivity, that nonsignificant groups are equivalent, or that the 45-patient late-safety denominator applies to all 174 patients. |
