### Evidence reconstruction and central-result checks

- Question/design/population: phase II, open-label, nonrandomized, multicenter, sequential multiarm signal-searching study in platinum-refractory/resistant ES-SCLC. Independent arms tested durvalumab plus tremelimumab followed by durvalumab (arm A), adavosertib plus carboplatin (arm B), and ceralasertib plus olaparib (arm C). There was no concurrent control and the arms were not head-to-head comparisons.
- Analysis sets: 49/13/26 patients were enrolled and 41/10/21 received treatment in arms A/B/C. The full analysis set comprised all treated patients for efficacy and safety. Arm A PD-L1 BEP was n=21, baseline-ctDNA BEP n=26, and the prose-described paired baseline/on-treatment ctDNA set n=10. Arm C circulating biomarker BEP was n=21, with 74 samples and 76% reported to have paired baseline/on-treatment samples.
- Endpoints/method chain: investigator-assessed confirmed RECIST 1.1 ORR was primary; DoR, DCR at 12 weeks, TTR, PFS, OS, and safety were secondary. Arm A used SP263 PD-L1 and GuardantOMNI MaxVAF as a ctDNA surrogate; arm C used an Olink 183-analyte targeted plasma panel. All analyses were descriptive; the main PDF reports no study-wide multiplicity correction or independent biomarker validation.

- Claim ceiling: BALTIC showed limited single-arm activity and clinically important toxicity in refractory/resistant ES-SCLC. ### Evidence reconstruction and central-result checks

- Question/design/population: retrospective transcriptomic biomarker development asking whether a 12-gene KEAP1-mutant-like expression phenotype (“KEAPness”) identifies adverse clinical/immune biology beyond KEAP1 mutation status. TCGA generated the signature; SU2C (ICI, RNA n=153) was the identification cohort; OAK/POPLAR atezolizumab (n=439) was clinical validation; OAK/POPLAR docetaxel (n=452) provided a non-ICI context; TRACERx421 (347 patients, 947 regions) supplied early-stage multiregion WES/RNA evolutionary and immune analyses.
- Analysis sets/cutoff: SU2C endpoint sets were PFS n=136, OS n=143, response n=140, PD-L1 n=71, and matched WES n=61. The survival cutoff was outcome-optimized in 93 non-squamous SU2C patients without prior TKI using Youden ROC and 2,000 bootstrap resamples, then carried forward. The exact clinical cutoff is not printed; TCGA z≥.5 is a different scale and must not substitute for it.
- Method chain: bulk RNA signature, PROGENy/DoRothEA pathway/TF inference, BostonGene immune-state inference, dNdScv positive selection, REVOLVER trajectory inference, and DNA-derived T-cell ExTRECT. These are computational estimates from bulk/multiregion data, not single-cell/spatial protein measurements or perturbational proof.

- Positive findings: the expression phenotype replicated adverse survival/response and immune-depletion associations across two ICI cohorts; integrated grouping captured more cases than mutation alone; multiregion and ExTRECT analyses supported convergent biology.
- Negative/uncertain findings: adverse association was also reported under docetaxel; no treatment interaction established prediction; exact clinical cutoff/HRs/CIs were absent from the main PDF; PD-L1/TMB and covariate data were incomplete; 31% showed regional discordance; KEAPness-versus-mutant survival contrasts were nonsignificant, not equivalent.
- Safety findings: secondary biomarker analyses did not report treatment-emergent safety. The study cannot support a safety comparison or a treatment recommendation.
- Source alerts: (1) Figure 4 risk sets total 314, whereas Methods’ matched SU2C WES n=61 plus OAK/POPLAR atezolizumab mutation n=251 total 312; the pooled denominator is unresolved; (2) Figure 3 response panels print values after a chi-square symbol in a form that does not clearly distinguish statistic from P value; do not reuse them as verified P values; (3) adverse outcomes in docetaxel-treated patients and lack of interaction preclude ICI prediction; (4) “outperforms” lacks a formal incremental predictive-performance/utility comparison; (5) the cutoff was outcome-optimized and the exact transported clinical value is absent; (6) the statement that multiregion consistency reflects “time and space” exceeds cross-sectional regional sampling; (7) computational immune/pathway states are not directly measured; (8) CTLA4/chemotherapy intensification is speculative and untested by this signature; (9) supporting supplements S1–S23/tables were not supplied.
- Limitations: retrospective secondary analyses; outcome-informed cutoff; incomplete independent development/validation within SU2C; missing exact cutoff; endpoint-specific denominators; sparse matched WES; pooled denominator conflict; limited PD-L1/TMB and OAK/POPLAR covariates; nominal clinical P values; no formal treatment interaction; docetaxel association; computational immune inference; early-stage TRACERx used to contextualize metastatic outcomes; spatial but not longitudinal sampling; no prospective assay/utility validation.
- Claim ceiling: KEAPness is an expression-defined, KEAP1-like phenotype associated with adverse survival, lower response, and inferred immune depletion across retrospective NSCLC cohorts. It may complement mutation annotation, but the paper establishes neither ICI-specific prediction nor treatment selection, causality, equivalence to KEAP1-mutant disease, universal spatial/temporal stability, or clinical superiority of the classifier.
