## Central checks

1. The trial was randomized 2:1 but explicitly noncomparative. Arm B provides contemporaneous descriptive context; no formal between-arm efficacy test was prespecified or performed.
2. Arm A 18-month OS was 53.8% (95% CI 44.3-62.5). The lower confidence bound exceeded the 40% historical benchmark, while the interval included the 50% target. This is not a simple negative primary endpoint.
3. Arm B 18-month OS was 65.7% (95% CI 52.0-76.3), numerically higher than Arm A. The design does not support a benefit, harm, superiority, noninferiority or equivalence claim between arms.
4. Plasma contributed information in 32/35 patients with noncontributory or unavailable tissue and increased genotyping success from 69% to 97%. This is operational/genomic yield, not clinical efficacy.
5. Early ctDNA clearance was associated with higher ORR and longer PFS/OS, but the analyses were available-case, used a 1-month landmark and univariable models, and had variable T1 timing. Independent prognostic value and treatment prediction were not established.
6. Results/Figure 3 report log-rank P values; Table 3 reports P values from univariable Cox models alongside HRs. These test-specific values must not be mixed.
7. The integrated molecular-volumetric model did not significantly improve PFS or OS discrimination over molecular response alone. Nonsignificance is not equivalence.
8. The 2.7-month molecular lead time was conditional on a selected subgroup in which ctDNA increase already preceded imaging. It does not quantify full-cohort performance or demonstrate benefit from earlier action.
9. Acquired-alteration and bTMB analyses were small and exploratory. No paired leukocyte sequencing or variant-origin prediction was available; KEAP1/STK11 mentions do not prove a resistance mechanism.

## Claim ceiling

Supported: operational feasibility of the networked serial-ctDNA/weekly-MTB workflow; plasma complementation of tissue profiling; exploratory association of early and longitudinal ctDNA kinetics with response and survival; hypothesis-generating molecular-evolution observations.

Not supported: survival benefit or harm from serial monitoring; efficacy of a ctDNA-guided intervention; independent prognosis or treatment prediction; benefit from acting before imaging progression; superiority of the integrated model; a confirmed STK11/KEAP1 resistance mechanism; review of the absent supplements.
