## Evidence, result, and transfer recheck

| Item | Source-grounded disposition |
|---|---|
| Evidence chain | A 1,235-patient retrospective LUAD cohort provides prognosis, while isogenic cell models, bulk clone RNA, a focused 1,463-gene screen, orthogonal genetic/drug evidence, and immune-deficient xenografts support a preclinical SCD1 dependency in tested STK11/KEAP1 joint-loss contexts. These are distinct denominators and evidence levels. |
| Central results | Median OS was 26.41 months for wild type, 18.23 for STK11-only, 13.77 for KEAP1-only, 11.50 for double, and 6.50 for triple alteration (Figure 1 p4). The focused screen produced SCD log-fold changes of -2.6/-1.9 in H358/H292, with about 90% joint-loss dropout versus 30-40% in single-loss backgrounds (Figure 6 pp11-12). Genetic and pharmacologic results converged; genotype-stratified xenografts used five mice/arm and showed a joint-loss growth effect (`P=0.008`) with a wild-type null comparison (Figure 7 pp13-14; embedded S7 p32). |
| Source qualifications | Genotype totals, TMA cores/patients, two-versus-three RNA backgrounds, and a figure panel letter conflict internally. “Single-cell clones” are clone-derived bulk RNA, not single-cell sequencing. Time to a prespecified tumor-size endpoint is not natural survival, and targeted screening is not genome-wide. Strong “synergy” wording lacks a formal combination model. |
| Claim ceiling | The study supports a selective preclinical dependency in tested joint-loss models and a separate poor-prognosis context. It does not identify every STK11-mutant patient as a responder, validate SCD1-treated patient benefit, establish an immune-competent therapeutic effect, or provide a clinical safety window. |
| Safe transfer | Safe prose names the joint genotype, specified models, genetic readout, independent pharmacological endpoint, and the prognostic-only clinical cohort. It must not merge cores with patients, bulk clones with scRNA, time-to-size with mortality, or prognosis with treatment prediction. |
