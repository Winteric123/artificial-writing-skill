### Evidence reconstruction and central-result checks

- Question/design/population: retrospective, single-center primary-lesion FFPE proteomics and prediction-model study. The full expanded cohort comprised 181 patients with differentiated thyroid carcinoma (DTC): 81 with lung metastasis (LMDTC) and 100 without lung metastasis (NMDTC). An initial discovery/validation subset comprised 44 LMDTC and 25 NMDTC samples; PRM used 8 versus 5 samples, and IHC confirmation used 10 paired LMDTC/NMDTC samples.
- Omics/method chain: 4D label-free proteomics identified 5,281 proteins and called differential expression at more than 1.2-fold with a nominal t test P<0.05; 418 proteins met these criteria (186 up, 232 down). Candidate selection combined fold change, enrichment, PPI degree, WGCNA, ROC/TNMplot comparisons, ferroptosis overlap, literature, and “researchers’ attention.” Thirty candidates were reduced to 17 by PRM, then to eight proteins by TNMplot/ROC and IHC: SUCLG1, SUCLG2, DLAT, IDH3B, ACSF2, ACO2, CYCS, and VDAC2. Protein-identification FDR was 1%, but the main text does not state a multiplicity-adjusted threshold for the 418 differential-expression tests.
- Prediction chain: the expanded 181-patient cohort supplied clinical variables and semiquantitative IHC. Univariable P<0.05 variables entered stepwise backward logistic models for clinical, protein, and combined predictors. Validation was internal only, using 2,000 bootstrap resamples; no temporal or external validation cohort was used. The same disease-status labels underlay marker discovery, model fitting, and reported performance.

- Source alerts: (1) Figure 3A’s 92/4/8/77 cells imply accuracy 0.934, not the stated 0.958; 0.958 is the no-metastasis-class precision; (2) sensitivity and specificity are intelligible only after declaring which class is positive; (3) Table 2 OR direction is inverse to several raw group patterns and the modeled event/reference coding is not explicit; (4) several multivariable CIs span from effectively zero to millions, indicating separation/overfit; (5) discovery used nominal t-test P<0.05 without a stated proteome-wide adjustment; (6) candidate selection partly used investigator interest and repeated data-dependent filtering; (7) all model validation was internal; (8) the “0–0.97” decision-curve range is nearly the full threshold range and is not evidence of realized patient benefit; and (9) Figure 4 is a literature-informed schematic, not a tested causal chain.
