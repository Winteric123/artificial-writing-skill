### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. Non-G12C KRAS is a heterogeneous comparator containing multiple alleles with different biology.
2. The retrospective survival analyses address left truncation/immortal-time concerns but cannot remove all selection bias.
3. Chemotherapy was summarized using time to treatment discontinuation, not progression-free survival.
4. Figure 2 wording refers to “duration of response,” while the displayed curves/analysis concern PFS; do not repeat the caption ambiguity.
5. The PD-L1 ≥50% result is P=0.07 and must not be written as a significant G12C-specific resistance signal.
6. STK11 and KEAP1 frequencies are descriptive co-mutation context, not an interaction analysis.
