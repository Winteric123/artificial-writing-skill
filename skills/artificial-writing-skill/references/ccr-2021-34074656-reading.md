## Question, design and source role

This retrospective observational study asks whether operationally defined CDKN2A loss-of-function (LOF) alterations correlate with ICI outcomes, whether findings reproduce across institutions/histologies, and whether urothelial carcinoma (UC) shows corresponding immune-expression differences. It contains separate clinical DNA and public DNA/RNA/IHC analyses, not RNA assays on every DFCI/MSKCC patient or functional CDKN2A perturbation.

Safe tags: retrospective clinical cohort; cross-tumor genomic association; histology-stratified ICI outcomes; targeted DNA panel; copy-number loss; bulk RNA reanalysis; gene-set enrichment; computational immune deconvolution; IHC immune context. Source roles: pan-tumor clinical-correlative with an explicit NSCLC subgroup; UC-focused immune-context analysis; biomarker-methods/language reference. This is molecular multiomics through DNA plus RNA, not whole-proteome research. No direct STK11/LKB1 analysis was identified; it is not an STK11 mechanism or efficacy source.

The operational LOF definition (p3) includes nonsense SNVs, out-of-frame and in-frame indels, consensus splice variants and homozygous deletion. Missense/synonymous variants are excluded. Heterozygous loss is excluded where ascertainable, but cannot be excluded from the MSKCC reference group. Do not equate single-copy loss with LOF or panel-WT with intact expression: promoter methylation was not measured. DFCI lacks matched normal, unlike MSK-IMPACT.

OS in clinical ICI cohorts starts at ICI initiation. DFCI TTF means next treatment or death, not RECIST PFS. Response is investigator-assessed CR/PR. TCGA OS/PFS start at diagnosis. Cox adjustment includes treatment type/prior lines in DFCI and treatment type in MSKCC; TMB is additionally included for significant cohorts. Clinical testing is exploratory without multiplicity correction. GSEA FDR<.1 is a separate rule.

## Source conflicts and claim ceiling

1. Table1 p4 gives MSKCC single-agent1032/1250=82.6%; prose gives963=77%, matching the anti-PD1/L1-only class row rather than all single-agent regimens. Keep type/class separate.
2. Fig2A/C p5 risk-row colors reverse the main curve legend. Do not assign UC genotype counts from those colors alone. Fig2E P=.01 differs from prose .017.
3. TCGA stageIV Methods135, Results/OS131 and PFS132 must not be collapsed into one denominator.
4. Fig4C p8 red top is labelledIC0 with counts12+84=96, but prose assigns96 toIC2+; pale bottom labelledIC2+ totals48. This also conflicts with the described lower IC expression in altered tumors. Do not silently swap labels; exact rates remain withheld.
5. P2 literally prints atezolizumab1,200mg at “3 times weekly.” This apparent schedule anomaly is not copied into reusable Methods or treatment advice and was not externally corrected here.
6. Response OR direction and platinum HR/CI/P-value problems are explicitly retained above; no inferred correction.
7. The strongest defensible finding is an exploratory, cross-cohort UC association under ICI with supportive but partially unreplicated immune correlates. Separate nonrandomized negative comparator cohorts do not prove a predictive-not-prognostic biomarker; no formal biomarker-by-treatment interaction is reported.
8. Important limitations: retrospective selection; 9% post-ICI biopsies; uneven clinical covariates/eligibility across centers; few altered cases in some subgroups; no matched normal in DFCI; unmeasured methylation; neighboring-gene co-deletion; no clinical multiplicity adjustment. Immune-expression associations do not prove a CDKN2A-causal mechanism.
9. No new safety endpoint or treatment-selection validation was established. Do not use these findings to recommend withholding ICI.
