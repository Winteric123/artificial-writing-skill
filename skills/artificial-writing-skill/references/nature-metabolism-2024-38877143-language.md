## Synthetic sentence frames

1. **Flux versus abundance:** “Although **[metabolite]** pool size and uptake were unchanged, isotope enrichment in **[product]** increased, supporting greater pathway use rather than simple metabolite accumulation.”
2. **Regulatory mechanism:** “LKB1 suppressed **[target]** through **[SIK effector]** rather than the canonical **[AMPK–mTOR branch]**, as shown by genetic and pharmacologic perturbation.”
3. **Promoter mechanism:** “Chromatin accessibility, motif analysis, ChIP, and reporter assays converged on direct **[transcription-factor complex]** regulation of **[gene]**.”
4. **Dependency:** “Suppression of either **[isoform 1]** or **[isoform 2]** impaired KL/KLK cells, whereas LKB1 or KEAP1 restoration reduced the phenotype.”
5. **Rescue logic:** “Antioxidants rescued the viability defect, whereas nucleosides and methyl donors did not, directing the interpretation toward redox maintenance rather than nucleotide synthesis or methylation.”
6. **Context boundary:** “The rescue pattern differed in pancreatic and lung models, indicating that the metabolic consequence of the same driver genotype is cell-of-origin dependent.”
7. **Combination caveat:** “The combination produced greater tumor control, but higher systemic **[drug]** exposure during coadministration indicates that pharmacokinetic interaction contributed to the effect.”
8. **Claim calibration:** “The data establish a preclinical metabolic dependency; clinical efficacy and biomarker utility remain untested.”

## Paragraph architecture

### Metabolic-flux paragraph

Begin with pathway enrichment, separate pool size/uptake from isotope labeling, use wild-type versus kinase-dead LKB1 for directionality, and finish with SHMT-specific conversion. This avoids calling abundance changes “flux.”

### Regulatory-mechanism paragraph

Contrast AMPK–mTOR with SIK1, then layer chromatin accessibility, promoter occupancy, reporter activity, and isotope output. Retain the partial mRNA–protein disconnect as evidence that the model is not complete.

### Redox-dependency paragraph

Move from selective viability to antioxidant rescue, report the failed nucleotide/methylation rescue, then use NADPH/GSH/ROS and isotope evidence to identify mitochondrial NADPH as the supported output. End with the pancreatic context contrast.

### Combination-treatment paragraph

Report single agents, combination, redox reversal, model genotype, and orthotopic/syngeneic extension; then explicitly discuss the paclitaxel–SHIN2 exposure interaction before any synergy claim.

## Unsafe transfers

- Do not equate metabolite abundance with pathway flux.
- Do not say SHMT dependence is driven by nucleotide synthesis in KL NSCLC; the rescue data argue against that interpretation.
- Do not generalize the NSCLC redox mechanism to KL pancreatic tumors.
- Do not call all LKB1-mutant tumors SHMT dependent without considering KEAP1/NRF2 functional status.
- Do not describe the paclitaxel combination as purely pharmacodynamic synergy; SHIN2 exposure increased.
- Do not claim patient benefit, clinical safety, or a validated biomarker from these preclinical studies.
