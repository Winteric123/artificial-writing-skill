### Results paragraph
- `We first summarized the available molecular and clinical data, keeping cohort-specific assay coverage and outcome missingness separate. Unsupervised molecular groups differed in inferred immune composition and epigenetic age. Selected recurrence comparisons reached nominal significance, whereas the main overall-survival contrasts did not. These findings support exploratory stratification, without establishing independent prognosis or differential treatment benefit.` 结构：分析集→分组→阳性/阴性→界限；pp3–10。

### Discussion paragraph
- `The study illustrates how epigenetic profiles may add context beyond mutation status. However, derived immune and age features share the same molecular input, outcome availability varies across cohorts, and subgroup comparisons lack external validation. Pathway methylation therefore generates mechanistic hypotheses rather than confirming pathway suppression, and a clinical treatment-selection role requires additional evidence.` 结构：增量价值→数据依赖/缺失→机制与临床边界；pp2–4,9–10。
