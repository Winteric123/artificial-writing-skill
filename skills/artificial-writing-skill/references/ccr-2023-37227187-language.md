Source alerts: Methods p3 gives SG10mg/m², while Results p6 and Figure1 use10mg/kg; retain the latter as the concordant reported dose and disclose the Methods unit error if discussing exact dosing. Results p7 calls Patient5 pancreatic adenocarcinoma; Table3 and Figure2 label Patient5 high-grade neuroendocrine carcinoma. Withhold a pancreatic-specific interpretation of that patient's24-week disease control and RNA phenotype. The manuscript calls its analysis GSEA while mentioning GSVA for enrichment-score computation; do not infer a fully specified algorithm from the names alone. Figure3A's plotted NES direction/color orientation is unusual (positive numerical values appear left of zero while the heat-map key differs); exact pathway directions should follow the checked narrative with this plotting caveat, not an unexamined sign transcription. Figure1 safety bars omit lymphopenia; Table2 is needed for complete grade4 reporting.

Claim ceiling: Feasible dose escalation with prophylactic support and preliminary activity in a small heterogeneous cohort. Historical comparisons do not establish safer treatment than chemotherapy. Combination responses do not demonstrate synergy or distinguish ATR-inhibitor contribution from ADC monotherapy. Trop-2-high nonresponders and Trop-2-low transformed-SCLC response prevent a validated predictive-expression claim. Preserve neuroendocrine transformation versus de novo disease and association versus causal mechanism.
