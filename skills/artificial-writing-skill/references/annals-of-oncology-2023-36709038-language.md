### Evidence and results

Prespecified exploratory retrospective biomarker analysis nested in an open-label randomized phaseIII comparison. Previously untreated advanced/metastatic PD-L1 TPS>=1% NSCLC without sensitizing EGFR/ALK; squamous and nonsquamous included. Overall trial1274, tissue-TMB evaluable793 (pembrolizumab414/chemotherapy379), matched tumor-normal mutation subset429, nonsquamous KRAS subset301. Data cutoff2018-09-04. Missing biomarker samples cannot be treated as wild type. Trial regimens, BICR/RECIST1.1, and tissue WES are documented in main Methods; not blood-TMB testing or original transcriptomics. Prior GEP used to derive a cutoff elsewhere does not make this a transcriptomic study.

TMB cutoff175mut/exome was prespecified before clinical/biomarker merge and approximates, but is not universally equivalent to, a specific assay's10mut/Mb threshold. TMB treated as continuous log10-transformed variable; one-sided Wald tests for pembrolizumab under an a-priori directional hypothesis, two-sided for chemotherapy. Nominal P values; do not omit sidedness or claim multiple-testing-adjusted significance. Figure1 caption calls OS/PFS/ORR analyses logistic regression; this does not authorize inferring a time-to-event model for missing supplement details. Main text alone is insufficient to reconstruct the entire SAP.

Claim ceiling: exploratory evidence of relative treatment effects within selected randomized-trial biomarker subsets, not proof STK11 has no prognostic effect, no biologic resistance, or a validated treatment interaction. STK11 and KEAP1 mutation do not establish universal immunotherapy exclusion. Preserve this counterpoint alongside observational resistance studies; different populations, PD-L1 eligibility, comparators and endpoints matter. No original functional experiments, single-cell, spatial, protein-wide or transcriptomic assay is demonstrated here. Safety was not a new biomarker-stratified endpoint in this report; do not invent one.
