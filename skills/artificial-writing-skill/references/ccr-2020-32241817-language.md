### Results paragraph
- `We first examined the alteration class within each clinical cohort and then summarized endpoint-specific estimates. The pooled association was favorable, although individual cohorts did not uniformly reach statistical significance. In the randomized-trial dataset, the marker-by-treatment interaction differed between endpoints. Separate bulk-RNA analyses identified selected immune and repair-associated signatures; these were treated as correlates rather than functional measurements.` 结构：分队列→合并→交互阴阳性→分离机制线索；pp3–11。

### Discussion paragraph
- `The findings suggest that molecular subclassification may refine a candidate biomarker beyond a gene-level mutation label. However, computational impact scores do not resolve functional direction, and heterogeneous assays, cohort overlap and retrospective selection limit generalization. Endpoint-specific interaction results should therefore be retained, while proposed combination strategies remain hypotheses for subsequent testing.` 结构：增量价值→分类/设计限制→终点边界→未来验证；pp9–12。
