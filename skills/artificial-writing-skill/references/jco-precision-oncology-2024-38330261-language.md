# PMID 38330261 — language-retention note

## Section-aware vocabulary and collocations

- **heterogeneity within STK11-mutant disease** — Introduction/Discussion framing for a genotype that contains biologically distinct subgroups.
- **immune-active transcriptional state** — Results wording for coordinated RNA signals; do not substitute “immune-infiltrated” without cellular validation.
- **inferred immune-cell abundance** — Methods/Results wording that preserves the computational nature of MCP-counter estimates.
- **directionally favorable but statistically inconclusive** — Results/Discussion wording for HRs whose confidence intervals cross the null.
- **post hoc treated subset** — Methods/Limitations label for the OAK/POPLAR and Rizvi analyses.
- **prognostic rather than predictive evidence** — Discussion claim boundary when no randomized genotype-by-treatment interaction was tested.
- **cross-dataset triangulation** — Synthesis term for evidence whose molecular and clinical links come from separate cohorts.
- **internal numeric discrepancy** — Audit language for the 14.5-month text versus 14.9-month figure value; preserve both with their locations.

## Synthetic sentence frames

1. “Within **[genotype-defined population]**, **[comutation]** was associated with a more **[immune/metabolic]** transcriptional state, although the cell-type estimates were computationally inferred.”
2. “The effect estimate favored **[group]**, but the confidence interval crossed the null, making the result hypothesis-generating rather than confirmatory.”
3. “A response-rate difference was observed, whereas adjusted survival endpoints were not significantly different.”
4. “Because molecular and clinical associations were evaluated in separate retrospective cohorts, the data do not establish a single causal or predictive chain.”
5. “The negative **[biomarker]** comparison argues against **[simple explanation]** as the sole basis for the observed immune phenotype.”

## Paragraph logic worth retaining

For a biomarker Results paragraph: define the nested genotype → report molecular state with assay type → give the main clinical estimate and CI → state the negative survival/pooled result → close with retrospective boundary. For Discussion: establish heterogeneity → triangulate independent cohorts → name discordant endpoints → distinguish prognostic from predictive → call for prospective validation.

## Safe transfer

- Safe for retrospective multi-cohort biomarker studies, especially when RNA deconvolution and small clinical subsets must be kept separate.
- Preserve denominators, endpoint direction, CI, adjustment status, and whether a quantity is measured or inferred.
- Use “associated with” for human molecular comparisons and “may identify” for unvalidated stratifiers.

## Unsafe transfer

- Do not call TP53 comutation an ICI-selection biomarker or evidence of treatment interaction.
- Do not translate MCP-counter estimates into histologically proven infiltration.
- Do not pool response, PFS, OS, and time-to-treatment-failure as if they were one endpoint.
- Do not resolve the 14.5/14.9-month discrepancy without checking the source; cite it explicitly when exact numbers matter.

## Indexed language units

- `Within [genotype-defined population], [comutation] was associated with a more [immune/metabolic] transcriptional state, although the cell-type estimates were computationally inferred.`
- `The effect estimate favored [group], but the confidence interval crossed the null, making the result hypothesis-generating rather than confirmatory.`
- `define the nested genotype → report the assay-qualified molecular state → present the clinical estimate and interval → state the negative endpoint → close with the predictive claim boundary`
