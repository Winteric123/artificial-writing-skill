Human protein correlation r=.23/P=.026 is modest, not quantitatively strong. Nuclear fractionation does not directly resolve nucleolar versus nucleoplasmic localization; nucleolar specificity partly relies on prior biology/domain annotation. Greater co-IP signal with higher USP36 expression does not alone prove increased intrinsic binding affinity or catalytic activity.
