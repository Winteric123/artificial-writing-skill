## Separate experimental and clinical populations
The isogenic system is mouse embryonic fibroblasts lacking Hras/Nras and conditionally endogenous Kras, expressing introduced human HA-tagged mutant KRAS after4OHT. It is neither a patient-derived lung-cancer panel nor a genetically engineered autochthonous lung tumor. Three-dimensional Matrigel cultures are cell-line-derived, not patient-derived organoids. RAS-GTP pull-down, Western blot, IncuCyte confluence, MTS/CellTiter-Glo, dose-response fitting, SHP2/MEK Loewe interaction and immunodeficient-mouse implants test intrinsic allele properties. Three fields/well and technical wells are not independent biological replicates. Bilateral flank tumors may be clustered within mice; raw tumor observations should not automatically be treated as independent animals.

## Figure-by-figure findings and limits
Figure1p18: endogenousKras removal increases introduced-mutant expression. Basal GTP loading especially high for G12D/V; under low serum G12D has greatest growth fitness, while full-serum mutant-to-mutant growth differences are not significant. Q61H grows less in3D. One-month tail-vein experiment reports G12D lung colonization3/3, with other alleles negative at that time; it does not prove lifelong inability to colonize. Figure1E table prints0/0for other groups despite legend n=3perisoform; flag invalid denominators rather than silently converting to0/3. Scale bar '100μM' is a dimensional typo for a length context. Tail-vein colonization bypasses primary invasion and is not a complete metastasis assay.

Figure2p19: selumetinib IC50 approximately .269μM G12C,.473Q61H,.777G13D,1.454G12A,3.167G12D,4.844G12V,7.405WT. This rank is drug/assay-specific: trametinib2D effects were more similar. PanelB explicitly uses logGR50, not interchangeable with IC50. In-vivo selumetinib50mg/kg daily for one week yields regressionG12C/Q61H, stableG12D/V/G13D and no responseG12A. These are experimental volume classifications, not clinical RECIST outcomes.

Figure3p20: sotorasib specificity forG12C and enhanced growth suppression with sotorasib+selumetinib over single agents in short-term mouse implants. 'Complete growth abrogation' is not eradication: residual tumors remain in photographs and follow-up is short. The formally described Loewe analysis concerns SHP099+selumetinib supplementary experiments; do not transfer a synergy score to the main sotorasib combination. No patient combination efficacy is tested.

Figure4p21: mutant versusWT OS is not significantly different overall(HR1.07,P=.21) or stageI–III(HR1.08,P=.39). StageIV OS16.2vs25.5months,HR1.37(95%CI1.2–1.57),P<.0001. The authors explicitly note treatment-access confounding by effective EGFR/ALK therapies inWT tumors. No demonstrated survival difference among KRAS alleles in any stage grouping (overallP=.2,earlyP=.4,advancedP=.6). Do not claim allele-specific survival based on cell fitness. No treatment interaction proves a predictive biomarker.

Figure5p22: co-mutation comparisons use KRASWT reference, which differs from comparing one allele against other KRAS mutants. STK11/ATM enrichment forG12C/A/V, not detected forG12D; KEAP1 enrichment forG12C/G13X; G13X has highest reported concurrentSTK11/KEAP1 frequency. These are observational enrichments, not genetic dependency or evidence that each alteration occurs in the same cell. Figure5B uses log2odds ratio: raw odds ratios cannot be negative despite the methods' wording. Plot q-value cutoff .25 differs from differential-expression FDR<.1; do not conflate with universalP<.05. TCGA pathway analysis identifies G12D lower chromosome-maintenance/DNA-repair programs andG13X lower immune-related programs. Expression patterns may reflect co-mutations and cell composition, not allele-intrinsic causal effects. Caption 'G61X' is a typo in the Q61 context. Methods describe DESeq2/RSEMv2, median-based fold change and Reactome; exact count input/scaling should be checked before reproducing, not assumed. Figure terms 'gene ontology' do not establish a different database than the methods' Reactome.
