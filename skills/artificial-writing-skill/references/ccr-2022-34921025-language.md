### Results paragraph
- `We first evaluated the paired clinical specimens, retaining separate denominators for the molecular assays. Target expression increased on average after resistance, but individual responses varied. Pathway-signature repression was supported in the augmentation group, while several opposite-direction comparisons did not meet the adjusted significance threshold. Model perturbations and combination assays then supplied preclinical support for the proposed strategy, without establishing a clinical treatment effect.` 结构：队列→异质性→阳性/阴性→模型与临床界限；pp4–11。

### Discussion paragraph
- `The combined evidence suggests that treatment-associated target regulation may be relevant to the design of antibody–drug conjugate combinations. Nevertheless, retrospective treatment continuation, incomplete assay overlap and limited model coverage constrain causal and predictive interpretation. Direct clinical evaluation is required to determine whether the biological rationale translates into benefit with acceptable toxicity; unresolved numerical and procedural discrepancies should remain explicit in any secondary synthesis.` 结构：转化意义→混杂/覆盖限制→临床验证→源透明；pp3–4,9–12。
