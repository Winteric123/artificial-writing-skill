## Three distinct evidence streams
- Retrospective MGH cohort: 374 KRAS-mutant NSCLC, 149 G12C and 225 other KRAS. BM at any time 150/374 (60/149 versus 90/225), P=0.97. No KRAS-wild-type comparator. The phrase “particularly G12C” does not imply greater G12C risk.
- Competing-risk analysis is restricted to 287 with metastatic disease, G12C 117/non-G12C 170; death without prior BM competes with BM. Synchronous BM 116/150 and metachronous 34/150. Figure 1B caption n=34 describes metachronous events, while plotted cohort labels retain 117/170; do not use 34 as the survival risk-set denominator. Gray P=0.8628/0.2119 does not establish genotype differences. Absence of systematic surveillance can miss asymptomatic later BM.
- Preclinical: intracranial human LU99, LU65 and H23 xenografts in immunodeficient mice, not blood-borne spontaneous metastasis or intact antitumor immunity. LU99 efficacy n=5/group, LU65/H23 n=10/group, PK commonly n=3. Direct brain implantation bypasses metastatic dissemination.
- Clinical: only two CSF-available KRYSTAL-1 phase Ib patients with active untreated BM, not the planned 25-patient cohort or a response-rate estimate.

## Pharmacology and results
- Cellular pERK IC50 5 nmol/L; human binding 99%, mouse 99.5%. Table 1 uses total plasma 8,600 nmol/L versus free plasma 43 nmol/L after mouse 200 mg/kg; text 8.6 μmol/L is equivalent, not 8.6 mmol/L from extraction. CSF 52 nmol/L. Do not compare total plasma concentrations directly with unbound CSF ratios.
- At mouse 100 mg/kg, CSF 19.1/7.4 nmol/L at 1/8 hours and Kp,uu about 0.4/0.2; at 200 mg/kg about 1. Exposure is time/dose dependent, not a fixed cross-species penetration percentage. Efflux ratio 13 and P-gp inhibition IC50 980 nmol/L support, but do not genetically prove, self-inhibition of efflux.
- LU99: only 100 mg/kg BID survival comparison significant after FDR; 2/5 without measurable tumor. BLI comparison was significant on day34, not all timepoints. LU65 survival 17 versus37 days, P<0.0001; H23 survival did not reach significance despite growth/pharmacodynamic effects.
- pERK reduction and Ki-67 staining support pathway and proliferation effects, not genomics or single-cell/spatial transcriptomics.
- Clinical case1 CSF34.6 nmol/L, systemic PR (-31%), disappearance of three BM; case2 CSF24.2 nmol/L, systemic SD (-16.7%), shrinkage of three BM. Mean Kp,uu0.47 in two selected cases. Neither a 100% systemic ORR nor a standardized confirmed intracranial ORR is established. Case2 grade3 rash must not be lost in “mostly grade1–2” safety wording.
- Methods specify rank-sum/log-rank with FDR for LU99; Figure3 shows Mann–Whitney/log-rank for additional models. Preserve test/model mapping rather than claiming all analyses multiplicity corrected.
