## Scientific reading record

- Separates SEZ6 from SEZ6L/SEZ6L2; related genes are not interchangeable. Normal neuronal expression and neuroendocrine tumor expression motivate targeting but also toxicity vigilance.
- ASCL1-associated induction does not yet establish direct promoter binding. Lineage-marker status is distinct from an oncogenic driver. Reported missense variants (~2% SCLC in cited public data) are not proven activating mutations.
- Prognostic associations conflict across cited cohorts; tumor expression does not automatically become a validated prognostic or predictive biomarker. IHC positivity thresholds vary across studies and antibody clones.
- Fig1 structure/normal tissue RNA;Fig2 development timeline;Fig3 illustrativeIHC and small histology groups (e.g.transformedSCLC n2);Fig4 contrasts ABBV011 calicheamicin/noncleavable linker/DAR2 withABBV706 topoI/cleavable linker/DAR6;Fig5 proposed modalities rather than tested clinical results.
