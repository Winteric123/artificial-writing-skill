## Language and transfer ceiling

Use baseline medication exposure, indication-related confounding, comorbidity burden, fixed covariate adjustment, center-level frailty, standardized mean difference, matched analysis, exploratory targeted transcriptomics, and residual confounding. Metformin association does not show drug harm or justify stopping diabetes therapy. Greater significance in one medication group does not establish a between-drug interaction. Use observational result→adjustment→residual uncertainty paragraphs, preserving heterogeneous tumor types and separate clinical/transcriptomic denominators.
