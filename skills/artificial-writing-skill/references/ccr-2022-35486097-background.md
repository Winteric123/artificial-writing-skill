## Mechanistic scope
The organizing argument links BRAF mutation class, RAS dependence, RAF monomer/dimer state, adaptive feedback and histology to inhibitor properties. First-generation CODI inhibitors select monomers and can paradoxically activate wild-type MAPK signaling through RAF dimers/negative allostery. PB, equipotent and dimer-selective inhibitors differ; absence of structural data is explicitly flagged in Table1 and inferred biochemical categories are not crystallographic proof. ARAF escape with particular agents is not a proven universal class effect. PROTAC evidence is preclinical. Fig2 is a conceptual combination map, not a trial establishing that every pictured multidrug regimen is effective or tolerable. Text p7 refers to SOS2 whereas Fig2 depicts SOS1; retain source discrepancy instead of silently treating those proteins as identical.

## Toxicity and transfer limits
Therapeutic index constrains pathway inhibition. On-target paradoxical cutaneous toxicities may decrease with added MEK inhibition, whereas nonspecific nausea/fatigue need not; combination is not toxicity-free. Early next-generation agent safety cohorts are small and heterogeneous. Table2 regulatory indications/years and trial statuses are publication-era history, not a current2026 treatment recommendation. Future directions are hypotheses, not completed outcomes.
