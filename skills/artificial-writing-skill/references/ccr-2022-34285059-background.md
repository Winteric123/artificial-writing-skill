# PMID 34285059 — background review read

Read all eight physical pages of the publisher PDF and inspected Figures 1–6 on pp3–6. Formal issue year 2022 despite 2021 online date. This is explicitly a narrative REVIEW, not a new cohort, experiment or systematic meta-analysis. Keep separately in background register; do not count its reproduced studies as newly read primary sources or feed its numerical claims into the original-study Results corpus. No independent acceptance review was performed.

The review connects hepatic antigen tolerance, liver-metastasis clinical associations, multi-site syngeneic models and proposed interventions. Pages1–2 distinguish Kupffer cells, LSECs, hepatocytes, costimulation, T-cell anergy and Tregs. Pages2–3 synthesize melanoma/NSCLC/urothelial ICI observations, whose confounding and original-study denominators cannot be re-evaluated from this review alone. Liver-metastasis-associated adverse prognosis is not by itself a predictive treatment interaction.

Pages3–5 describe two-site MC38/Renca experiments, suppressive CD11b-positive myeloid cells, FasL-mediated deletion of antigen-specific CD8 cells, and Treg perturbation. Figure3 reproduces CyTOF/viSNE data and Figure6 reproduces single-cell RNA-seq myeloid subclusters and MDSC scores: these are secondary illustrations, not newly generated omics. Figures2 and5 are conceptual schematics, not quantitative validation. Figure1 caption calls the curve OS, while its panel is explicitly labeled PFS with20.1/5.1-month medians; preserve the mismatch and verify the primary source before reuse. Do not attribute the melanoma Figure1 curves to NSCLC merely because adjacent prose discusses NSCLC.

Pages5–6 address liver-directed surgery, radiation, ablation, TACE/TARE and systemic combinations. Murine8Gy exposure is not equivalent to a validated clinical SBRT regimen; out-of-field responses are uncommon, and treatment sequence matters. Proposed reversal of hepatic immune suppression does not establish a clinical survival benefit from liver-directed therapy. The review itself calls optimal combination, sequencing and safety unresolved. Historical statements about ongoing trials remain historical. No STK11-specific result was identified. Terminology suitable for background understanding includes tolerogenic antigen presentation, concomitant tumor immunity, antigen-specific clonal deletion, systemic immune suppression and abscopal response, but this background-only record contributes no original-study sentence-bank entries.
