# Neoadjuvant immunotherapy review background reading

## PMID 37955563

Vaidya and Cohen, Facts and Hopes in Neoadjuvant Immunotherapy: Current Approvals and Emerging Evidence. CCR 2024;30:1232–1239; DOI 10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-23-0583. Supplied author manuscript with repository cover, 17 physical pages; PMC availability October 2024 is not the publication date. No main figures, original Methods/Results, or separately supplied supplements. Background main reading complete; no independent acceptance recheck.

Synthesis: intact tumor and draining lymph nodes provide a biological rationale for preoperative immune priming. Evidence spans TNBC, NSCLC, HNSCC, colorectal/rectal cancer, cutaneous SCC, melanoma and bladder cancer. Distinguish neoadjuvant-only from perioperative regimens, pCR from MPR and clinical complete response, radiographic from pathological response, and surgery feasibility from antitumor efficacy. Trials differ in phase, comparator, chemotherapy backbone, dose, timing, denominator and endpoint; cross-trial numerical rankings are not comparative evidence. S1801 directly compares treatment timing; many other trials cannot isolate the contribution of the postoperative component. Pathologic response is promising but not a universally validated OS surrogate. Biomarker and treatment-de-escalation proposals require prospective testing. Historical approvals, ongoing trials and counts are publication-era descriptions, not verified 2026 guidance.

STK11 relevance: Table 1 attributes reduced PFS with STK11/KEAP1/RB1/EGFR alterations to the cited NADIM trial; this is secondary reporting, not a new genotype analysis, and requires reading that primary report before using a quantitative STK11 claim.

Source alerts: prose calls NADIM phase 3 while Table 1 identifies phase 2 (46 patients); KEYNOTE-671 prose gives 379 while Table 1 gives 797 randomized. IMpassion031 prose 58% versus 48% conflicts with its 17-percentage-point table difference. Schoenfeld combination volumetric response 80% in prose versus 53% in Table 1. Table labels the nonoperative rectal study's endpoint pCR despite absence of resection; retain clinical response terminology until primary verification. NICHE prose CD8+PD-1+ versus table CD8+PD-L1+ is not interchangeable. Gross table sample size is printed 79%; it means neither a usable count nor a response rate without checking. Other CI/transcription anomalies include a 9% CI and an OR outside its printed CI. These exact numbers and phrases are quarantined from reusable factual templates.

Background vocabulary: surgery-precluding progression; pathological response assessment; feasibility of timely resection; response-adapted de-escalation; surrogate endpoint validation; intact tumor–draining lymph node axis; durable immune memory; biomarker-enriched patient selection. Architecture: biological timing rationale → tumor-specific trial landscape → efficacy/surgical feasibility/toxicity trade-offs → endpoint and biomarker limitations → prospective validation. No original-study Methods/Results expressions are learned from this review.
