### Evidence reconstruction and numerical checks

| Evidence unit | Source-grounded reconstruction | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical cohort | 1,181 patients with resected pathologic stage IB–IIIA NSCLC and common EGFR mutations were analyzed. | This is a single-center, retrospective, predominantly Asian surgical cohort. |

### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. No patient received adjuvant EGFR TKI in the analyzed surgical cohort. The study cannot determine who should receive or omit current adjuvant targeted therapy.
2. The molecular risk model is based on 56 matched cases and controls, not all 1,181 patients.
3. The APOBEC–PFS analysis has only 27 patients, with eight in the high-score group; it is hypothesis generating.
4. Some tabulated confidence intervals and formatting are internally awkward. Preserve the reported source values and do not “correct” them without supplemental or author clarification.
5. RNA subtype, TP53 status, stage, and histologic features are related but not interchangeable risk labels.
