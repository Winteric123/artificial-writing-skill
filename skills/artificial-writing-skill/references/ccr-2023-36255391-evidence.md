## Identity and scope
A Phase II Clinical Trial of Nivolumab and Temozolomide for Neuroendocrine Neoplasms. CCR 2023; DOI 10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-22-1552. Original single-center, nonrandomized phase II NEN cohort; the separately enrolled SCLC arm is not this report. Of 28 participants, 20 had NET and eight NEC; 11 lung primaries were seven atypical carcinoids, two typical carcinoids, one NEC and one unknown. This is not NSCLC or SCLC validation. No new STK11 priority designation.

## Design and numerical checks
Simon minimax two-stage design: 15 then up to 28 patients, 80% power, one-sided alpha .05, null ORR 15% versus target 35%. RECIST 1.1 imaging every eight weeks initially; CTCAE v5. Nivolumab 480 mg every four weeks, TMZ days 1–5 every 28 days. First 13 received TMZ 200 mg/m2; amendment lowered it to 150 mg/m2 after four severe hematologic toxicities. Do not describe all exposure as 150 mg/m2.

Primary confirmed ORR 9/28, 32.1% (95% CI 15.9–52.4); confirmed plus unconfirmed ORR 10/28, 35.7% (18.6–55.9), including ten PRs, 16 SD and two PD. DCR 93%. Correlative/subgroup response definitions include unconfirmed responses. Lung 7/11 versus nonlung 3/17, exploratory P=.020; pancreas 2/3. Median PFS 8.8 months (3.9–11.1), OS 32.3 (20.7–NR). Lung PFS 11.1 versus other 7.2 months, P=.210; OS P=.602. Source minor table/prose discordances: other-primary PFS upper CI 10.0 vs10.7 and pancreatic comparison P=.752 vs.480. Neither establishes a subgroup benefit.

Grade 3/4 neutropenia and thrombocytopenia 14% each; seven related serious adverse events; no treatment-related deaths. Dose amendment was not a randomized safety comparison.

## Immune assays and inference
PBMC mass cytometry: 37 markers, 35 cell populations, Helios acquisition, t-SNE/viSNE and gated immunophenotyping. This is not scRNA-seq or global proteomics. Nine paired baseline/C1D15 specimens underlie longitudinal analyses, not all 28 patients. CD4 frequency decreased (P=.001) and CD8 increased (P=.03). Baseline LAG3-positive T-cell frequency compared five responders with nine nonresponders (P=.028); Figure 3E caption misleadingly resembles a paired timepoint description. Subsequent sample sizes differ. Correlative P values explicitly lack multiplicity adjustment. LAG3 subgroup P=.08 and absent PFS/OS correlations prevent predictive claims.

MDSCs: lineage-negative CD11b+CD33+ HLA-DR-low/negative; CD14 monocytic and CD66b granulocytic subsets. Relative abundance changes do not establish suppressive function. CFSE proliferation after anti-CD3/CD28 stimulation for 72 hours in nine paired samples: CD4 P=.055 and CD8 P=.087 (rounded .08 in figure), NOT statistically significant despite stronger heading/discussion wording. Altered MDSC function remains a hypothesis. Missing samples, COVID interruptions, TMZ-related day-15 myelosuppression, small heterogeneous cohort and lack of comparator constrain interpretation.

## Language curation priorities
