## Classification and transfer boundaries

- CDKN2A: pan-cancer targeted DNA and bulk-RNA reanalysis, with an explicit NSCLC subgroup. The replicated clinical signal is urothelial, not a positive NSCLC or STK11 treatment result. Unresolved graph/prose coding discrepancies remain alerts.
- RET: cross-tumor DNA/RNA fusion-detection and IHC/FISH comparison, including lung cancers. DNA rearrangement, expressed functional fusion and protein staining are different observations. Assay-specific analysis sets must remain separate.
- Methylation: LUAD methylation plus targeted DNA profiling. Inferred immune fractions and epigenetic age come from methylation, not direct single-cell or RNA assays. Null OS tests and exact-P discrepancies constrain prognosis claims. STK11 findings are limited exploratory context.
- HER3: EGFR-mutant NSCLC preclinical ADC/pathway work with tissue context. Model, exposure schedule, receptor abundance and tumor-response endpoints must remain distinct; preclinical combination findings are not patient efficacy evidence.
- NOTCH: NSCLC biomarker associations with endpoint-specific treatment interaction and separate TCGA transcriptional context. PFS interaction does not establish OS interaction; adjusted STK11 covariates are not causal or predictive STK11 evidence. The disputed response-rate estimates are not reusable without disclosure.
- Organoids: NSCLC model qualification with genomic/transcriptomic comparison. Normal airway outgrowth, tumor identity, culture establishment and drug sensitivity are distinct; clinical predictive utility is not established.

These are article-level classifications, not a new disease-wide profile or general proteomics capability. Vocabulary/collocations retain source locations; sentence and paragraph frames are synthetic, not authorized verbatim quotations.

The [co-alteration scope notes](ccr-2026-10-07-coalteration-scope.md) distinguish descriptive sequence co-mutation, neighboring co-deletion, sequence-plus-fusion and mutation-plus-copy-number contexts. All six have bounded topic annotations; these do not turn every paper into direct STK11 evidence or establish positive gene-pair interactions.
