### Evidence, numerical checks and transfer boundaries

Retrospective multicenter LUAD cohorts plus external TCGA/SU2C/POPLAR-OAK RNA data. Do not merge all cohorts into one matched multiomic population. The discovery DFCI cohort contains 3297 patients (TTF-1 positive2801, negative496); genomic OncoPanel subset2811 (2398/413); mIF subset337 (296/41); stage-specific mIF subsets are distinct. TTF-1 is an IHC protein phenotype, not STK11 genotype or a proteome-wide assay. The paper performs no new animal perturbation, single-cell sequencing or spatial transcriptomics. Its mechanistic discussion cites other studies.

TTF-1 negativity is enriched for STK11/KEAP1/SMARCA4/NKX2-1/CDKN2A alterations; Figure1 uses multiple-testing-adjusted q values. STK11 frequencies approximately30% negative versus11% positive; no TMB difference (Figure1B P=.36). STK11/NKX2-1 mutual exclusivity is reported only in TTF-1-negative cases (supplement described in main text). This is statistical context, not demonstrated genetic antagonism. CNA and sequence mutations remain separate, including 19p losses.

Claim ceiling: TTF-1 phenotype associates with outcomes and refines molecular context in retrospective LUAD cohorts. It does not establish STK11 causality, a validated treatment-selection rule, or that bulk RNA/mIF findings apply to every clinical cohort. Local IHC practice, incomplete data, noncentral pathology, institution/treatment heterogeneity and residual confounding limit inference. The RNA signature is external-dataset analysis rather than newly generated single-cell or spatial sequencing.
