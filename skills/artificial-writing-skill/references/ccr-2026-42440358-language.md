Evidence: An ambispective pilot combines retrospective FGFR-inhibitor treatment records (36 patients, 2011–2016), prospective sampling (46 patients, 2014–2018), targeted DNA/FISH, RNA profiling, targeted multiplex mass-spectrometric protein quantification, FACT reporter assays and PDX establishment. These overlapping evidence sets have distinct denominators. Clinical benefit was operationally defined using shrinkage and disease control of at least four months; Figure 4 describes partial response and/or prolonged stabilization. Neither definition is simply RECIST ORR. FISH positivity was gene/CEN ratio ≥2.2 or copy number ≥6, whereas a proteomics Methods sentence uses >2.2; thresholds must retain their assay context. FACT measures reporter nuclear-to-cytoplasmic ratios after variant expression; ≥20% increase defines activation for selected reporters, whereas FOXO3 activation is inferred from a decrease. This is an exploratory reporter system, not proof of oncogenic addiction or clinical actionability.

Source alerts: Figure 2E displays discordant cells of eight proteomics-positive/RNA-negative and one RNA-positive/proteomics-negative, with five double-negative and two double-positive. This does not support the Results sentence describing eight RNA-high versus two protein-positive tumors; withhold exact RNA-versus-protein detection superiority. Figure 3A labels FGFR3 T689M nonactivating, whereas Figure 3B, caption and Results describe it as activating; do not use it as a resolved variant classification. FGFR2 N549D versus N550D varies between the longitudinal case descriptions; retain the reported transcript/assay context instead of silently harmonizing the residue. Figure 6D gives FOXA1 correlation 0.685, whereas Figure 6E gives 0.697; its plot and caption use different P thresholds. Avoid a single pooled exact estimate. Figure 4C percentages do not transparently reconcile with all implanted-sample counts in the caption. Data availability explicitly notes that some historic primary files cannot be retrieved; processed data availability is not complete raw-data reproducibility.

Claim ceiling: The platform produces hypotheses about context-dependent FGFR signaling, expression and resistance. Genomic, transcriptomic and protein discordance motivates integrated evaluation but does not validate expression-guided prescribing. FACT co-activation does not establish patient-level resistance causality; a FOXA1 expression correlation does not prove transcriptional regulation. The mass-spectrometric assay is targeted proteomics, not an untargeted global proteome. No primary single-cell or spatial transcriptomics dataset is reported. PDX drug-response results cited from a separate publication are not new experiments in this paper.
