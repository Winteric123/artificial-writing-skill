Study question: can camrelizumab plus low-dose apatinib show activity and tolerability in chemotherapy-pretreated, immunotherapy-naive advanced nonsquamous NSCLC, and which exploratory molecular features correlate with outcomes? There is no concurrent monotherapy or chemotherapy control. Consequently, activity within the cohort and between-biomarker-group associations do not establish the incremental contribution of apatinib, reversal of resistance, or a predictive treatment-by-biomarker effect.

The safest scientific conclusion is observed activity with substantial toxicity in a selected single-arm cohort, alongside imprecise exploratory genotype signals. Neither reversal of STK11/KEAP1-mediated ICI resistance nor selection of this regimen by genotype was demonstrated. Angiogenesis/redox explanations in Discussion remain hypotheses based on prior literature. The future phase III trial mentioned by the source must not be described as still ongoing today without current verification.
