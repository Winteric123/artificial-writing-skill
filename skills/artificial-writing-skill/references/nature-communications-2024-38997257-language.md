# PMID 38997257 — language-retention note

## Vocabulary and collocations retained

- **maintain redox homeostasis** — broad function; pair with the measured NADPH/glutathione endpoints.
- **genotype-selective dependency** — use only after direct molecular-genotype comparison.
- **p53-contingent growth suppression** — captures genetic epistasis without universalizing across TP53 variants.
- **adaptive serine utilization** — suitable for increased uptake/consumption after redox-pathway disruption.
- **alternative NADPH source** — requires isotope or enzymatic support.
- **partial dietary rescue** — prevents binary overstatement when only some endpoints normalize.
- **preserved labeling fraction** — safer than “unchanged flux” for isotope-fraction data.
- **simultaneous initiation model** — concise cue for the prevention-versus-treatment limitation.

## Sentence frames

1. “Genetic ablation of **[target]** suppressed tumorigenesis in **[genotype A]** but had little effect in **[genotype B]**, indicating a context-dependent dependency.”
2. “Loss of **[target]** reduced **[redox metric]** and increased **[damage markers]**, consistent with impaired redox homeostasis.”
3. “Deletion of **[modifier]** abrogated the growth-suppressive effect without eliminating **[stress phenotype]**, suggesting that stress alone was insufficient for tumor control.”
4. “Although **[dietary/metabolic intervention]** partially rescued **[endpoint]**, it did not normalize **[other endpoint]**, indicating an incomplete rescue.”
5. “Similar isotopic enrichment should not be interpreted as equivalent absolute flux in the absence of pool-size and rate measurements.”
6. “These data nominate **[target]** as a preclinical vulnerability; pharmacologic tractability and clinical therapeutic index remain unresolved.”

## Paragraph architecture

For a genotype-specific metabolism paragraph: establish genetic selectivity → quantify survival/burden → identify redox consequence → use genetic epistasis to define the required effector → add lipid/serine adaptations → close with initiation-model and pharmacologic limitations.

## Unsafe transfers to avoid

- Do not claim that high-dose vitamin C or serine/glycine restriction is clinically effective.
- Do not translate isotopic enrichment directly into absolute metabolic flux.
- Do not generalize Trp53 deletion results to all human TP53 alterations.
- Do not label the pooled mRNA survival analysis as treatment-predictive.

## Indexed language units

- `Genetic ablation of [target] suppressed tumorigenesis in [genotype A] but had little effect in [genotype B], indicating a context-dependent dependency.`
- `Deletion of [modifier] abrogated the growth-suppressive effect without eliminating [stress phenotype], suggesting that stress alone was insufficient for tumor control.`
- `These data nominate [target] as a preclinical vulnerability; pharmacologic tractability and clinical therapeutic index remain unresolved.`
