### Results paragraph
- `We separated culture initiation from sustained propagation and evaluated tumor purity in the available models. Selected long-term cultures retained lineage features and showed molecular concordance with matched source tissue, although concordance varied by assay and specimen pair. Drug testing identified model-specific sensitivity and a combination effect that was supported in the corresponding xenograft. These findings describe preclinical model performance, not patient response.` 结构：建立率→纯度→保真→应用→边界；pp3–10。

### Discussion paragraph
- `The models provide an experimentally accessible representation of selected tumor features, but normal-cell outgrowth and culture-associated selection constrain generalization. Molecular similarity should therefore be reported by assay and source pair rather than as complete fidelity. Matched xenograft responses strengthen the preclinical rationale, whereas clinical prediction and treatment benefit remain separate questions requiring patient-level evaluation.` 结构：用途→偏倚→准确保真表述→转化边界；pp5,8–11。
