# PMID 40749670 — language-retention note

## Section-aware vocabulary and collocations

- **harmonized joint-cohort analysis** — Methods phrase for combining old and new cohorts after batch correction.
- **modality-specific denominator** — audit phrase for assays available only in subsets.
- **genome contiguity and breakage intensity** — Results wording that distinguishes fragmentation topology from aneuploidy.
- **context-dependent role of STK11 mutation** — Results/Discussion framing for heterogeneous mutation effects.
- **proteomic-context heterogeneity** — synthesis phrase for C1/C3 versus C2 STK11-mutant tumors.
- **cluster-wide versus genotype-specific feature** — essential language for avoiding attribution of all C2 biology to STK11.
- **signature-inferred carcinogen exposure** — Methods/Limitations phrase that preserves indirect measurement.
- **late-like early-stage disease** — Results label for molecularly advanced stage-I tumors; keep quotation marks or define operationally.
- **target prioritization rather than efficacy evidence** — Discussion boundary for DepMap/PRISM integration.

## Synthetic sentence frames

1. “The expanded cohort did not reproduce a uniform **[phenotype]**; instead, the association was restricted to **[feature/subgroup]**, prompting within-genotype stratification.”
2. “The feature tracked the proteomic cluster in both mutant and wild-type tumors, indicating a cluster-wide state rather than a genotype-specific consequence.”
3. “After adjustment for **[pathway covariate]**, **[feature]** remained enriched in the mutant subgroup, supporting—but not proving—a genotype-specific association.”
4. “The exposure class was inferred from mutational signatures and self-report; it should not be interpreted as a direct quantitative exposure measurement.”
5. “Integration of tumor abundance with cell-line dependency nominated **[target]**, but neither patient-level response nor a prospective biomarker interaction was tested.”

## Paragraph architecture

For heterogeneous genotype evidence: state the prior expectation → report the expanded-cohort negative result → define the within-genotype split → distinguish cluster-wide from mutation-specific features → name covariate adjustment → close with non-causal interpretation. For a multi-cohort paper: provenance and batch harmonization → shared data layers → subset-only layers → discovery → orthogonal validation → clinical ceiling.

## Safe transfer

- Keep newly generated data, joint reanalysis, external cohort validation, and cell-line screening explicitly separated.
- Report assay denominators rather than carrying the headline n=406 into every result.
- Use “signature-inferred” for carcinogens and “deconvolution-estimated” for immune composition.

## Unsafe transfer

- Do not call C1/C3 and C2 validated clinical STK11 subtypes.
- Do not infer causality from cluster-associated NRF2, ferroptosis, immune, or gastric-differentiation programs.
- Do not interpret PRISM tanespimycin sensitivity as patient efficacy.
- Do not treat a batch-corrected, cross-sectional cohort as longitudinal disease evolution.
