## Question, design and evidence sequence

This experimental/translational study asks whether organoid cultures derived from primary NSCLC and previously established patient-derived xenografts can retain tumor identity, molecular features and selected targeted-drug responses. It is a **preclinical model-establishment and characterization study**, not a clinical treatment trial or prospective patient-response prediction study.

Between2015 and2017 the investigators processed76 tissue models:19 primary LUAD,15 primary LUSC,16 LUAD PDX and26 LUSC PDX. Eleven were excluded for insufficient starting tumor material, mouse/normal epithelial contamination or metastatic site, leaving65 eligible tissues, described in the abstract as30 primary patient tissues plus35 PDX models. These are tissue/model units, not65 independent patients. LPTO/PDXO distinguish primary-tumor-derived and PDX-derived organoids; PDO/XDO also appear in the prose, with inconsistent POD/XOD spelling in places.

The sequence of evidence is: define initiation versus short-/long-term propagation → check tumor purity and histologic lineage → compare mutation/CNV and RNA profiles with matched source tissues → test genotype-associated drug sensitivity in organoids → compare selected responses in matched PDX models. WES and bulk RNA-seq are actual molecular data layers. Flow cytometry, IHC, qPCR and selected immunoblots provide validation, not a whole-proteome dataset. Xenome separates mouse from human reads; CNVkit/GISTIC address copy number; expression correlations use selected LUAD/LUSC-discriminating genes rather than a claim of entire-transcriptome identity.

The strongest supported conclusion is feasibility of selected NSCLC organoid models for biological characterization and preclinical drug testing. Selected paired PDX experiments add in-vivo preclinical support, but do not establish clinical predictive accuracy, patient benefit or safety. No direct STK11/LKB1 experiment or explicit STK11 result appears in the supplied main article. It can support general lung-organoid/multi-omics methods writing; it is not direct STK11 evidence, and no STK11 membership/highlight is added by this read.

## Source and transfer alerts

1. **Success-rate denominator:** maintain the sequence76 processed →65 eligible →57 initiated →47 short-term plus10 long-term. Do not call88% a long-term establishment rate, do not call65 unique patients, and do not change the small purity-assessment denominator12.
2. **Model fidelity is partial:** expression correlations differ between primary tissue and PDX, and selected gene lists were used to reduce stromal effects. WES Fig3A displays substantial count differences in some matched samples; “preserved selected features” is defensible, “genomically identical/no drift” is not demonstrated.
3. **Distinct purity assays:** EpCAM positivity is not itself a universal tumor-specific measurement. Histology, lineage IHC and species-specific flow were combined. Short-term cultures lacked histologically detectable immune/stromal cells, and NOD/SCID hosts are immunodeficient; this is not a model validating ICI efficacy.
4. **Drug causality and generalization:** one KRAS-altered model and one FGFR1-amplified combination model cannot isolate the causal effect of an alteration or establish efficacy across all KRAS/FGFR1/STK11 tumors. PDX corroboration remains preclinical.
5. **Combination nuance:** Fig5D BKM120 combination is not synergistic at both plotted inhibition levels. Preserve the ED50/ED75 distinction rather than copying the blanket weak-synergy description.
6. **Source-label inconsistencies:** p11 Discussion refers to the MEK result as short-term, whereas p8 Results and Fig4 explicitly place it under long-term drug testing; use the primary experiment-specific description with disclosure. p6 prose refers to mutation burden in five models, but the surrounding genomic analysis and Fig3 caption enumerate nine model groupings; do not replace the clear figure-level denominator with five.
7. **Unusual significance symbols and extraction units:** Fig4D uses `*` for P>.05 and `**` for P<.05; do not apply conventional star meanings. Visual inspection resolves extracted `mmol/L` to **µmol/L**, `mm` tissue-section/scale-bar text to **µm**, and implant-volume `mL` to **µL**. Extraction artifacts are not source scientific dose changes.
8. **Scope:** no explicit STK11/LKB1 finding occurs in the supplied main text. Unprovided mutation tables could contain other variants; absence from this supplied main article must not be expanded to a claim about unseen supplements.
