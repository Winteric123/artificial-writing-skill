### Abstract background
- `paired pre- and post-treatment specimens` — longitudinal sampling, not necessarily the same lesion.
- `acquired resistance to immune checkpoint inhibition` — define the study-specific response/duration threshold.
### Abstract methods
- `We integrated [targeted genomic profiling] with [spatial immune phenotyping] in paired samples.` — synthesized frame; not a verbatim quotation or whole-omics claim.
### Abstract results
- `At progression, [alterations] were newly detected in [number/denominator] evaluable pairs.` — distinguish detection from de novo emergence.
### Abstract conclusion
- `The findings support heterogeneous resistance pathways and warrant functional validation.` — synthesized cautious conclusion.
### Introduction
- `personalized post-progression strategies` — rationale rather than a proven treatment recommendation.
- `the mechanisms underlying acquired resistance remain incompletely characterized` — gap frame, limit to the relevant population.
### Methods
- `tumor lineage similarity` — establish relatedness before interpreting longitudinal changes.
- `arm-level copy-number alterations` — distinct from sequence mutations.
- `paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test` — retain paired sampling and exact analysis subset.
- `image-derived nearest-neighbor distance` — spatial protein/pathology measurement, not transcriptomics.
### Results
- `newly detected biallelic alteration` — same-gene sequence plus copy-number context, not two-gene sequence co-mutation.
- `The within-group change did not reach statistical significance, although between-cohort changes differed.` — synthesized frame grounded in HLA-I results.
- `The evaluable subset differed across assays.` — report denominators separately.
- `No corresponding change was detected in the comparator cohort.` — do not infer interaction from separate significance tests.
### Discussion
- `temporal association does not establish causality` — longitudinal sequencing still needs perturbational confirmation.
- `The findings should be interpreted in the context of treatment heterogeneity and selective rebiopsy.` — synthesized limitation frame.
- `preclinical validation is necessary` — hypothesis-level therapeutic vulnerability.
### Conclusion
- `Distinct genomic and immune changes may contribute to acquired resistance.` — avoid declaring each observed alteration a causal mechanism.
### Figure legends
- `Only newly detected alterations are displayed.` — specify exclusions and denominator.
- `Lines connect paired specimens from the same patient.` — specify whether same lesion is required.
### Discussion paragraph structure
- `Define resistance and sampling → establish tumor relatedness → compare sequence/CNA changes → use treatment controls → quantify paired immune changes → distinguish association from mechanism.` — synthesized reusable structure, not copied prose.
