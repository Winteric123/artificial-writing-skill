## Experimental architecture
Human ARPE-19 cells engineered via PiggyBAC to express mouse or human IL2 were encapsulated in alginate microcapsules. Human producer-cell origin does not make AB1 mouse tumors human xenografts. AB1-Fluc syngeneic intraperitoneal tumors in immunocompetent Balb/C mice provide efficacy; Sprague Dawley rat pleural administration provides safety/PK, not pleural tumor efficacy. Tumor-size-stratified randomization is described, but measurements were unblinded. Dose is controlled by cell density and capsule count; units are micrograms/day, not the milligrams misextracted from PDF typography. Anti-PD1 200 micrograms/mouse and depletion antibodies100micrograms/mouse are not mg doses. Protocol reproduction requires reopening rendered units, including microliters and micrometers.

CyTOF uses43antibodies, palladium barcoding, bead normalization, debarcoding, manual immune phenotypes, UMAP and mean metal intensity. This is single-cell protein cytometry, not single-cell RNA-seq or untargeted proteomics. Forty-thousand downsampled cells/sample and four mice/group across four groups imply640,000cells, inconsistent with Figure3 caption's one million. Cells are not independent mouse replicates. One-way ANOVA with Holm–Sidak comparisons is stated. A public GSE117358 gene-set analysis is supplementary secondary-dataset evidence; its detailed output was not supplied.

## Main evidence, figures and negative findings
Figure1p5: cell density controls secretion without visibly reduced cell viability; dose groups have five–seven mice. Nineteen of26treated mice had greater than45% signal reduction after one week; 11/12at≥2.5micrograms/day had>75% reduction, and all at5micrograms/day had90% reduction. These dose-specific estimates should not be collapsed into a universal80% or universal eradication. The day11 delayed-treatment model described from supplements reduced burden but did not eradicate tumors. Sham and unengineered-cell capsules control surgery/material effects. Bioluminescence is a tumor-burden surrogate, not direct viable-cell census.

Figure2p6: groups seven–eight mice, combination7/7eradication versus IL2+isotype5/7, with untreated/antiPD1/controlcapsule+antiPD1 growth. Follow-up extends roughly59days from inoculation. Superior numerical clearance is not a formal pharmacologic synergy test, and IL2-only versus combination survival is labeled nonsignificant. Rechallenge is a selected subset of cured combination mice roughly60days later, with no observed subcutaneous growth; five of six naive controls grew tumors. Main prose does not clearly specify the treated rechallenge denominator; do not call it all seven. This demonstrates resistance to same-cell-line reinoculation, not clinical relapse prevention or cross-antigen protection. A P>.0001 printed at rechallenge should not silently become P<.0001. CD8-depletion dependency and CD4-depletion result are described in main text but visuals reside in unavailable FigureS2.

Figures3–4pp8–9: four mice per group. Total T-cell and macrophage proportions were not significantly changed, whereas B-cell proportions fell. M2-like CD86-negative/PDL1-negative fractions decreased; M1-like CD86-positive/PDL1-positive increase was significant for IL2 alone, while cDC increase was seen with combination. Do not claim every effect occurred in both groups. Activated and effector-memory T-cell fractions increased and naive fractions decreased. CD40/MMI, IFNg, Ki67 and other protein heatmaps describe phenotypic changes; marker-defined M1/M2 shifts do not prove macrophage lineage conversion or functional polarization mechanisms. Frequencies are conditional on parent populations and are not absolute tissue counts. T-cell activation does not negate the stated CD8-dependency experiment.

Figure5p11: humanIL2 factories administered in20/20rats,65capsules per animal at reported2micrograms/day, with five animals/timepoint assays. Pleural versus blood concentrations peak24hours, then decline with pericapsular overgrowth. Abstract/discussion say150fold local enrichment; results say≥100fold at all timepoints, but byday30 plotted local/blood values converge near baseline. Restrict high local enrichment to the active-release period rather than assert100fold after cessation. Capsule retrieval and longer mouse foreign-body histology are supplementary and not directly checked. No significant blood-count changes is not equivalence or proof of no systemic effect.

Figure6p12: rat tissue H&E and serum chemistry generally show no persistent toxicity signal. However day1triglycerides decreased (P=.0014), contradicting a blanket 'all values nonsignificant' caption. Pleural/peripheral-lung immune changes occurred and reportedly resolved byday21. Stable HDL/LDL alone cannot establish complete cardiac safety, and30-day rodent safety does not establish human safety. Porcine cadaver catheter administration is supplementary feasibility, not in-vivo safety or efficacy. The delivery platform still requires invasive access; use proposed clinical translation, not established clinical use.
