### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. TMB cutoffs and values depend on assay, specimen, panel composition, and calibration. Do not treat 13 or 16 mut/Mb as universally interchangeable.
2. FMI tissue, FMI blood, and local-tissue groups are not independent randomized groups; numerical response differences cannot be attributed solely to platform performance.
3. The trial lacks a TMB-low comparator and a non-atezolizumab control arm. It therefore supports activity in a selected population, not definitive predictive utility.
4. Lung cancers were present but not separately analyzed. Do not quote the overall basket ORR as an NSCLC or SCLC ORR.
5. TPS/CPS analyses are exploratory, small, and span tumor types with different PD-L1 conventions.
6. Microsatellite instability, co-mutations, medications, microbiome, HLA, and T-cell repertoire were not fully resolved and may confound response.
