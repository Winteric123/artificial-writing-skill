## Study and denominators
Phase-I correlative pCSI series:14 leptomeningeal-metastasis patients,9 NSCLC/3 breast/1 esophageal/1 rectal;13 post-CSF samples at1 month and1 at3 months. Eleven deaths/CNS progressions; no CNS progression within3 months. Median OS9 months, IQR5–21 (not CI). Systemic treatment held around irradiation, except specified endocrine/trastuzumab use, and resumed4 weeks after. No randomized control. Comparison cohort50 CNS-metastasis cases:30 non-pCSI CNS RT,20 no RT,21 lung/21 breast; mixed brain/LM disease, not external validation of pCSI benefit.

## Genomics and analysis
MSK-IMPACT505-gene panel, not WES. Matched germline except1 pooled normal; depth>=20,variant reads>=8,hotspot VAF>=2%/novel>=5%;14 manually retained lower-depth variants. Separate >=1-read look-back only for overlap, not principal clinical associations. FACETS two-pass segmentation; purity-unestimable samples excluded; CCF<=.80 subclonal. Reactome overlap/FDR is gene-list enrichment, not transcriptomic GSEA. Eleven CCF-evaluable cases (5 high/6 low), median cut .61. No multiplicity correction for exploratory clinical tests.
