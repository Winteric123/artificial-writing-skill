## Synthetic sentence frames

- Results：`The adverse association of [gene] was concentrated in the [molecular-context] stratum and was not apparent in the comparator stratum.`
- Results：`Although the double-altered group had the poorest outcomes, the gene-by-gene interaction was not supported, favoring an additive descriptive pattern.`
- Discussion：`A similar adverse association under platinum chemotherapy argues against an exclusively checkpoint-inhibitor-specific explanation.`
- Mechanism：`Bulk RNA-seq deconvolution inferred lower abundance of [cell populations], a finding that requires orthogonal cellular or spatial validation.`
- Claim ceiling：`Stratum-specific significance should not be taken as proof of interaction unless the cross-stratum contrast is tested directly.`

## Paragraph logic

临床结果段可按：合并队列变异频率 → 分 KRAS 层的 ORR/PFS/OS → 多变量调整 → 两个独立队列方向一致性 → 四分类拆解 STK11 与 KEAP1 → 正式 interaction/多重性限制。该结构能防止把并集或双变异组结果错误归因于任一单基因。

机制讨论段可按：临床关联 → TCGA 差异通路 → 去卷积推断 → 与临床表型的一致性 → 因果和跨队列限制。不要从 bulk transcriptomics 直接写成细胞层面的确定机制。

## Unsafe transfer

- 不要将标题中的 `diminished efficacy` 不加限定地改写为已证实的 ICI 特异性预测耐药。
- 不要把 `STK11`、`KEAP1` 或其并集互换；两者有分别的效应量和机制信号。
- 不要以 `KRAS` 两层内 p 值一显著一不显著作为 interaction 的证明。
- 不要把 xCell 结果称为实际 TIL 计数或因果免疫重塑。
- 不要把双变异组最差写成 `synergy`；文中 `STK11×KEAP1` 交互未获支持。
