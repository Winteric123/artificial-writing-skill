# Non-IMRaD and auxiliary genres

Do not force these genres into original-research section logic.

## Commentary or editorial

Use:

`focal study and key result -> field context -> evidence appraisal -> unresolved question -> specific research or trial-design implication`

- Attribute the focal study's data to its authors; do not write `we found` unless the user is an author of the primary study and asks for that voice.
- Distinguish study facts from the commentator's interpretation.
- Compare alternatives by evidence type, feasibility, and uncertainty rather than by decorative contrast.
- End with a concrete research, validation, or trial-design path.

The corpus rules for this genre are qualitative: eight commentary articles without standard section headings had source-text coverage processed through page-level fallback but are absent from standardized section statistics and the phrase-example bank. This processing history does not establish language-focused deep reading. Every article has a complete bibliographic title. Do not attach quantitative frequency claims to those eight papers.

## Narrative review

Use:

`scope/taxonomy -> biological mechanism -> clinical evidence -> resistance, safety, or implementation gaps -> development roadmap`

Organize by a defensible conceptual axis rather than summarizing citations one by one. Separate established evidence, emerging observations, and the authors' synthesis. Do not present a review inference as primary data.

## Regulatory or approval summary

Use:

`approval and indication -> pivotal evidence -> efficacy -> safety -> benefit-risk reasoning -> residual uncertainty or postmarketing requirement`

Preserve the exact indication, population, line of therapy, approval pathway, dose, and regulatory status. Do not generalize an approval beyond its stated label or turn accelerated approval evidence into mature confirmatory evidence.

## Response to reviewers

Use:

`acknowledge -> answer directly -> describe exact revision or justify no change -> point to location`

Remain professional without conceding a claim the data cannot support. Quote or paraphrase the revised manuscript text accurately. Never claim an analysis, experiment, or citation was added unless it was.

## Figure legends and table notes

- Make the unit independently interpretable.
- Define panels, cohorts, symbols, abbreviations, statistical tests, summary measures, error bars, and significance markers.
- Distinguish biological replicates from technical replicates and state N or n consistently.
- Describe what is displayed; reserve interpretation for the main text unless the journal requires otherwise.
- Do not infer missing methods or sample sizes from the visual alone.

## Cover letters and highlights

State the problem, contribution, fit, and evidence boundary concisely. Avoid unverifiable priority claims, guaranteed impact, or claims of clinical practice change not supported by the study.
