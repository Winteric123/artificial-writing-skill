Limits: Assumption-bound correction, unobserved true clinical effect, cause-of-death accuracy, PH and CI-method sensitivity, limited simulated settings and untested dependent competing events. Not recommended by authors when treatment effects on competing events cannot be bounded, e.g. certain two-systemic-therapy comparisons. Closeness to trials with different populations/comparators is contextual agreement, not empirical proof of unbiasedness. No omics, wet-lab or immunotherapy-specific evidence. STK11 transfer is statistical reporting and confounding limitations only; no auto-highlight.
