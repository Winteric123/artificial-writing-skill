### Evidence reconstruction and numerical checks

| Evidence unit | Source-grounded reconstruction | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Data integration | Public scRNA-seq data from melanoma, HNSCC, and lung cancer were integrated; bulk TCGA and immunotherapy cohorts were used for downstream associations. | Datasets differ in platform, sampling, tumor type, and treatment context. |
| Clustering | Seven initial fibroblast clusters were generated; one low-quality cluster was excluded, leaving six named pan-CAF states: myCAF, dCAF, iCAF, iCAF2, nCAF, and pCAF. | Cluster labels are analysis-derived states, not universally fixed cell types. |
| Signature transfer | CIBERSORTx and related signature methods estimated CAF-state abundance in bulk cohorts. | Deconvolution is inferred abundance, not direct cell counting. |
| Regulatory analysis | SCENIC implicated MEF2C, TWIST1, NR1H3, RELB, and FOXM1 as state-associated transcriptional regulators. | Regulon activity is computationally inferred, not perturbationally validated. |
| Outcome associations | CAF signatures were related to TCGA survival and to checkpoint-blockade outcomes in bladder, melanoma, and kidney cohorts. | Cross-cohort association does not establish CAF-mediated resistance. |
| Spatial claim | Multiregion bulk data were used to examine regional variation. | This is not spatial transcriptomics and lacks single-cell spatial localization. |

### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. Reported cluster counts/percentages in the main text contain an internal arithmetic inconsistency: some displayed percentages cannot sum to a coherent total. Do not reproduce a total cell denominator without resolving it from source data.
2. The study is computational and lacks direct experimental perturbation of the proposed CAF regulators.
3. CIBERSORTx estimates are not measured cell fractions, and bulk signatures can reflect correlated non-CAF programs.
4. Multiregion bulk sampling is not spatial transcriptomics.
5. Figure 6 labeling of anti–PD-1 versus anti–PD-L1 contexts is not fully aligned with the Methods description for all cohorts; identify the source cohort rather than generalizing one checkpoint label.
6. Immunotherapy associations occur in non-lung cohorts and should not be reported as NSCLC efficacy evidence.
