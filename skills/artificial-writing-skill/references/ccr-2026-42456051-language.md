- Final analysis297 H&E whole-slide images:109 patients with one slide and94 with two; patient-level aggregation follows slide-level features. An earlier Methods description of roughly five sampled slides concerns initial selection, not the final297 analytical images.
- AI segments cancer/stroma and locates lymphocytes at cellular resolution. This is computational histopathology, not single-cell RNA sequencing, spatial transcriptomics or proteomics. GATA6 IHC is a surrogate, not a transcriptome assay.
