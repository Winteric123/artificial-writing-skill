# 2026-10-19 CCR/JTO supplement: co-mutation and co-alteration scope

## Scope

This topic recheck covers the nine PMID-indexed original articles added from the supplied 2026-10-19 batch. It uses the completed main-text and main-visual reading records; separate supplements were not supplied. The labels below are article-level retrieval annotations, not exhaustive variant inventories, new acceptance decisions, or proof that an unlisted pair is absent.

`co_mutation_status=analyzed` requires an actually analyzed sequence-mutation combination or compound mutation. `co_alteration_status=analyzed` is broader and may include fusions, copy-number changes or engineered genotype combinations. `context_only` keeps a measured or modeled genotype context searchable without treating it as a natural patient co-mutation analysis.

## PMID 31548343

- PDF: SHA-256 `77cbc42e11cdc50ed1a050355ecc138830cd163894605c6e08381018ec6c3559`; 23 physical pages.
- Context pages checked: 4–6 and 16–23.
- Decision: sequence co-mutation and broader co-alteration were analyzed.
- Evidence: the study described the METex14 co-alteration landscape, including TP53, EGFR and RAS–MAPK alterations; assessed MET copy-number gain and secondary MET mutations; and tested KRAS overexpression or NF1 downregulation in engineered Ba/F3 models.
- Retrieval contexts: `MET+TP53`, `MET+EGFR`, `MET+NF1`, `MET+BRAF`, `MET+KRAS`, and single-gene `MET` for same-gene secondary/CN events.
- Boundary: the treatment-naive RAS–MAPK comparison was nominally significant but did not survive multiplicity correction (`P=0.0297`, `q=0.297`). cfDNA cannot determine tissue or clonal origin. Single-gene `MET` does not imply that every second-site mutation and amplification was in cis.

## PMID 38578683

- PDF: SHA-256 `7a339160ef52117411e95dee515c25a4ba49dda32c2cd77de033490e7d78a40e`; 11 physical pages.
- Context pages checked: 1 and 4–9.
- Decision: sequence co-mutation and broader co-alteration were analyzed.
- Evidence: EGFR G719X/E709X was recurrently observed in cis, compared with G719X alone in treatment cohorts, and reconstructed in functional models.
- Retrieval context: single-gene `EGFR`, tagged as a same-gene compound mutation and engineered combination.
- Boundary: same-gene compound mutation is not a cross-gene co-mutation. The nonsignificant afatinib comparisons do not establish equivalence, and regimen cohorts were not randomized head to head.

## PMID 42848316

- PDF: SHA-256 `9f16d5b59b7d8e1c4a194ffc8136dba37e41f7caa47e04ec69bb6be072b80359`; 30 physical pages.
- Context pages checked: 10, 11, 15, 25, 26, 29 and 30.
- Decision: sequence co-mutation and broader co-alteration were analyzed.
- Evidence: RET-fusion models were combined with secondary RET substitutions; in the small clinical series, one patient's cfDNA simultaneously detected RET G810N and G810C after selpercatinib.
- Retrieval context: single-gene `RET`, with sequence mutation, fusion/rearrangement, same-gene compound and engineered-combination tags.
- Boundary: a RET fusion plus one secondary substitution is a broad co-alteration, not by itself two sequence mutations. The sequence co-mutation label is supported only by the one G810N/G810C cfDNA observation; phase and same-clone origin were not established. The single case cannot establish comparative efficacy or a sequencing standard.

## PMID 31634668

- PDF: SHA-256 `cf7f06640270fee531fe9d7695f2d267abe4e9db678f00b276b148ec688a83ea`; 11 physical pages.
- Context pages checked: 3–8.
- Decision: sequence co-mutation is context-only; broader engineered co-alteration was analyzed.
- Evidence: KRAS/LKB1 genotype backgrounds, CRISPR/knockout models and mouse systems were used to test ERK-inhibitor vulnerability and PI3K/AKT bypass.
- Retrieval context: `KRAS+STK11`, tagged as an engineered combination.
- Boundary: the work is wholly preclinical. LKB1 deficiency includes engineered loss or functional deficiency and must not be rewritten as a measured patient STK11 sequence co-mutation distribution.

## PMID 33887464

- PDF: SHA-256 `734cd8cb99c086c49e57d08d63e972b7f2554e42576a259ba3d16fb3ef313935`; 14 physical pages.
- Context pages checked: 3–11.
- Decision: sequence co-mutation and broader co-alteration were analyzed.
- Evidence: the small INT cohort was KRAS-mutant and included LKB1-mutant cases, while four KRAS/LKB1 isogenic backgrounds were experimentally compared.
- Retrieval context: `KRAS+STK11`, with sequence-mutation and engineered-combination tags.
- Boundary: the descriptive KRAS/LKB1 background does not establish a KRAS-by-STK11 prognostic interaction. The principal clinical claim is miR-17/LKB1-stratified prognosis, and the PDX tumor-volume endpoint was negative.

## PMID 33992811

- PDF: SHA-256 `de5459e0834771f84367cd59d7c1ad5320bd67cb1026ca5ff783a685f27b85fc`; 17 physical pages.
- Context pages checked: 3, 5 and 11–17.
- Decision: sequence co-mutation and broader co-alteration were analyzed.
- Evidence: the study tested a `KEAP1/STK11/PBRM1/SMARCA4 CoMt` composite variable in the local-regional control analysis.
- Retrieval context: `KEAP1+STK11+PBRM1+SMARCA4`.
- Boundary: this is a union/composite covariate, not proof that all four genes were altered in the same tumor. It cannot support an STK11-specific conclusion, and no treatment-by-genotype interaction was reported.

## PMID 34601169

- PDF: SHA-256 `e5e5b5a387029acb7e612304fcb6ff0439e04fd6eeed643e023ab3eeb05af446`; 13 physical pages.
- Context pages checked: 4–11.
- Decision: sequence co-mutation and broader co-alteration were analyzed.
- Evidence: TP53 mutation classes were examined within KEAP1-mutant disease, including stage-specific survival associations.
- Retrieval context: `KEAP1+TP53`.
- Boundary: the associations are prognostic and exploratory, not treatment-predictive. Gene-testing denominators differ and must remain explicit; the main-text numeric conflict warning remains in force.

## PMID 35490853

- PDF: SHA-256 `a09377d054ce1d0474c9ea72ebcf7aea5c4474f5d5ae4a7f0051ffff268b1533`; 17 physical pages.
- Context pages checked: 9 and 12.
- Decision: sequence co-mutation and broader co-alteration are context-only.
- Evidence: KRAS/STK11 and KRAS/KEAP1 co-mutation groups appear as background/limitations; the study's measured evidence is compartment-specific protein profiling rather than a genomic co-mutation analysis.
- Retrieval contexts: `KRAS+STK11` and `KRAS+KEAP1`, restricted to background or eligibility use.
- Boundary: do not convert cited or limitation-level co-mutation context into an analyzed result. CD66b is treatment-associated/indicative in this design, not a predictive biomarker established by interaction testing.

## PMID 37473958

- PDF: SHA-256 `99cc48491f0be389d3dcf44432bc0d17625f3573d07c95dbb49e50cbc873732e`; 18 physical pages.
- Context pages checked: 3–10 and 12.
- Decision: sequence co-mutation and broader co-alteration were analyzed.
- Evidence: TP53 and KRAS distributions were compared in KEAP1- and NFE2L2-altered groups; SMARCA4, CUL3 and LRP1 were assessed as alternative mutations in score-positive canonical-wild-type tumors.
- Retrieval contexts: `KEAP1+TP53`, `KEAP1+KRAS`, `NFE2L2+TP53`, `NFE2L2+KRAS`, plus single-gene `SMARCA4`, `CUL3` and `LRP1` alternative-activation candidates.
- Boundary: contexts include group comparisons and candidate alternatives, not uniformly positive co-occurrence. LRP1 did not survive adjustment; immune values are bulk-expression inferences; the held-out set was within-center rather than external validation.
