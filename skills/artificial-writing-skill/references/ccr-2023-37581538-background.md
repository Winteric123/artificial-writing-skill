These mechanisms come from cited studies, not a new dataset generated by this review.

STK11/LKB1 discussion p3: neutrophil-rich immune exclusion, IL6, STING/type-I-IFN and antigen-presentation defects. KEAP1/EMSY is a different co-alteration pathway; do not collapse the genes into one mechanism. KRAS-inhibitor immune remodeling/TLS and ICI synergy vary with model immunogenicity and orthotopic versus subcutaneous implantation; cold tumors may not become responsive.

Early KRYSTAL-7 ORR49% is cited secondary evidence, not a head-to-head efficacy estimate or current standard.
