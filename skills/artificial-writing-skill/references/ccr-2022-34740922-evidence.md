## Design, treatment and endpoints
Single-institution open-label 3+3 dose-escalation study, n=15;12 stageIII and3 oligometastatic stageIVA. Not exclusively stageIII despite shortened abstract/title framing. Patients all adenocarcinoma,12female/3male. Three stageIVA cases had two solitary brain metastases and one nonregional node; local metastatic treatment is an additional clinical context.
TRC102 flat doses50/100/150/200mg with pemetrexed day1 in two cycles; cisplatin and radiation startday3. Thoracic60Gy/30fractions, then two additional cisplatin/pemetrexed cycles. 200mg dose cap was prespecified from prior experience. No durvalumab administered in this study: adding immunotherapy is proposed future research, not an evaluated regimen.
Primary: safety/DLT/dose selection; secondary response and6-monthPFS. RECIST1.1; Wilson responseCI; KM survival. LRRFS/DRFS use recurrence-specific definitions, not the composite progression/death definition of PFS.

## Results and numerical boundaries
All15 evaluable:3CR+12PR, ORR100% (reported95%CI0.8–1). Single-arm uncontrolled phaseI activity cannot establish improvement attributable toTRC102. Median follow-up25.7months.6-monthPFS80%,2-yearOS83%; censored KM estimates, not integer fractions of15. Figures2–4 include wide95%bands and no detailed risk tables; survival tails have limited support.
No DLT at tested levels and no grade5toxicity or clinical radiation pneumonitis. This does not prove absence of pneumonitis risk. Two grade3anemia requiring transfusion, one grade4neutropenia delaying cycle3; Table2 also grade3lymphopenia7,grade4lymphopenia3,grade3WBCdecrease7. Seven grade2esophagitis. Do not translate “well tolerated” into no severe toxicity.
Authors call200mg theMTD; because escalation stopped at a preselected cap withoutDLT, safer reusable wording is highest tested dose/recommended phaseII dose, not an empirically reached toxicity boundary.
Five distant and three locoregional relapses cannot be added as eight unique patients without an overlap map. Two deaths, seven without relapse at lastfollow-up. Existing metastatic disease/heterogeneous stage may bias OS interpretation.

## Transfer and caveats

No tumor omics, pharmacodynamic target-engagement data or STK11 subgroup.
