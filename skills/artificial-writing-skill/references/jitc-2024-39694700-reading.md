## Scientific question and study logic

The study asks whether LKB1 regulates PD-L1 through protein turnover and whether this can explain an immune-response phenotype. The mechanistic sequence is LKB1/AMPK manipulation, interaction discovery by immunoprecipitation-mass spectrometry, Skp2 gain/loss, ubiquitin-linkage and PD-L1-site mutants, then mouse anti-PD-L1 treatment with Skp2 inhibition. The proposed model is Skp2-dependent K63-linked ubiquitination at PD-L1 K136/K280, opposing beta-TrCP-associated degradation and supporting PD-L1 stability. Loss of LKB1/Skp2 lowers PD-L1 and is associated with an immune-cold phenotype.

This is direct functional STK11/LKB1 evidence with important clinical-expression, genotype and treatment distinctions. The 193 STK11-mutant sequencing cohort, the 40 surgical expression/genotype specimens, and the 40 checkpoint-treated patients are not interchangeable populations. Notably, all 40 checkpoint-treated patients were STK11 wild type; that analysis evaluates LKB1/Skp2 protein expression, not STK11-mutation-specific immunotherapy prediction. Mouse Skp2 inhibition attenuated anti-PD-L1 benefit: this article does not support a Skp2 inhibitor/checkpoint combination as an effective treatment strategy.

## Methods and classification

Treatment scope: clinical anti-PD-1/PD-L1 exposure with a heterogeneous small observational cohort; preclinical anti-PD-L1 antibody, Skp2 inhibitor compound25, AMPK-related metformin/compound C. Explicitly separate response association from efficacy prediction. NGS mutation/TMB analysis is a clinical DNA-assay layer; the main text does not justify relabeling it whole-exome sequencing. Mechanistic assays include CRISPR/shRNA/overexpression, qPCR, co-IP, CHX chase, MG132, ubiquitin linkage mutants and site-directed PD-L1 lysine mutants. IP-MS is a selected interaction-discovery experiment, not unbiased tissue proteomics. IHC/multiplex IF are targeted tissue protein imaging, not spatial transcriptomics. No original scRNA-seq is reported.

STK11 relevance: direct mutation-context and LKB1-function study; modules are protein stability, PD-L1 biology, immune infiltration and checkpoint response. The clinical expression finding cannot be merged with a mutation-based resistance label. Low expression, mutation, kinase loss and experimental knockdown must remain separate.

5. The clinical DCB abbreviation denotes CR/PR, not a conventional duration-based durable-benefit endpoint. Always carry its source definition.
6. Clinical treatment cohort all STK11-WT: no mutation-based predictive biomarker claim or direct mutant-versus-WT clinical benefit inference.
7. IP-MS discovery counts alternate between peptides and proteins (Fig.5 caption p.10 versus Results pp.7-8). Preserve that uncertainty; no proteome-scale quantitative inference.
8. Human immune-marker prose p.5 labels CD56 macrophages although methods/panels use CD68. Use CD68 for the verified human assay; do not store CD56 as a macrophage-specific marker.
9. Cross-trial survival comparisons between distinct disease/therapy settings in Discussion cannot establish that Skp2 explains the difference. Ethics wording also differs between mouse approval in Methods and end-section not-applicable wording; no blanket ethics assertion should be synthesized from that line alone.

## Limitations and Chinese-safe synthesis

Clinical n=40, observational response/expression association, all-STK11-WT selection, unclear endpoint labeling, stage-table inconsistencies, model-specific pharmacology and limited animal group sizes constrain the claim ceiling. PD-L1 abundance is not a sufficient stand-alone measure of an immunosuppressive or responsive microenvironment. A combination can reduce PD-L1 yet weaken antibody benefit; direction of a molecular marker and direction of treatment efficacy are not interchangeable.

可写：在所研究的细胞和小鼠模型中，LKB1/AMPK-Skp2轴参与PD-L1蛋白稳定性调控；Skp2抑制降低PD-L1，并在该LLC模型中削弱抗PD-L1治疗效果。40例STK11野生型患者中的LKB1/Skp2表达与疗效相关，尚不足以证明STK11突变的预测价值。不可写：抑制Skp2可以逆转STK11突变患者免疫耐药，或本研究已建立STK11突变伴低TMB的规律。
