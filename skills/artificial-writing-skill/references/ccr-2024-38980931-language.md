### Evidence reconstruction and numerical checks

Study question: can an expression phenotype recover KEAP1-like biology and outcome associations missed by mutation-only grouping? This is a secondary biomarker analysis, not prospective clinical validation of treatment selection.

### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. Figure4 risk sets sum to 314 (155 free +56 mutant +103 KEAPness), whereas Methods matched SU2C61 plus atezolizumab251 sum to 312. This denominator discrepancy remains unresolved; do not repair it or describe one pooled n as unambiguously confirmed.
2. Figure3 response bars label numbers using chi-square notation, without clearly distinguishing the statistic from a P value. Do not transplant .03788/.03491 as verified P values or estimate unprinted response percentages from bar heights.
3. Adverse outcome associations also occur in the docetaxel group (p7; supplement referenced). The paper therefore does not establish an immunotherapy-specific predictive effect. Do not write that the signature selects patients who should receive or avoid ICI.
4. The broad claim of outperforming mutation-only classification does not by itself establish incremental clinical prediction, discrimination or treatment utility. Outcome-derived threshold optimization and retrospective testing require prospective validation.
5. The Methods clustering argument combines Euclidean-distance and Ward-linkage terminology in a way that should not be copied as executable R code (p3). Reconstruct distance and linkage from the actual code before reproducing the analysis.
6. Supplementary figures S1–S23/tables are referenced, not supplied. Missing exact clinical cutoff and covariate detail remain missing. No single-cell, spatial-transcriptomic, proteomic or new perturbation experiment is present in this main article. Multiregion WES/RNA and T-cell inference do not change those modality boundaries.
7. CTLA4 combinations and NRF2-directed interventions are interpretation/background, not treatments tested experimentally here. KEAPness is neither an STK11 mutation classifier nor direct evidence of STK11-driven resistance.
