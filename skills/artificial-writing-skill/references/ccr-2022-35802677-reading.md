## Research question and evidence chain

The study asks whether methylation patterns expose clinically relevant heterogeneity in resected lung adenocarcinoma beyond driver mutations. It joins independent targeted DNA sequencing with genome-wide methylation arrays, then derives immune composition and epigenetic age from the methylation layer. The evidence sequence is clinical cohort → methylation-derived immune estimates → mutation/age associations → unsupervised subgroups → outcome comparison → candidate pathway interpretation. It is not an intervention trial, not an immunotherapy-response cohort and not a direct mechanistic demonstration that DNA methylation controls recurrence.

The 88 tumors comprise 27 newly profiled EPIC/850K cases and 61 previously profiled 450K cases. Their DNA assays also differ: the new cohort used a 580-gene exonic panel; the older cohort a 57-gene LUAD panel. Six common genes (KRAS, EGFR, TP53, STK11, KEAP1, ATM) support the reported genotype comparisons. Calling all 88 samples uniform whole-exome sequencing would be inaccurate. Methylation plus targeted genomics meets molecular multiomics; MethylCIBERSORT does not establish an RNA-seq assay, and CD8/CD20 IHC does not constitute proteomics.

## Population, analysis sets and methods

- Table 1 (p3): 88 patients; stage I/II/III/IV = 69/13/4/2. The cohort is predominantly, not exclusively, early-stage. Smoking history yes/no/unknown = 72/14/2; recorded ethnicity is uneven (73 Caucasian, 4 African American, 7 Asian, 2 other, 2 unknown). These small categories do not support biological essentialism or broad ethnic generalization.
- OS information is available for 85 patients: 66 alive, 19 dead, 3 unavailable. All 27 EPIC patients are recorded alive at the reported follow-up. The Results give average follow-up 50 months (p4). OS runs from resection to death or last follow-up (Figure 5 caption).
- Recurrence information is present for 56 patients in Table 1 (21 yes, 35 no), with 32 unavailable; all 27 EPIC cases lack recurrence information. RFS additionally uses a minimum contact time of 36 months (pp9–10), but per-group Kaplan–Meier analysis numbers are not stated in the main text. Do not report an 88-person fully observed recurrence cohort or assume every nonmissing table entry appears in each survival comparison.
- EPIC/450K analyses combine overlapping probes, background correction and quantile normalization; sex-chromosome and SNP-associated probes are removed for clustering. The 10,000 most variable probes feed Euclidean/ward.D2 hierarchical clustering; K-means is described as identifying groups for visualization (p2). Figure 3 instead calls it unsupervised K-mean clustering; retain this wording difference rather than invent a single fully specified reproducible clustering pipeline.
- MethylCIBERSORT estimates stromal/immune fractions; PAM classifies hot/cold tumors. Estimates describe composition, not directly enumerated functional immune cells. Representative subgroup 1–4 specimens underwent CD8 and CD20 IHC, with 10 ROIs scored per subgroup (pp3,8); ROI observations are not ten independent patients. Main text does not state the complete independent patient count for this IHC validation.
- Horvath clock uses 353 CpGs. Unlike clustering, its input retains sex/SNP probes (p3). DNAm age is an algorithmic estimate, not chronological age or direct cellular senescence measurement.
- Mann–Whitney for two groups, Kruskal–Wallis for multiple groups, log-rank for survival and linear regression/95% CIs for continuous relationships. The paper's phrase “nonparametric t” should be normalized to Mann–Whitney in new writing. No multivariable prognostic model, independent validation, treatment interaction or comprehensive multiple-comparison correction is established for the numerous subgroup/immune tests; Figure 5F separately displays adjusted enrichment P values.

## Source alerts and limitations

1. Abstract/Discussion and Figure 2/5 headings overstate survival correlation relative to nonsignificant log-rank tests. Use actual results above. The printed caption phrase “No statistical significance ... (P < 0.05)” is awkward shorthand/wording, not evidence that those tests were significant.
2. Figure 5A .0037 versus Results .0007 is unresolved. The general across-group DNAm difference can be described with this discrepancy disclosed; the exact P must not be silently chosen.
3. Table 1 prints recurrence “No” 35 (38.8%) among 88. Count and printed percentage do not agree: a reader arithmetic check gives 35/88≈39.8%, not 38.8%. Preserve count 35 and flag the source percentage; do not silently rewrite the original table. The 850K cohort has no recurrence data.
4. Smoking/CD4 text prints “P=0183”; Figure 1D clearly prints .0183. Use the figure value if this minor comparison is needed and mention the typography correction. Figure 5 caption also mistakenly says group 1 versus groups “1–5”; the enumerated comparisons are 2–5.
5. Retrospective, limited cohort; mixed assay panels/arrays; potential cohort/follow-up confounding; no independent subgroup validation; small subgroup 6 and ethnic cells; multiple exploratory tests; no stated adjusted survival model. The same methylation data generate subtype, immune estimate and age, so cross-feature relationships are not independent assays.
6. Main text mentions S1–S10 and Data S1–S4, but these files were not supplied. References to their reported results do not certify the plots/data read. Exact subgroup mutation denominators and detailed recurrence eligibility remain bounded by the main article.

## STK11 relationship and safe use

Direct STK11 evidence exists, but it is limited: p4 reports no detected immune-composition difference by mutation status; Figure 2D shows lower DNAm age (unadjusted P=.0476); p7 reports no enrichment across methylation subgroups. This supports article-level STK11 epigenetic-context discussion, not a core-highlight designation, causal STK11-mediated immune exclusion or immunotherapy resistance. Genotype status comes from DNA panels; no STK11 functional perturbation is performed.

Strongest defensible claim: in a small, predominantly early-stage resected LUAD cohort, methylation profiling identified exploratory subgroups with differing inferred immune composition and epigenetic age, with selected recurrence comparisons but no statistically established OS separation. Potential treatment selection remains hypothetical.
