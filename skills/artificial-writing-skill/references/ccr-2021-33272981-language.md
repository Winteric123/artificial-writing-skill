### Discussion paragraph

- `The comparison indicates that diagnostic performance depends on both the molecular event and the method used to define positivity. A high aggregate estimate can conceal a partner-specific blind spot, while protein expression may not track the genomic event closely enough to serve as a universal surrogate. Selective co-testing and incomplete reference confirmation limit generalization. The findings therefore motivate a complementary testing strategy without establishing that the proposed sequence is superior in every setting.` 结构：方法依赖→亚型/替代标志物限制→设计限制→克制结论；pp9-11，synthetic。
