### Evidence and transfer boundaries

- Six cell lines were tested. NFE2L2-E79K LK-2 IC50 68nM; KRAS/KEAP1 A549 approximately1µM; other tested lines >1µM for TAK-228 in Figure1. Wild-type and mutant lines differ in histology/genetic background: not an isogenic causal contrast.
- TAK-228 inhibited AKT-S473/4E-BP1 phosphorylation, while everolimus showed feedback AKT activation; equal protein loading and GAPDH controls described. LK-2 xenografts N=5/group, drug from day6, SD error bars, transient regression about55% followed by sustained growth inhibition. Decreased late Ki-67 AND cleaved caspase3 described in supplement-based analyses: do not rewrite as increased apoptosis.
- Single-center open-label phase2, NCT02417701. Simon two-stage design for each cohort: null ORR5%, target40%, stage1five with >=1response to expand to total10, >=2responses to warrant further study; power90%, typeIerror10%. Actual treated/assessable counts differ from planned counts; don't substitute plan for accrual.
- Treated/safety N=34: NFE2L2-LUSC18, KEAP1-LUSC10, KRAS/co-mutant6. Response-assessable N=24:12/6/6. ORR3/12=25%,1/6=17%,0/6=0%; no randomized efficacy comparison or proven genotype-by-treatment interaction.
- Assessable PFS8.9(95%CI5.6–NR),3.7(1.8–NR),2.1(1.6–NR)months; all-treated PFS7.9(4.4–NR),1.8(.9–NR),2.1(1.6–NR). Response durations5.4/8.9/11.4months for three NFE2L2 responders. All-treated OS14.0/18.5/17.9months, imprecise and not comparative evidence.
- Grade3 hyperglycemia11/34=32%; dose reduction24%, hold44%, no AE-driven discontinuation. Table2 permits the same person at multiple grades, so grade-specific counts may exceed N when summed; cannot derive unique all-grade incidence by addition.
- Five end-of-study paired biopsies, no recurrent secondary mutation reported; data not displayed. TAK-228+CB-839 activity in xenografts described with reduced TAK dose .5 versus3mg/kg monotherapy; no clinical combination efficacy data here. Single-patient fluoroglutamine PET uptake declined20%; metabolic-flux interpretation is indirect, not definitive proof of acquired glutamine dependence.
- Figure3 draws five KRAS patients despite six response-evaluable in text; do not infer all individual trajectories are displayed. Waterfall marks new lesion in a case, so target-lesion shrinkage alone does not determine RECIST response. No formal covariate testing given small cohorts; null qualitative association is not absence of effect.
- Source rhetoric about first successful targeting and prediction is stronger than single-arm evidence warrants. Introduction's phrase describing prior efficacy as OS rates below10% is ambiguous; do not reuse it as a validated endpoint statement. Future-trial status is historical at publication, not verified current status. Mutation != pathway activation in every KEAP1 case; mechanism requires functional assessment.
