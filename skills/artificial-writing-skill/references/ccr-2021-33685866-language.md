### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. This is a biopsy-selected retrospective cohort and likely enriches patients able to undergo repeat profiling.
2. Lorlatinib selection bias is possible because patients with known ROS1 mutations may have been preferentially treated with it.
3. Not all sequencing platforms covered L2086; the observed frequency may be underestimated.
4. Plasma/tissue discordance may reflect heterogeneity, shedding, timing, or assay coverage; it is not automatically a false result.
5. About 40% of lorlatinib-resistant cases remained unexplained by gene sequencing. DNA-only testing cannot exclude RNA, epigenetic, protein, or phosphoprotein mechanisms.
6. Cabozantinib activity in one patient and Ba/F3 cells is hypothesis generating; toxicity and multikinase effects limit direct treatment recommendations.
7. The paper's 2021 treatment-sequencing discussion must not be presented as current clinical guidance without a current source check.
