# PMID 36150391 — language-retention note

## Section-aware vocabulary and collocations

- **prime tumor immunogenicity** — title/Discussion phrase for preparing a tumor for immune recognition; not synonymous with clinical sensitization.
- **pulsed MPS1 inhibition** — Methods/Results wording; schedule is mechanistically relevant.
- **chromosome-missegregation-induced micronuclei** — Results wording linking mitosis to cGAS substrate formation.
- **residual versus epigenetically restored STING signaling** — Results distinction between STING-low and STING-absent models.
- **state-matched epigenetic priming** — synthetic Discussion phrase for choosing EZH2/DNMT intervention by baseline STING state.
- **tumor-cell-intrinsic innate sensing** — mechanistic phrase that separates tumor cells from host innate cells.
- **immune-dependent antitumor activity** — justified only with depletion, knockout, or immune-deficient controls.
- **reductionist immune co-culture** — Limitations wording for engineered 3D systems.

## Synthetic sentence frames

1. “A brief pulse of **[mitotic perturbation]** increased **[micronuclei/cGAMP]** and activated **[pathway]**, whereas continuous exposure produced a weaker immunogenic response.”
2. “Orthogonal inhibitors and genetic depletion converged on the same phenotype, supporting an on-target mechanism.”
3. “The priming requirement differed by baseline pathway state: **[agent A]** restored signaling in **[state 1]**, whereas **[agent B/combination]** was required in **[state 2]**.”
4. “Antitumor activity was attenuated by **[immune-cell depletion]**, lost after **[tumor-gene knockout]**, and weaker in immunodeficient hosts, supporting an immune-mediated component.”
5. “Durable responses in small mouse cohorts justify translational testing but do not establish clinical efficacy or tolerability.”

## Paragraph logic

For a mechanistic Results paragraph: perturbation and schedule → chromosome/micronucleus phenotype → second messenger → pathway activation → genetic epistasis → functional immune output. For therapy synthesis: baseline state → priming regimen → immune recruitment → in vivo dependency controls → checkpoint combination → clinical claim ceiling.

## Safe transfer

- Use “required” only where knockout/depletion removes the effect; use “associated with” for state comparisons.
- Preserve species and model at every step: human cell/PDX-derived culture, engineered co-culture, syngeneic mouse, or NSG control.
- When reporting durable-response counts, retain the small denominators and regimen sequence.

## Unsafe transfer

- Do not call the combination clinically effective, safe, or ready for biomarker-guided use.
- Do not treat STING-low and STING-absent tumors as interchangeable.
- Do not imply MPS1 inhibition is a direct STING agonist; the proposed route is chromosome missegregation → micronuclei → cGAS/cGAMP.
- Do not infer natural human immune infiltration from engineered Jurkat/NK/PBMC co-cultures.
