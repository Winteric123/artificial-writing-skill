## Main figures and inference ceiling

Figures1–2: treatment-stratified survival/response, PD-L1 relation, DE/enrichment/deconvolution. Figure3: tumor IDO1 protein, survival, spatial CD8 relationship. Figure4: TLS-localized FDC IDO1, tonsil scRNA, nonsignificant survival, metabolite/Tfh/B assay. Figure5: GeoMx AOIs, proliferating Tregs and small clinical subset. These support an inflamed-context marker and a feedback hypothesis; they do not demonstrate clinical IDO1-inhibitor benefit, combination synergy, or a validated predictive biomarker.

## Language and safe transfer

Use region-of-interest segmentation, compartment-specific expression, deconvolution-estimated abundance, nearest-neighbor distance, maximally selected rank statistic, and hypothesis-generating therapeutic rationale.
