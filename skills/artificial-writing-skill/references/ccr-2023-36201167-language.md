### Source alerts and transfer limits

1. Tissue testing is the comparator but not a flawless gold standard; describe concordance rather than diagnostic accuracy unless the reference standard is explicitly defined.
2. The author discussion uses language resembling “not inferior” for plasma-identified treatment outcomes, but no randomized noninferiority design or margin was used. Do not transfer that wording as a formal conclusion.
3. Fusion PPA was substantially lower than DNA-driver PPA. A positive plasma result may support treatment selection when tissue is unavailable, but a negative plasma result cannot replace tissue evaluation.
4. The plasma-only treatment subgroup comprised 13 patients. Its 85% ORR is descriptive and highly imprecise.
5. Turnaround time, assay success, driver detection, and matched-treatment outcomes are separate endpoints and must retain their own denominators.
