Evidence: Retrospective model development from Japanese C-CAT paired tumor–normal sequencing, translated into tumor-only prediction. Methods discovery7048 and validation2926 patients; variant-bearing subset3372; complete-case model2297 patients/3317 variants (2981 somatic,336 germline). Matched-normal sequencing supplies labels; pathologist estimates tumor content. Normal depth≥10, alternate≥5,VAF≥0.25 defines germline; source labels germline variants with tumorVAF<0.05 as CH, an operational rule not universal biological proof. Pathogenicity uses ClinVar/OncoKB; population MAF≥0.001 filtering; discovery panel124 genes, validation platform different. Patient-level folds prevent between-fold sharing of variants from the same person. Cluster-robust covariance accounts for repeated variants, backward-selected logistic nomogram, 500-bootstrap internal validation, LightGBM/random forest tenfold patient-grouped CV, beta-calibration, DeLong AUC comparisons, Youden threshold. Do not describe tuning on the first training fold as nested CV.

Figures/formula: rVAF=VAF/tumor content; TAR=(VAF−0.5+0.5×tumor content)/tumor content. TAR can be negative and is a model-derived indicator, not a directly measured mutated-allele proportion. Fig3 illustrates copy-number/LOH assumptions; avoid its literal 'proportional' claim because the somatic curve contains 1/T. Fig1 separates GPV detection prevalence from germline conversion rate among pathogenic tumor variants; lung GCR2.8% is not germline prevalence among all lung patients. STK11 has no observed germline event in this series; this does not exclude hereditary STK11 disease. Fig2 normal VAF narrowly peaks around0.5 but tumor VAF overlaps somatic/germline; Fig4 distributions support discrimination, not perfect separation. Fig5 shows nomogram, discoveryROC, DCA and validationROC.

Central findings: Source reports488 patients with GPV,6.9% of7048. Model Brier0.03, R2=0.723,c-index0.961. Discovery AUCs nomogram0.96,LightGBM0.97,RF0.96 versus ESMO0.85/Kosugi0.88 (Results/Fig5). Validation2089 variants=2018 somatic+71 germline; AUC0.97 for each model versus ESMO0.90. Missing validation germline tumor VAF initially fixed at0.5, then100 multiple imputations from discovery empirical distribution pooled with Rubin rules; neither equals directly observed validation VAF. Apparent external validation is a separate Japanese cohort/platform, not ancestry-external validation. DCA is model-estimated net benefit, not observed clinical outcome improvement or proof of safe tumor-only hereditary diagnosis.

Source alerts: Abstract7078 versus Methods/Results7048 discovery patients. Reported4905 total pathogenic variants do not equal4410 somatic+491 germline+8 CH=4909. Methods' stated32 genes conflicts with its36-name list; Fig1 caption32 and visible gene bars must not be combined into an assumed identical gene universe. Validation description '1223 variants' immediately accompanied by581 male/642 female patients is unit-ambiguous; next analysis uses2089 variants. Abstract ESMO AUC0.88 conflicts with Results/Fig5 ESMO0.85 (Kosugi0.88). Abstract/TR/Discussion net benefit1.2% differs from Results1.6–1.8% at5–10% thresholds; exact clinical-utility magnitude held pending clarification. Fig4 'Fisher' label does not identify an exact test on continuous values; do not invent its implementation. Reported sensitivity/specificity intervals require positive-class/denominator recheck before numerical reuse. Keep abstract mean age61 separate from analysis median63, not a contradiction by itself.

Limitations: Retrospective selection, missing VAF and imputation, small germline-positive validation set, ancestry and assay coverage, germline CNVs/large indels not comprehensively captured, uncommon genotypes, imperfect calibration and residual tumor-purity measurement error. Germline origin still requires confirmatory normal testing. No immunotherapy efficacy, spatial or single-cell experiment was conducted.
