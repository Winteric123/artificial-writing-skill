## Abstract
- Regulatory evidence requirements differed between molecularly selected and broader patient populations.
- The review separates initial indication approvals from subsequent label extensions.
## Introduction
- Biomarker-defined subgroups create distinct opportunities and constraints for clinical development.
- Approval patterns provide a historical view of how evidence requirements evolve with therapeutic context.
## Discussion
- Response magnitude and durability must be interpreted alongside study design and uncertainty.
- An expedited regulatory pathway does not eliminate the requirement to confirm clinical benefit.
- Experimental-arm sample sizes should not be confused with total randomized enrollment.
- Changes in treatment setting can alter the feasibility and interpretation of confirmatory trials.
- Regulatory conclusions are specific to the jurisdiction, indication and date of assessment.
## Conclusion
- Timely confirmatory evidence remains essential when early access is based on limited datasets.
