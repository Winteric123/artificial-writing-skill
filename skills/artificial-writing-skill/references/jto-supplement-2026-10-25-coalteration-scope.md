# Co-alteration scope for the 2026-10-25 JTO supplement

## PMID 32068166

The article stratifies STING-pathway expression by `STK11`, `TP53` and other genotype contexts. It compares mutation distributions and notes that a subset of `STK11`-mutant, STING-high tumors is enriched for `TP53`, but it is not a systematic pairwise co-mutation study. Register gene context and immune association only; do not infer a general `STK11/TP53` co-occurrence rate or treatment prediction. Main-PDF denominator conflicts remain active.

## PMID 33096269

No co-mutation or broader genomic co-alteration question is a principal analyzed result. WES supplies TMB and genomic context to an immune-remodeling comparison. Register both co-mutation and co-alteration as `not_identified`; do not convert assay availability into a co-alteration analysis.

## PMID 37247843

Chromosome-arm alterations and global aneuploidy are directly analyzed. The principal arm-level event is 9p loss, with 1q gain retained as an adjusted-negative result. This is a copy-number/co-alteration analysis, not a pairwise sequence co-mutation analysis. ICI outcome associations remain treatment-context candidates without a treatment interaction; mIF and TCGA evidence use independent cohorts.

## PMID 40056978

The article is an immune-mechanism study centered on CCR8-positive Treg cells, CCL5-positive dendritic cells and PD1 blockade. It does not analyze tumor genomic co-mutation or co-alteration patterns. Register both statuses as `not_identified`; scRNA/scTCR, targeted protein panels and cell-cell interaction analysis are not genomic co-alteration evidence.

## PMID 40850615

The article integrates CEACAM7 RNA expression and DNA methylation with survival. Correlation between two molecular layers is not a genomic co-alteration analysis, and no pairwise mutation question is reported. Register both co-mutation and co-alteration as `not_identified`; retain the expression-methylation work under omics and prognostic topics.
